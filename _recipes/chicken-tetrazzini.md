> I first had this dish at my sister's house a few months ago and it was fabulous. Since then, I've made it a few times, though I've lightened it up a little (in place of the half & half cream, I use evaporated skimmed milk). I like to add broccoli florets to the dish also.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups cooked chicken
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- 1 cup fresh sliced mushrooms
- 1 cup evaporated skimmed milk
- 1 tsp. salt
- ⅛ tsp. pepper
- ¼ cup flour
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 (8 oz.) pkg. spaghetti
- 1 cup sharp shredded cheddar cheese

## Steps

1. Melt butter (2 Tbsp.); add mushrooms (1 cup) and cook for 2 minutes.
2. Add flour (¼ cup), broth (2 cups), milk, salt (1 tsp.), and pepper (⅛ tsp.); stir until thickened.
3. In 2 quart casserole, put 1 layer of cooked spaghetti (1 pkg., 8 oz.), layer of chicken (3 cups), then sauce; sprinkle cheese (1 cup) and repeat.
4. Bake at 400° until bubbly hot, about 20 minutes.
