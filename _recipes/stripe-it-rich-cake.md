## Ingredients

- 1 pkg. cake mix
- 2 pkg. (4 serving size) instant pudding
- 4 cups cold milk
- 1 cup confectioner sugar

## Steps

1. Prepare and bake cake mix (1 pkg.) as directed on package. Remove from oven.
2. Poke holes at once down through the cake to the pan with the round handle of a wooden spoon. Holes should be 1 inch apart.
3. Only after poking holes, combine pudding (2 pkg., 4 serving size) mix with sugar in large bowl. Gradually stir in milk (4 cups). Beat at low speed not more than 1 minute. Do not overbeat.
4. Quickly, before pudding thickens, pour one half of the thin pudding evenly over warm cake and into the holes. Spoon remaining pudding over top of cake.
5. Chill at least 1 hour.
