## Ingredients

- ¼ cup cornstarch
- 2 cups cold water
- Food coloring

## Steps

1. Mix cornstarch (¼ cup) with cold water (2 cups) in a saucepan. Boil the mixture until it thickens.
2. Pour into several individual containers and add several drops of food coloring.
