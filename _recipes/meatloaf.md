## Ingredients

- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1 lb. ground turkey (drained of its water if there's a lot)
- 2 eggs
- 1½ cups bread crumbs
- ¾ cup ketchup
- ½ cup hot water and 1 pkg. onion broth, or 1 Tbsp. onion powder
- Carrots (optional)

## Steps

1. Mix all the above together; shape into a loaf.
2. Cover and bake for 1½ hours at 350°. I like to put carrots around the loaf before baking.

**Variation:** After mixing the meatloaf up, pat out onto a wax paper lined cookie sheet. Cook 1 frozen package chopped spinach and drain. Shred some mozzarella cheese. Spread the spinach on top of the meatloaf and then the mozzarella. Roll up the meatloaf tightly, pulling away the waxed paper as you get it rolled. Bake the same as above. This is very good. Try your own stuffing in the middle to surprise the family.
