> This recipe is from a member of my church. She used to bring it occasionally to church gatherings. It's a very tasty sandwich.

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 2 pkg. dry yeast
- 2 cups warm water
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 Tbsp. salt
- ¼ cup oil
- 6–6½ cups flour

### To brush on top

- 1 unbeaten egg white
- 2 Tbsp. water
- 1 Tbsp. oregano
- ½ tsp. salt
- ⅛ tsp. pepper

### Filling

- Chipped Virginia baked ham
- Slices of cheese (Provolone is good)

## Steps

1. Dissolve yeast (2 pkg.) in warm water. Add remaining dough ingredients. Mix well and knead until smooth and elastic.
2. Let rise until double (45 minutes). Punch down and divide in half. Pat into 2 round pizza pans.
3. Brush tops with mixture of egg white (1), water, oregano (1 Tbsp.), salt and pepper (⅛ tsp.). Cover with plastic wrap. Let rise 40 minutes.
4. Bake at 350° for 30 minutes. Cool.
5. Slice through center and fill with chipped Virginia baked ham and slices of cheese.
6. Wrap in foil and heat for 25 to 30 minutes in 400° oven.

Either filled or unfilled, this bread can be frozen to use later.
