## Ingredients

- Eggplant, as many as you need (look for thin eggplants — they have less seeds)
- Salt
- Egg, slightly beaten, with a little milk
- 1 cup grated parmesan
- 1 cup Italian bread crumbs
- Cooking spray
- Mozzarella cheese, sliced
- Spaghetti sauce (hopefully homemade)

## Steps

1. Peel eggplant and cut into ¼ to ⅓ inch slices.
2. Put salt on each side of slice and place them all in a colander with weight on it to remove some of the moisture for a couple hours.
3. Blot moisture off each slice with paper towels and dip in slightly beaten egg and a little milk, and then coat in the parmesan (1 cup) and Italian bread crumbs (1 cup) mixed together.
4. Dip into egg mixture again and coat a second coat.
5. Place on cooking spray coated cookie sheet and bake in 375° oven for about 30 minutes, turning once. Bake until eggplant is tender.
6. Slice mozzarella cheese and place on top of slices of eggplant. Place back in oven to melt.
7. Serve with hopefully homemade spaghetti sauce.

This is very good!
