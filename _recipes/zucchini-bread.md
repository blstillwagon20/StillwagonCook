## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 cup oil
- 2½ cups shredded zucchini
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 3 cups flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. baking powder
- 3 tsp. cinnamon

## Steps

1. Beat together sugar (2 cups), eggs (3), oil (1 cup), zucchini (2½ cups), and vanilla (2 tsp.).
2. Add remaining ingredients.
3. Bake for 1 hour at 325° in two greased loaf pans.

For extra flavor, add nuts, chocolate chips, or raisins.
