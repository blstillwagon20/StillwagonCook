## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter or margarine
- 1¼ tsp. seasoned salt
- 4½ tsp. Worcestershire sauce
- 2⅔ cups corn Chex cereal*
- 2⅔ cups rice Chex cereal
- 2⅔ cups wheat Chex cereal
- 1 cup salted mixed nuts

*Measure cereal in glass measuring cup.

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 250°. Melt butter (½ cup) in large shallow roasting pan (about 15" x 10" x 2") in oven. Remove.
2. Stir in seasoned salt (1¼ tsp.) and Worcestershire sauce (4½ tsp.). Gradually add cereal and nuts (1 cup), mixing until all pieces are coated.
3. Bake 1 hour. Stir every 15 minutes. Spread on absorbent paper to cool.
