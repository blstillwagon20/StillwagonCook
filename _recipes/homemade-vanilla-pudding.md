## Ingredients

- 3 cups milk
- ⅔ cup sugar
- 2 heaping Tbsp. cornstarch
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp. vanilla extract

## Steps

1. Whisk together milk (3 cups), sugar (⅔ cup), and cornstarch (2 heaping Tbsp.) and heat in microwave to almost boiling, checking and stirring every 2 to 3 minutes.
2. Separate 2 eggs and beat the yolks. Add ¼ cup of hot milk mixture to egg yolks; whisk together well.
3. Then add egg mixture to the rest of the hot milk mixture and continue cooking on high at one minute intervals, stirring after each, until nice and thick.
4. Stir in vanilla extract (2 tsp.).
