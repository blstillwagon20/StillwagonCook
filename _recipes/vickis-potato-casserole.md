> This recipe is another recipe from my sister, Vicki. It's a very good potato casserole she likes to make for picnics and everyone just loves it including myself. It's also very easy to make.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb. package frozen hash browns
- 1 can (10¾ oz.) cream of chicken soup
- 16 oz. sour cream (fat-free works as well)
- 1 medium onion, diced very small and sautéed in oil until very soft
- 8 oz. shredded cheddar cheese
- ½ cup melted butter
- Salt and pepper
- Cracker crumbs for the top (I like Waverly butter crackers)

## Steps

1. Mix all ingredients above together except the cracker crumbs in a big bowl.
2. Place in a greased 9" x 13" pan. Place cracker crumbs over the top and cover with foil.
3. Bake in 375° oven for at least 1 hour.

Do not mix this one up ahead of time. The hash browns should be right out of the freezer.
