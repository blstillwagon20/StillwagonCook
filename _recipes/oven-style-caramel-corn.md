## Ingredients

- 2 cups brown sugar
- 2 sticks butter
- ½ cup lt. Karo corn syrup
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 tsp. salt
- 8 qt. of popcorn

## Steps

1. Boil brown sugar (2 cups), butter (2 sticks) and corn syrup (½ cup) 4 minutes, stirring constantly.
2. Add vanilla (1 tsp.) and salt (1 tsp.). Pour over popcorn (8 qt.). Place in pans.
3. Bake at 250° for 1 hour. Stir occasionally.
