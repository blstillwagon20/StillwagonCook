> I've been using this recipe for years now and it is the best I've every tasted and always turns out great. Since it's not cooked, it stays very soft and light.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups butter or margarine, softened
- 5 to 6 cups sifted confectioners' sugar
- 2 cups creamy peanut butter
- 1¾ cups marshmallow creme
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 1¾ pounds (approximately) any chocolate-flavored compound coating, melted, or real chocolate, tempered

## Steps

1. Cream butter (1½ cups) and 2 cups confectioners' sugar in large bowl until light and fluffy. Add peanut butter (2 cups), marshmallow creme (1¾ cups) and vanilla (2 tsp.); blend well. Gradually add remaining confectioners' sugar; mix to consistency that can be easily handled.
2. Form into balls. Dip balls in melted chocolate and place on cookie sheets covered with waxed paper; let stand until firm or for speedier results, put in refrigerator for a few minutes. Make sure when you're taking them off the waxed paper to store them that the bottom is completely sealed. If not, place a little more chocolate on the bottom and let harden.

This recipe can be used to make large Easter eggs for gifts also. Just shape and spoon chocolate over the egg.

I find that melting chocolate compound coating is the easiest if done in the microwave. Start the microwave 1 minute first; stir (chocolate will appear to not have melted at all until it is stirred!). Then place in microwave on high for 30 second increments, stirring after each until it is completely melted. DO NOT OVERHEAT!!!
