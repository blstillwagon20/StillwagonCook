> I got this recipe from my mother who got it from her mother and so on. It's German in origin and very good. My mother usually makes it after she's had a big ham dinner and has lots of leftover ham and broth. You can also just boil a chicken or ham hocks or shanks with some onions, carrots, celery, and a bay leaf to get a nice chicken or ham stock for this. This is a wonderfully hearty soup that I hope you try.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 Tbsp. butter or margarine
- 2 eggs, beaten
- ¼ cup water
- 1½ gal. ham or chicken broth
- Ham or chicken pieces
- Grated carrot and parsley (optional)

## Steps

1. Sift dry ingredients; cut in butter (1 Tbsp.) or margarine.
2. Add eggs (2) and water (¼ cup).
3. Roll out very thin in flour (2 cups); let stand for 15 minutes, then cut in 1" squares.
4. Drop in boiling ham (1½ gal.) or chicken broth. Cook until tender.
5. Add in either ham or chicken pieces. You can also add some grated carrot and parsley for color.
