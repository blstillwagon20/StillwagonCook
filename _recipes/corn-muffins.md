> I got this recipe from Home Economics when I was in 7th grade and have used it since then. It's very good.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup flour
- ¼ cup corn meal
- 3 Tbsp. sugar
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 1 egg
- ⅓ cup milk
- 2½ Tbsp. oil

## Steps

1. Grease 6 muffin tins.
2. Combine flour (¾ cup), cornmeal (¼ cup), sugar (3 Tbsp.), baking powder (2 tsp.), and salt (¼ tsp.). Stir well.
3. Combine egg (1), milk (⅓ cup) and oil (2½ Tbsp.). Add to flour mixture. Stir only to blend.
4. Bake for 15 minutes at 400°.

**For a special treat:** Try spooning corn mixture half way up in the muffin tin, then put a little jelly in the middle, then fill the tin the rest of the way. Bake the same as above. Everyone loves this.
