## Ingredients

### Dough

- 4 cups flour
- Pinch salt
- 1 lb. butter
- ½ pt. sour cream
- 1 cup buttermilk

### Filling

- 12 Tbsp. flour
- 2 cups milk
- 2 cups shortening
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- Dash of salt

## Steps

1. **Dough:** Mix as you would pie dough. Divide into 4 parts.
2. Take 4 sheets waxed paper 12 inches long. Spread parts on paper like spreading butter, then remove paper. Fold each sheet bottom up ⅓ way, then down. Refrigerate overnight.
3. Cut in half and roll on floured board to about 8 inches in size — not too thin. Roll in rectangle and cut 1 inch strips. Twist on stick, starting on top and overlap ¼ inch. Go around 3 times. Leave end lay.
4. Place in ungreased baking sheet. Bake 375° for 25 minutes. Slide off on brown paper bag. Let cool for a second.
5. **Filling:** Cook flour (12 Tbsp.) and milk (2 cups) until thick. Cool. Cream shortening (2 cups). Beat until creamy. Add vanilla (2 tsp.) and dash of salt. Beat in remaining ingredients and cream (½ pt.) together.
6. Fill; sprinkle powdered sugar (1 cup) on top.
