> This is one of my favorite cake-like cookies to make and have been making them for years. Hope you try them.

## Ingredients

### Mix together thoroughly

- ½ cup soft shortening
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg

### Stir in

- ¾ cup buttermilk or sour milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla

### Sift together and stir in

- 2 cups flour
- ½ tsp. soda
- ½ tsp. salt

### Topping

- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 tsp. cinnamon

## Steps

1. Mix shortening (½ cup), sugar and egg (1) thoroughly. Stir in buttermilk (¾ cup) and vanilla (1 tsp.). Sift together flour (2 cups), soda (½ tsp.) and salt (½ tsp.) and stir in.
2. Chill dough. Drop by rounded teaspoonful onto lightly greased baking sheet. Sprinkle with mixture of sugar and cinnamon (1 tsp.). (I also sprinkle the cinnamon sugar on again after the cookies are in the oven for a couple minutes and have had a chance to flatten out.)
3. Bake until set but not brown in a 400° oven approximately 8 to 10 minutes.
