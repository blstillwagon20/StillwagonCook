## Ingredients

- 12 medium potatoes, peeled
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¾ cup oleo
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 cup milk
- 2 eggs, beaten

## Steps

1. Boil potatoes (12 medium) until tender. Drain and mash potatoes until smooth.
2. Cook and stir butter, cheese (2 cups), milk (1 cup), and a dash of salt (1 tsp.) until smooth.
3. Stir in potatoes; fold in eggs (2).
4. Pour into greased 3 qt. baking dish. Bake uncovered at 350° for 40 minutes or until puffy and golden brown.
