> These cookies stay soft because of the coconut, but it's not enough to change the true chocolate chip cookie taste. Your kids will love these along with all of us bigger kids.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup butter flavor Crisco
- 1¼ cup packed brown sugar
- 3 Tbsp. milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 egg
- 1¾ cup flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¾ tsp. baking soda
- 1 cup semi-sweet bits
- ½ cup coconut

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 375°. Cream Crisco (¾ cup), sugar, milk (3 Tbsp.), and vanilla (1 tsp.). Add egg (1).
2. Combine flour (1¾ cup), salt (1 tsp.), and soda (¾ tsp.). Add to creamed mixture. Stir in bits (1 cup) and coconut (½ cup).
3. Drop on ungreased baking sheet. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes.
