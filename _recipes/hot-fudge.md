> This is Graham Kerr's Basic Hot Fudge Sauce and it's guilt free and delicious. There might be an ingredient you don't have in your spice rack. It's the arrowroot. You can find it in the bottled McCormick spices. It's a white powder and a thickening agent that doesn't cloud everything up. It works at a lower heat than corn starch or flour and doesn't taste starchy. Try this recipe wherever you like hot fudge. It's good as a fondue or on ice cream.

## Ingredients

- 5 Tbsp. warm water
- 3 Tbsp. unsweetened cocoa powder (I prefer the European cocoa powder — Hershey's makes it so it's readily available. It's darker in color, not as bitter, and mixes better with liquid.)
- 4 Tbsp. brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp. arrowroot, mixed with 2 Tbsp. water

## Steps

1. Pour the water (5 Tbsp.), cocoa powder and sugar into a small saucepan and stir over low heat until dissolved.
2. Remove from the heat, stir in the arrowroot slurry, return to the heat and whisk until thickened.
3. Remove from the heat and whisk until smooth.

If this isn't sweet enough for your taste, add a couple packs of Equal or Nutrasweet to it after it has cooked.
