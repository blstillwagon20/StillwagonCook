## Ingredients

- 1 cup graham cracker crumbs
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- ¼ cup butter or margarine, melted

### Filling

- 4 pkgs. (8 oz. each) cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 egg
- 1 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1½ tsp. vanilla extract
- 1 Tbsp. lemon juice
- 1 cup (8 oz.) sour cream or like
- 2 tsp. grated lemon peel (or juice)

## Steps

1. Combine first 3 ingredients, press into 9 inch pan. Bake 325° for 6 minutes. Cool.
2. Beat cream cheese (4 pkgs., 8 oz. each) until smooth. Gradually add sugar; mix well, add eggs (2), one at a time. Beat well after each addition.
3. In small bowl mix cornstarch (1 Tbsp.), vanilla, and lemon juice (1 Tbsp.) until smooth. Add to cream cheese mixture. Fold in sour cream (1 cup, 8 oz.).
4. Bake at 325° for 45 to 50 minutes.
