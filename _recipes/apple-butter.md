## Ingredients

- 6 lb. apples
- 4 cups apple cider or juice
- 3 cups sugar
- 1½ tsp. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. cloves

## Steps

1. Cut apples (6 lb.) into quarters; do not peel. Heat apples and cider (4 cups) to boiling. Reduce heat to medium and cook 15 to 20 minutes or until tender.
2. Press through sieve. Return to saucepan, add sugar (3 cups) (for diabetic cooking, omit sugar and add 1 pkg. of Equal to jar when ready to use), cinnamon (1½ tsp.), and cloves (1 tsp.).
3. Boil about 1 hour and 15 minutes or until mixture is thickened, dark and mounds slightly when dropped from a spoon. 1 cup crushed pineapple may be use.
4. Ladle into jar. Process 10 minutes.
