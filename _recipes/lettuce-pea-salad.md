## Ingredients

- Lettuce
- Green pepper
- Celery
- Onion
- Frozen peas
- Mayonnaise (Hellmann's)
- Sugar
- Shredded mild cheddar cheese
- Real bacon bits

## Steps

1. Break up and layer lettuce in bottom of a flat dish. (I usually use a 9" x 13" pan, depending upon the crowd I'm serving.)
2. Cut into dices the green pepper, celery, and onion. Sprinkle on top of lettuce.
3. Add your frozen peas on top of that. Spread mayo (however much you like) on top of that. Sprinkle with sugar.
4. Layer the shredded cheese, and add bacon bits to top.
5. The recipe calls for 15 hours of refrigeration. Be sure to tightly wrap your dish with aluminum foil.

That's it... enjoy! Your salad will be a hit.
