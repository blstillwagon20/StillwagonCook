## Ingredients

- 1 can cream of mushroom soup, divided
- 1 lb. ground beef
- ½ cup dry bread crumbs
- 1 egg, beaten
- ¼ cup finely chopped onion
- 1½ cups sliced mushrooms

## Steps

1. Mix ¼ cup soup, beef (1 lb.), crumbs (½ cup), egg (1) and onion (¼ cup). Shape into 6 patties.
2. Brown on both sides. Remove fat.
3. Stir in remaining soup and mushrooms (1½ cups). Simmer 20 minutes, turning patties occasionally.
