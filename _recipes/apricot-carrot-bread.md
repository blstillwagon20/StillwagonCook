> I got this recipe out of a Mott's applesauce cookbook that helps lessen the fat and cholesterol in recipes and found it to be very good. It's a sweet bread and is great with tea and coffee for brunch or an after dinner snack.

## Ingredients

- 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- ¼ tsp. baking soda
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- ½ cup finely shredded carrots
- ½ cup Mott's Natural Apple Sauce
- 1 egg, beaten lightly
- 2 Tbsp. vegetable oil
- ⅓ cup dried apricots, snipped into small bits
- ½ cup powdered sugar
- 2 tsp. apple juice

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray 8 x 4-inch pan with nonstick cooking spray.
2. In large bowl, combine flour (1¾ cups), baking powder (1 tsp.), baking soda (¼ tsp.) and salt (¼ tsp.).
3. In small bowl, combine granulated sugar (½ cup), carrots (½ cup), apple sauce (½ cup), egg (1) and oil (2 Tbsp.).
4. Stir apple sauce mixture into flour mixture just until moistened. (Batter will be thick.) Fold in apricots (⅓ cup). Spread batter into prepared pan.
5. Bake 45 to 50 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool in pan 10 minutes. Invert onto wire rack; turn right side up. Cool completely. For best flavor, wrap loaf in plastic wrap or foil; store at room temperature overnight.
6. Just before serving, in small bowl, combine powdered sugar (½ cup) and apple juice (2 tsp.) until smooth. Drizzle over top of loaf. Cut into 12 slices.
