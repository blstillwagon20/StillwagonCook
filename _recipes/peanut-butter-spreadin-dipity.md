> I got this recipe from Graham Kerr's cook book and we love it. It's my favorite way to prepare chicken breasts on the grill. There's three ways to use this recipe. You'll see what I mean below.

## Spread

- ½ cup strained yogurt (put nonfat plain yogurt into either a coffee filter or paper towel, then into a strainer; strain for at least 5 hours and you'll end up with a yogurt cheese)
- ½ cup creamy peanut butter
- 1 banana, peeled and mashed

**Tip:** You can also use nonfat cottage cheese. Just blend it up until smooth in a food processor or blender and use ½ cup in place of the strained yogurt.

In a small bowl, combine the strained yogurt, peanut butter and mashed banana. Enjoy 1 Tbsp. of this spread on a slice of whole wheat bread, a muffin or even a waffle. Continue adding to these ingredients for the Dip and Sauce (which is for the chicken).

## Dip

- 2 Tbsp. fresh chopped cilantro (I usually use dried coriander seed I grind with my pepper grinder, since I very rarely have the cilantro. Use about ½ to 1 tsp.)
- ¼ tsp. cayenne pepper, or more if you like it hot!

Now let's take that spread and move it up to the dipping level. Add the cilantro and cayenne and let sit for 5 minutes. Allow 7 ounces of fresh vegetables for each of your guests to dip. There is enough dip to serve 10 at a party. Continue for chicken.

## Sauce

- 2 Tbsp. lemon juice
- 4 tsp. soy sauce

The final variation is the creation of the sauce. Add the lemon juice and soy sauce for the Skewered Chicken (below).

## Skewered Chicken

- 4 cloves garlic, peeled and smashed
- 1 tsp. oil (sesame oil if you have it)
- 4 tsp. soy sauce
- Four 3½ ounce boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into ½ inch cubes

1. In a small bowl, mix the garlic (4 cloves), oil (1 tsp.) and soy sauce. Add chicken pieces and stir until well coated and let stand for 10 minutes.
2. Preheat broiler, or I like to do these on the grill.
3. Divide the chicken pieces equally among 4 skewers, brush chicken with the sauce and place on a rack in a roaster pan and broil for 5 minutes, or put on the grill.
4. Remove from the broiler or grill, turn the skewers, brush the chicken with the sauce again and put back under the broiler for 2 minutes.
5. Serve with the rest of the sauce on the side, for dipping.
