## Ingredients

- ½ cup shortening
- ½ cup butter or margarine, softened
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- ½ cup brown sugar (packed)
- 1 to 2 tsp. grated orange peel
- 1 Tbsp. orange juice
- 1 egg
- 2½ cups flour
- ¼ tsp. soda
- ¼ tsp. salt

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 375°. Cream shortening (½ cup), butter (½ cup), and sugar (½ cup). Blend in remaining ingredients.
2. Fill cookie press with ¼ of dough at a time; form desired shapes on ungreased baking sheet.
3. Bake 10 to 12 minutes or until light brown on edges. Makes 6 dozen 2 inch cookies.
