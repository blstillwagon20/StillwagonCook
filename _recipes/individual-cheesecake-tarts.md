## Ingredients

- Vanilla wafers
- 2 (8 oz.) cream cheese, soft
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 can cherry pie filling

## Steps

1. Place one vanilla (1 tsp.) wafer into each lined (with cupcake papers) muffin tin.
2. Whip together all ingredients, except cherries. Fill cups ⅓ from top. Dab with cherry pie filling (1 can).
3. Bake at 350° until set and golden brown.
