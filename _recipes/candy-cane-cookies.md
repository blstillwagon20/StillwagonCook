## Ingredients

- 1 cup soft shortening
- 1 cup sifted confectioners sugar
- 1 egg
- ½ tsp. almond flavoring
- 1½ tsp. vanilla
- 2½ cups flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. red food coloring

## Steps

1. Mix shortening (1 cup), sugar, egg (1) and flavorings (½ tsp.). Mix in dry ingredients.
2. Divide dough in half. Blend food coloring (½ tsp.) into one half.
3. Roll 1 tsp. each color into a strip about 4 inches long. Place strips side by side. Press lightly together and twist like a rope.
4. Put on ungreased baking sheet. Curve top for handle. Bake 9 minutes at 375°.
