> These cookies are delicious and very pretty.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup vegetable oil
- 4 sq. (4 oz.) unsweetened chocolate, melted
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 4 eggs
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 cup confectioners sugar

## Steps

1. Mix oil (½ cup), chocolate and granulated sugar (2 cups). Blend in 1 egg at a time until well mixed. Add vanilla (2 tsp.).
2. Stir flour (2 cups), baking powder (2 tsp.) and salt (½ tsp.) into oil mixture. Chill several hours or overnight.
3. Heat oven to 350°. Drop teaspoonfuls of dough into confectioners sugar (1 cup). Roll in sugar; shape into balls.
4. Place 2 inches apart on greased baking sheet. Bake 10 to 12 minutes or until almost no imprint remains when touched lightly in center. DO NOT OVERBAKE! Makes 6 dozen cookies.
