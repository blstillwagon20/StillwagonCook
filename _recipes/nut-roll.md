## Ingredients

### Dough

- 2 pkg. dry yeast
- 1 cup warm water
- 1 cup warm mashed potatoes
- 2 sticks butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 cup warm canned milk
- 2 eggs
- 7 cups flour
- 2 or 3 Tbsp. sour cream

### Nut mixture

- 5 lbs. walnuts, ground
- 3 cups sugar
- Vanilla
- A little canned milk to moisten

### Topping

- Egg white, for brushing

## Steps

1. Beat eggs (2), butter (2 sticks), sugar, salt (1 tsp.) and potatoes (1 cup). Mix in canned milk (1 cup).
2. Dissolve yeast (2 pkg.) in 1 cup warm water. Add into the first mixture, then add flour (7 cups). Hand press until soft.
3. Mix together walnuts (5 lbs.), sugar, vanilla, and a little canned milk to moisten.
4. Divide dough into 7 equal amounts. Roll out (not too thin or it will break).
5. Spread nut mixture and roll. Brush with egg white.
6. Bake at 350° for 30 minutes, 3 rolls in a pan.
