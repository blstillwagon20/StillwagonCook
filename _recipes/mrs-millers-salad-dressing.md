## Ingredients

- ½ cup oil
- 1 cup sugar
- ⅓ cup wine vinegar
- 2 tsp. dry mustard
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 tsp. celery seed
- 2 tsp. horseradish
- ½ of onion, grated

## Steps

1. Mix the above ingredients in blender.
2. Store in refrigerator.
