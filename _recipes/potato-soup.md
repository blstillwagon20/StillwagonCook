## Ingredients

- 1 small onion, diced
- Potatoes, cubed (enough to fill a large saucepan)
- 6 Tbsp. butter or margarine
- 4 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 2 tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. pepper
- 5 cups milk
- About 5 Tbsp. Velveeta cheese, or 4 slices American cheese

## Steps

1. Dice one small onion (1 small), and cube enough potatoes to fill a large saucepan. Cover with water and boil until tender. Drain.
2. Melt butter (6 Tbsp.) or margarine in a large pot. Stir in cornstarch (4 Tbsp.), salt (2 tsp.) and pepper (½ tsp.).
3. When smooth add the milk (5 cups) and the cheese.
4. Add the potatoes and onion. Heat through. Serve.
