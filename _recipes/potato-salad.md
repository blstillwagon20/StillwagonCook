## Ingredients

- 8–10 potatoes
- ½ cup onions
- 6 eggs
- ½ cup chopped celery (optional)

### Dressing

- 3–4 Tbsp. mayonnaise
- 4 eggs
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup vinegar
- 1 can evaporated milk
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Steps

1. Boil eggs and potatoes (8–10). Mix with onions (½ cup) and celery (½ cup).
2. To make dressing, combine mayonnaise (3–4 Tbsp.), eggs, sugar (½ cup), and vinegar (½ cup). Cook until it thickens while stirring constantly.
3. Then add evaporated milk (1 can), and salt and pepper to taste.
4. Pour over potato mixture.
