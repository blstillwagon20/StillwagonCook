## Ingredients

- 2 lb. ground beef
- 2 eggs
- 1½ cups bread crumbs
- ¾ cup ketchup, plus more for the top
- 1 pkg. onion soup mix or pkg. of onion flavoring
- ½ cup warm water
- A few carrots

## Steps

1. Add onion soup mix (1 pkg.) to the warm water (½ cup). Slightly beat eggs (2).
2. Combine all ingredients. Shape into loaf. Top with ketchup (¾ cup).
3. Surround with a few carrots.
4. Cover and bake 1 to 1½ hours at 350°. Add a little water to pan if it dries out during baking.
