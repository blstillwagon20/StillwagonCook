> This recipe makes 2 loaves. Very good; may be frozen.

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs, slightly beaten
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup oil
- 3 tsp. vanilla
- 2 cups grated zucchini
- 1 cup chopped nuts

### Blend the following

- 3 cups sifted flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 3 tsp. cinnamon
- 2 tsp. baking powder

## Steps

1. Beat eggs (3) slightly; add sugar (2 cups) and cream well. Add the oil (1 cup); blend well.
2. Add the vanilla (3 tsp.), zucchini (2 cups), nuts (1 cup) and finally the flour (3 cups) mixture gradually.
3. Grease 2 bread pans very well and line with waxed paper, just the bottom of pans. Divide dough evenly in pans.
4. Bake at 325° for 60 minutes. Let set in pans for a few minutes and remove and cool.
