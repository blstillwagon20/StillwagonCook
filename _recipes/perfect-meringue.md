## Ingredients

- 4 egg whites
- 3 Tbsp. ice water
- 1 Tbsp. baking powder
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Steps

1. Beat egg whites (4), ice water (3 Tbsp.) and baking powder (1 Tbsp.) until very stiff in a bowl that has been chilled in the freezer.
2. Fold in gradually sugar (½ cup) and vanilla (1 tsp.).

If using this for coconut cream pie, top with sprinkled coconut.
