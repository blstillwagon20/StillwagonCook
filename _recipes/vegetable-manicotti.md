## Ingredients

- 8 uncooked manicotti shells
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 1 tsp. olive or vegetable oil
- ½ cup shredded carrot
- ½ cup shredded zucchini
- ½ cup sliced mushrooms
- ¼ cup sliced green onions
- 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
- 2 cups nonfat ricotta cheese
- ¼ cup grated parmesan cheese
- 2 Tbsp. chopped fresh or 2 tsp. dried basil leaves
- 2 egg whites
- ½ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella cheese

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 350°. Spray rectangular baking dish, 11" x 7" x 1½", with nonstick cooking spray.
2. Cook manicotti shells (8) as directed on package.
3. Pour ⅓ cup of tomato sauce into baking dish.
4. Heat oil in 10-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Cook carrots (½ cup), zucchini (½ cup), mushrooms (½ cup), onions (¼ cup), and garlic (1 clove) in oil, stirring frequently, until vegetables are crisp-tender.
5. Stir in remaining ingredients except mozzarella cheese (½ cup).
6. Fill manicotti shells with vegetable mixture; place in baking dish. Pour remaining tomato sauce over manicotti. Sprinkle with mozzarella cheese.
7. Cover and bake 40 to 45 minutes or until hot and bubbly.
