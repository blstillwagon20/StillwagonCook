## Ingredients

- 6 oz. apricot Jello
- 1 (2 lb.) can crushed pineapple
- ¾ cup sugar
- 2 small jars strained baby apricot
- 8 oz. cream cheese
- 13 oz. can chilled evaporated milk
- ⅔ cup chopped walnuts

## Steps

1. Heat Jello (6 oz.) (little or no water) and pineapple (1 can, 2 lb.) until simmers.
2. Add sugar (¾ cup), strained apricots (2 small jars) and cream cheese (8 oz.). Heat and stir until cheese melts.
3. Chill until slightly thickened (1 or 2 hours).
4. Beat canned milk until thick and foamy; add nuts; stir into Jello mixture thoroughly.
5. Chill until firm and serve.
