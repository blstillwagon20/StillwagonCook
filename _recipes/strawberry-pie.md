## Ingredients

- 1 graham cracker pie crust
- ¼ cup cornstarch
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1 (3 oz.) box strawberry Jell-O
- 1 qt. strawberries
- Cool Whip

## Steps

1. Bring to boil cornstarch (¼ cup), sugar (1 cup), and water (1 cup), stirring constantly.
2. Remove from heat and add Jell-O (1 box, 3 oz.). Let cool.
3. Add strawberries (1 qt.). Put filling in pie crust (1).
4. Top with Cool Whip.
