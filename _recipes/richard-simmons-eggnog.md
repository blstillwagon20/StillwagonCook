## Ingredients

- 1½ cup liquid egg product
- 1–2 T. liquid sweetener (I used Equal packets instead)
- 2 Tbsp. vanilla extract
- 1 Tbsp. brandy extract (optional)
- 3 cups skim milk
- 1½ cup evaporated skim milk
- Freshly grated nutmeg to garnish

## Steps

1. Put all above ingredients except nutmeg in blender and blend until frothy.
2. Garnish with nutmeg.
