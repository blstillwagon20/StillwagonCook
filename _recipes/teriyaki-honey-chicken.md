> I got this recipe from a magazine and it is quite good.

## Ingredients

- 1 cut up fryer chicken
- ½ cup honey
- ½ cup soy sauce
- 1 tsp. grated fresh ginger
- 2 medium garlic cloves, crushed
- ¼ cup water

## Steps

1. Place chicken (1) in plastic bag. Combine remaining ingredients and marinate chicken in this for at least 6 hours, turning 2 to 3 times.
2. Bake on rack on top of another pan lined with foil (this burns on pans and won't come off!) 30 minutes at 350°, covered with foil.
3. Remove foil and brush with marinade. Bake uncovered 30 to 45 minutes or until done, brushing with marinade often.
