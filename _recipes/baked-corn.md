## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk
- 2 cups cracker crumbs
- 2 eggs
- 1 qt. cream corn
- ½ tsp. salt
- ⅛ tsp. pepper
- 3 tsp. sugar
- 1 Tbsp. grated onion
- 3 Tbsp. butter

## Steps

1. Beat eggs (2) slightly; add milk (2 cups), cracker crumbs (2 cups), corn (1 qt.), salt (½ tsp.), pepper (⅛ tsp.), and onion (1 Tbsp.).
2. Pour in greased casserole and dot with butter (3 Tbsp.).
3. Set casserole in pan of water and bake at 350° for 50 to 60 minutes or until thick.
