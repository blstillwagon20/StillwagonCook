## Ingredients

- 4 pork loin or rib chops, about ½" thick (1 lb.)
- 3 medium parsnips, cut crosswise into ½" slices
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- ½ cup chicken broth
- 1 tsp. ground mustard
- ¼ tsp. ground allspice
- ⅛ tsp. pepper
- Salt
- 2 Tbsp. chopped fresh parsley

## Steps

1. Trim fat from pork chops. Cook pork in 10-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat, turning once, until brown; drain.
2. Place parsnips (3 medium) and onion (1 medium) on pork.
3. Mix broth (½ cup), mustard (1 tsp.), salt, allspice (¼ tsp.) and pepper (⅛ tsp.); pour over vegetables and pork.
4. Heat to boiling; reduce heat. Cover and simmer about 30 minutes or until pork is tender.
5. Sprinkle with parsley (2 Tbsp.).
