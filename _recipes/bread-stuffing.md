## Ingredients

- 1½ cups chopped celery (with leaves)
- ¾ cup finely chopped onion
- ¾ cup margarine or butter (or if you're watching your fat intake, substitute ½ cup chicken broth and just ¼ cup margarine. This tastes just as good and is good for the heart.)
- 9 cups soft bread cubes
- 1 tsp. ground sage
- 1 tsp. dried thyme leaves
- ¼ tsp. pepper

## Steps

1. Cook and stir celery and onion (¾ cup) in margarine (¾ cup) in Dutch oven until celery and onion are very soft; remove from heat.
2. Stir in remaining ingredients. Makes 5 cups stuffing.

I usually boil a container of chicken livers till done, chop, then add this to the stuffing also. It makes it very tasty. Also try adding some golden raisins. I did this when I stuffed an orange glazed duck and it was just terrific!
