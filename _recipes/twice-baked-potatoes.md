## Ingredients

- 4 medium baking potatoes
- 1 cup plain non-fat yogurt
- ¼ cup parmesan cheese
- 2 Tbsp. freeze-dried chives or parsley
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ⅛ tsp. pepper
- Paprika

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 400°. Bake potatoes (4 medium) about 1 hour or until tender (or microwave if you don't have the time to bake).
2. Cut a thin length-wise slice from each potato and scoop out potato, leaving a thin shell.
3. Mash potatoes until no lumps remain. Beat in remaining ingredients, except paprika.
4. Stuff mashed potato mixture into shell and sprinkle with paprika.
5. Place on cookie sheet. Bake about 20 minutes or until filling is golden brown.
