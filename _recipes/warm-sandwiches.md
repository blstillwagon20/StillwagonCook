## Ingredients

- ½ lb. lunch meat
- ½ lb. diced American cheese
- ⅓ cup olives
- 2 hard boiled eggs
- 3 Tbsp. mayonnaise
- ½ cup chili sauce
- Buns

## Steps

1. Mix the above together. Put on buns.
2. Wrap buns in foil.
3. Bake for 10 minutes in 400° oven.
