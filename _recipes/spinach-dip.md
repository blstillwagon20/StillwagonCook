> This is our easy go-to for any party.

## Ingredients

- 1 package frozen spinach
- 1 (8 oz.) block hot pepper jack cheese
- 1 (8 oz.) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 (16 oz.) container sour cream (almost 2 cups)
- Tortilla chips or crackers, for serving

## Steps

1. Thaw frozen spinach (1 package) on stove top and drain.
2. Cube pepper jack cheese (1 block, 8 oz.).
3. Mix sour cream (1 container, 16 oz.), cream cheese (1 package, 8 oz.), spinach and cubed cheese.
4. Heat in microwave, stirring every few minutes, until hot.
5. Serve hot with tortilla chips or crackers.
