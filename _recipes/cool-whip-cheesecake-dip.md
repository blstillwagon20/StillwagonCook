## Ingredients

- 3 oz. cream cheese, softened
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 3 Tbsp. lowfat milk
- 2 cups Cool Whip Lite whipped topping
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Steps

1. Beat cheese (3 oz.), sugar (2 Tbsp.), and milk (3 Tbsp.) until well blended and smooth.
2. Fold in whipped topping (2 cups) and vanilla (1 tsp.). Store in refrigerator.
3. Use to dip cookies, cake cubes, fresh fruit, pretzel sticks, etc.

Makes approximately 2 cups — 6 calories per Tbsp.
