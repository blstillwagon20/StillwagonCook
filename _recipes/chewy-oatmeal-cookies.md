> This is a wonderfully chewy recipe for oatmeal cookies from my sister, Vicki.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups quick rolled oats
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- ½ cup vegetable oil
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. vanilla
- 2 egg whites

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 375°. Stir together oats (2 cups), sugar, and oil (½ cup) in mixing bowl. Beat egg whites (2) until frothy and add to oat mixture. Stir in salt (¼ tsp.) and vanilla (½ tsp.).
2. Drop mixture by teaspoonfuls on lightly oiled baking sheet. Bake about 15 minutes.
3. Cool, remove from baking sheet. Makes 3 dozen cookies.
