> I usually serve this for Thanksgiving and it's very good!

## Ingredients

### Dissolve

- 1 pkg. cherry Jell-O
- 2 cups hot water
- 2 cups sugar

### Add

- 1 can crushed pineapple
- 2 cups grapes
- 2 cups celery
- 1 cup chopped nuts
- 2 cups ground cranberries

## Steps

1. Dissolve the Jell-O (1 pkg.) and sugar (2 cups) in the hot water (2 cups).
2. Add pineapple (1 can), grapes (2 cups), celery (2 cups), nuts (1 cup) and cranberries (2 cups).
3. Refrigerate until set.
