## Ingredients

- 1 cup quick-cooking oats
- 1 cup nonfat milk or buttermilk
- ¾ cup packed brown sugar
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
- 2 Tbsp. light molasses
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2 egg whites or ¼ cup cholesterol-free egg product
- 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
- 1¼ tsp. ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 cup chopped fresh, frozen (thawed) or canned (well drained) peaches
- Sugar, for sprinkling (optional)

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 400°. Spray bottoms only of 12 medium muffin cups, 2½ x 1¼ inches, with nonstick cooking spray, or line with paper baking cups.
2. Mix oats (1 cup) and milk (1 cup) in large bowl. Beat in brown sugar (¾ cup), oil (¼ cup), molasses (2 Tbsp.), vanilla (1 tsp.), and egg whites (2).
3. Stir in remaining ingredients except peaches just until flour (1¼ cups) is moistened (batter will be lumpy). Fold in peaches.
4. Divide batter evenly among muffin cups (cups will be full). Sprinkle with sugar if desired.
5. Bake about 20 minutes or until golden brown. Immediately remove from pan.
