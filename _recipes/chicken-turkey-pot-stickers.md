## Ingredients

- 1½ lbs. ground chicken or turkey
- ½ cup finely chopped red pepper
- ½ cup shredded green cabbage
- ⅓ cup chopped green onion
- 2 tsp. chopped ginger root
- 1 tsp. sesame oil or regular oil
- ¼ tsp. white pepper
- 1 egg white
- 1 pkg. wonton skins
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 4 tsp. reduced-sodium soy sauce

## Steps

1. Mix all ingredients except wonton skins (1 pkg.), broth (2 cups), and soy sauce (4 tsp.).
2. Brush each wonton skin with water. Place a scant Tbsp. of chicken (1½ lbs.)/turkey mixture on center of skin. Fold skin over and pinch edge to seal. Repeat with remaining skins and mixture.
3. Spray 12 inch skillet with nonstick cooking spray. Heat over medium heat.
4. Cook pot stickers 3 minutes or until light brown; turn. Stir in ½ cup of broth and 1 tsp. of the soy sauce. Cover and cook 5 minutes.
5. Uncover and cook 1 minute longer or until liquid has evaporated.
6. Repeat with remaining pot stickers, broth and soy sauce.
