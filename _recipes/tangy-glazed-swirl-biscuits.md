> These are more of a dessert biscuit that are great with hot tea or coffee.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Bisquick baking mix
- ½ cup milk
- 1 package (3 oz.) cream cheese, softened
- 1 package (3 oz.) fruit-flavored gelatin (whatever you like)

### Glaze

- ½ cup powdered sugar
- 1 Tbsp. margarine or butter, softened
- 3–4 tsp. milk

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 425°. Grease round pan, 9" x 1½".
2. Mix baking mix (2 cups) and milk until dough forms; beat 20 strokes. Turn dough onto cloth covered surface dusted with baking mix; knead 10 times.
3. Roll dough into rectangle 12" x 10". Mix cream cheese (1 package, 3 oz.) and dry gelatin (1 package, 3 oz.); spread over dough.
4. Roll up tightly beginning at 12" end. Pinch edge into roll to seal. Cut into twelve 1" slices. Arrange slices in pan.
5. Bake until golden brown, 12 to 14 minutes. Invert on heatproof serving plate. Remove pan.
6. **Glaze:** Mix powdered sugar (½ cup) and margarine (1 Tbsp.); stir in milk (3–4 tsp.) until smooth and of desired consistency. Drizzle glaze over biscuits. Serve warm.
