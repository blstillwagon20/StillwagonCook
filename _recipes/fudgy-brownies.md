## Ingredients

- 2 squares (1 oz. each) unsweetened chocolate
- ½ cup butter or margarine
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- ½ cup flour
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ½ cup chopped nuts

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 350°. Melt chocolate and butter (½ cup) over low heat, stirring constantly; remove from heat.
2. Mix in sugar (1 cup), eggs (2), and vanilla (1 tsp.). Stir in remaining ingredients.
3. Spread in greased square pan, 9 x 9 x 2 inches. Bake 20 to 25 minutes.
