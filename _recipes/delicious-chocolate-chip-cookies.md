> These are the best chocolate chips cookies I've ever had and a recipe from my sister, Sandy. They always stay soft and chewy.

## Ingredients

- 2¾ cups unsifted flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. salt
- ½ cup softened butter or margarine
- ½ cup shortening
- ¾ cup granulated sugar
- ¾ cup firmly packed brown sugar
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 12 oz. pkg. (2 cups) chocolate bits
- 1 cup chopped nuts (optional) — coconut is good too!

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°. In small bowl, combine flour (2¾ cups), baking soda (1 tsp.) and salt (1 tsp.); set aside.
2. Combine butter (½ cup), shortening (½ cup), sugar (¾ cup), brown sugar (¾ cup) and vanilla (1 tsp.); beat until creamy. Beat in eggs (2).
3. Add flour mixture. Mix well. Stir in chocolate bits and chopped nuts (1 cup) or coconut.
4. Bake on ungreased cookie sheet for 8 to 10 minutes or until lightly browned.
