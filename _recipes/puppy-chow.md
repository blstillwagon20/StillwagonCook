> This is a sweet snack mix that everyone loves to dig into when I make it.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup chocolate chips
- 1 stick butter
- 8 cups Rice Chex
- 2 cups powdered sugar

## Steps

1. Melt peanut butter (1 cup), chocolate chips (1 cup), and butter (1 stick). Pour over Rice Chex (8 cups).
2. Put in large container; sprinkle sugar over this mixture.
3. Close with lid and shake. You may need to add more powdered sugar (2 cups) if necessary.
