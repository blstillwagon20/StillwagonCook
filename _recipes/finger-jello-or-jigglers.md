## Ingredients

- 2 family size pkgs. (8 serving size) or 4 small pkgs. Jello
- 2½ cups boiling water

## Steps

1. Dissolve gelatin in boiling water (2½ cups). Pour into 9" x 13" pan.
2. Chill (about 3 hours).
3. To unmold, dip pan in warm water about 15 seconds. Cut into squares or use cookie cutter. Lift from pan.
