## Ingredients

- 1¼ cup flour
- ¾ tsp. baking powder
- ¼ tsp. baking soda
- ¼ tsp. salt
- Pinch nutmeg
- Pinch cinnamon
- 1 egg at room temp.
- ⅓ cup sugar
- ⅓ cup buttermilk
- 2 Tbsp. melted butter
- Oil, for frying
- Confectioner sugar

## Steps

1. Sift flour (1¼ cup), baking powder (¾ tsp.), soda (¼ tsp.), and salt (¼ tsp.) together. Add nutmeg and cinnamon.
2. In a bowl, whisk together egg, sugar (⅓ cup), buttermilk (⅓ cup), and melted butter (2 Tbsp.). Combine with dry ingredients and mix until smooth.
3. Heat oil to 375°. Drop batter by teaspoonful into oil. Let dough fry until nicely browned, about 2 minutes, turning halfway through cooking.
4. With a slotted spoon transfer them to paper towel to drain. While donuts are still hot, shake them in confectioner sugar to coat.

For a nice change, you can add cinnamon or cocoa to the confectioner sugar and then coat donuts in this.
