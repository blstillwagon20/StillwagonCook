## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp. flour
- 1 cup sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 1 Tbsp. melted butter
- Juice and rind of 1 lemon
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 cup milk
- 2 egg whites
- Candies or maraschino cherry, to decorate

## Steps

1. Stir together flour (3 Tbsp.), sugar (1 cup) and salt. Add melted butter (1 Tbsp.) and the juice and rind of the lemon.
2. Lightly beat egg yolks (2) and milk (1 cup). Add to flour mixture.
3. Beat egg whites (2) until stiff. Fold into mixture. Pour into 6 lightly buttered custard cups.
4. Set in baking pan filled with 1 inch of water. Bake for 30 minutes in a 350° oven.
5. Cool before removing cakes. Decorate with half of candies or maraschino cherry pressed in center.
