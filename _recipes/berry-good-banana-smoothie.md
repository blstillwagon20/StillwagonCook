> These are blender delights made from yogurt, fruit, and milk.

## Ingredients

- 2 bananas
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 cups strawberries (frozen are okay, too)

## Steps

1. Peel and slice the bananas (2). Wash and cut off the tops of the strawberries (2 cups) (if using fresh).
2. Put into the blender with the milk (1 cup) and yogurt (1 cup) and whirl away.

## Variations

**Apple Advantage:** Instead of the yogurt and milk, add ½ cup apple juice, 1 cup crushed ice and 1 Tbsp. honey.

**M&M Powdered:** Use only ½ cup milk and substitute ½ cup vanilla ice cream for the yogurt. Then add a handful of crushed ice and a small bag of M&M's.
