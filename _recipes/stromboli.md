## Ingredients

### Dough

- 5½ to 6 cups flour
- 2 pkgs. yeast
- ½ cup sugar
- 1½ tsp. salt
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup water
- ¼ cup butter and margarine
- 2 eggs

### Filling

- Your favorite lunch meats, cheeses and vegetables
- A couple Tbsp. of your favorite spaghetti sauce

## Steps

1. **Dough:** In large mixing bowl, combine 2 cups flour, yeast (2 pkgs.), sugar (½ cup) and salt (1½ tsp.).
2. In saucepan heat milk (1 cup), water (1 cup), and butter until very warm. Add to flour mixture. Add eggs (2). Blend on low speed until moistened; beat 3 minutes at medium speed.
3. By hand, stir in enough remaining flour to make a soft dough. Knead until smooth. Place in greased bowl. Cover and let rise in warm place until doubled.
4. **To make stromboli:** Punch down dough. Divide into 6 parts. On lightly floured surface, roll out each part of dough into a rectangle shape.
5. Put favorite lunch meats, cheeses, vegetables, and a couple of Tbsp. of your favorite spaghetti sauce on half. Fold dough in half over filling.
6. Place on cookie sheet and bake at 350° about 25 minutes until dough is brown.
