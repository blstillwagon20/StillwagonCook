## Ingredients

- 4 cups sliced peaches
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 Tbsp. lemon juice
- 2 cups Bisquick
- 4 Tbsp. melted butter
- About ⅔ cup milk

## Steps

1. Arrange peaches (4 cups) in a greased 9" baking dish.
2. Mix sugar (1 cup) and lemon juice (1 Tbsp.) together and sprinkle over fruit. Place in a 400° oven for 15 minutes.
3. Mix Bisquick (2 cups), melted butter (4 Tbsp.) and about ⅔ cup milk (to make a soft dough).
4. Remove peach mixture from oven. Drop dough onto hot peaches, making small biscuits.
5. Bake at 400° uncovered for 10 to 15 minutes (until biscuits are golden brown). Bake an additional 10 to 15 minutes covered, for a total of about 30 minutes.
