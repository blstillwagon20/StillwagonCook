## Ingredients

- 4½ cups water
- 2 envelopes Lipton chicken noodle soup
- 1 cup uncooked rice
- 1 lb. sausage
- 1½ cups diced celery
- 1 medium onion, diced
- ½ cup chopped green pepper
- ⅔ cup slivered almonds

## Steps

1. Bring water (4½ cups) and soup (2 envelopes) to boil; add rice (1 cup) and cover. Reduce heat. Gently boil 25 minutes or until absorbed.
2. Fry sausage (1 lb.). Pour off fat.
3. Sauté celery (1½ cups), onion (1 medium), and green pepper (½ cup).
4. Add rice and sausage and half of almonds (⅔ cup). Put in 2 qt. casserole. Top with remaining almonds.
5. Cover and bake at 350° for 30 minutes.
