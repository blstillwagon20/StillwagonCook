## Ingredients

- ½ cup milk
- 3 Tbsp. sugar
- 2 tsp. salt
- 3 Tbsp. melted Crisco shortening
- 1½ cups cool water
- 1 package dry yeast
- About 5½ cups flour
- Butter, for the top (optional)

## Steps

1. Scald milk (½ cup); stir in sugar (3 Tbsp.), salt (2 tsp.), and melted Crisco. Add cool water (1½ cups) to make milk mixture a warm temperature.
2. Sprinkle in yeast (1 package). Stir until dissolved.
3. Add 3 cups flour. Beat until smooth. Add about 2½ more cups of flour (until it's not sticky but still very soft), adding a little at a time.
4. Knead until smooth and elastic (8 to 10 minutes). Put in greased bowl. Turn it around and flip to grease all over. Cover and let rise in warm place until double, about 1 hour.
5. Punch down and let rest 15 minutes. Divide dough in half; shape into 2 loaves. Put into two greased loaf pans. Let rise about 1 more hour, covered.
6. Bake at 350° about 30 minutes until golden brown.
7. Take out of pan immediately and place on wire rack to cool. Rub butter all over the top if you like a nice soft crust.
