## Ingredients

### Mix together

- 4 cups beef stock
- 1 oz. dry mushroom

### Sauté

- 1 bunch scallions
- 3 Tbsp. butter
- 1 clove garlic

### To finish

- 3 Tbsp. flour
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Mix beef stock (4 cups) and dry mushroom together.
2. Sauté the scallions (1 bunch) and garlic in the butter (3 Tbsp.). Mix altogether, then add 3 Tbsp. flour (may look lumpy).
3. Put sauté mixture into beef stock. Stir altogether.
4. Then add heavy cream (1 cup), salt, and pepper.

## Lowfat version

- 6 cups water
- 4 cubes beef bouillon
- ½ stalk celery
- 2 bunches green onion
- 1 clove garlic
- 12 oz. fresh mushrooms, sliced
- ½ to ¾ cup water mixed with 3 Tbsp. flour
- 1 cup skim milk
- Dash of salt and pepper

1. Bring water (6 cups) and bouillon (4) to a boil, then set on low heat. Add celery (½ stalk) (take this out after soup is done).
2. Sauté the green onion (2 bunches), garlic and mushrooms: cook in a little water, then put in beef broth.
3. Mix ½ to ¾ cup water with 3 Tbsp. flour until smooth. Put in beef broth. Bring to a boil and boil for 1 minute to thicken.
4. Then add skim milk (1 cup) and a dash of salt and pepper.
