## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup salt
- Water

## Steps

1. Mix flour (2 cups) and salt (1 cup) with just enough water to make a rubbery and soft feeling mixture.
2. Store in a plastic container with tight fitting lid, to prevent drying out.
