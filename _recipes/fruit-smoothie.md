> This is a wonderful type of fruit shake that everyone loves and its great for you too!

I like to use mostly frozen fruit for this but you can use fresh or canned too. The frozen fruit just helps it stay slushy more.

## Ingredients

- ½ of a banana
- 1 cup strawberries
- Any other fruit you like (only fill to about ½ or a little more)
- Fruit juice, to cover the fruit
- ¼ cup evaporated milk (or a little powdered milk)
- A couple packs of Equal or 1 Tbsp. honey
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- Some ice

## Steps

1. In blender combine banana (½), strawberries (1 cup) and any other fruit you like.
2. Add enough fruit juice to cover fruit. I like to use either apple or pineapple if I have it but any will do. Add evaporated milk (¼ cup), Equal or honey, and vanilla (1 tsp.), and turn on the blender until smooth.
3. Add some ice and blend til ice is ground up to make thick like a shake.

There's really no specific recipe for this, and it's all good. Just add what you like and create your own. Serve this with a straw, whipped cream on top and a piece of fruit on top.
