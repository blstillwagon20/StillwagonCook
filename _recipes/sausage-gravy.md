## Ingredients

- 1 lb. sausage
- 2 Tbsp. chopped onion
- 6 Tbsp. flour
- 1 qt. milk
- ½ tsp. poultry seasoning
- ½ tsp. nutmeg
- ¼ tsp. salt
- Dash Worcestershire sauce
- Dash hot pepper sauce
- Biscuits, for serving

## Steps

1. Cook sausage (1 lb.). Add onion (2 Tbsp.) and cook until soft.
2. Drain, keeping 2 Tbsp. of drippings. Stir flour (6 Tbsp.) in. Stir in milk (1 qt.).
3. Add seasonings (½ tsp.). Cook until thickened.
4. Serve over biscuits.
