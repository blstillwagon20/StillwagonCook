## Ingredients

- 1 can (14 oz.) sweetened condensed milk
- 1 package (12 oz.) semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 Tbsp. margarine or butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Steps

1. Heat sweetened condensed milk (1 can, 14 oz.) and chocolate chips (1 package, 12 oz.) until melted. (I prefer to do this in the microwave stirring at 1 minute intervals until melted.)
2. Stir in butter and vanilla (1 tsp.).
3. Serve over your favorite ice cream or dessert. This is truly too delicious!
