> I got this recipe from my mother-in-law and it's great for a crowd and completely vegetarian. It's tasty and quick to prepare.

## Ingredients

- 2 pkgs. crescent rolls
- 2 (8 oz.) pkgs. cream cheese
- ⅔ cup mayonnaise
- Dry ranch dressing mix
- Shredded mozzarella and cheddar cheese
- Chopped vegetables: broccoli, mushroom, tomato, cauliflower, green or red pepper, or any vegetable of your choice

## Steps

1. Unroll crescent roll dough and form into a 9" x 13" pan. Prick with fork and bake crust at 350° for 10 minutes or until brown. Cool.
2. Meanwhile, mix cream cheese (2 pkgs., 8 oz.), mayonnaise (⅔ cup), and ranch dressing mix until smooth.
3. When crust is cool, spread cream cheese mixture onto it, then top with mozzarella and cheddar cheese and the vegetables.

**Tip:** Fat-free or light versions of the cream cheese and mayonnaise work just as well!
