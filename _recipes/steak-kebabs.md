> This recipe is just delicious on steak, chicken, or even tofu cubes. I hope you try it!

## Ingredients

- 4 Tbsp. soy sauce
- 4 Tbsp. honey
- 2 Tbsp. grated fresh ginger or 1 tsp. ground ginger
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp. grated lemon peel
- ¼ tsp. cayenne pepper
- 12 ounces boneless sirloin steak, chicken, or tofu, trimmed and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 medium zucchini, cut into ½ inch slices
- 1 red bell pepper, cored, seeded, and cut into 8 squares
- Pineapple cubes
- Mushrooms

## Steps

1. In a shallow glass dish, combine soy sauce (4 Tbsp.), honey (4 Tbsp.), ginger (2 Tbsp.), garlic (2 cloves), lemon peel (2 tsp.), and cayenne pepper (¼ tsp.). Mix well. Remove half of this mixture and save for basting the meat and vegetables while they cook.
2. To the other half, add the meat; stir to coat. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 1 to 2 hours, stirring occasionally.
3. Preheat broiler or grill. Remove meat from the marinade. Discard this marinade.
4. Using four 10-inch metal skewers, alternately thread beef, zucchini (1 medium), pineapple, mushrooms, and red pepper. Place on broiler pan or grill.
5. Broil 2 inches from heat, turning 2 or 3 times and basting everything on the skewers frequently with the remaining marinade, until meat (if using steak (12 ounces)) is medium-rare, chicken has no pink remaining and vegetables are lightly browned, about 10 to 15 minutes depending on your broiler or grill. Check it frequently so that it doesn't overcook.

I like to serve this with rice or a baked potato. Enjoy!
