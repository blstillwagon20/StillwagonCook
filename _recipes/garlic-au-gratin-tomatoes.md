## Ingredients

- 8 medium tomatoes
- 1½ cups soft bread crumbs
- 2 to 4 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ cup grated Parmesan or Romano cheese
- ⅓ cup chopped fresh parsley
- ½ tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- ⅓ cup olive or vegetable oil
- 8 oz. mozzarella cheese, shredded

## Steps

1. Cut a thin slice from the top of each tomato. Scoop out the pulp, leaving a ½-inch-thick shell. Invert tomatoes (8 medium) onto paper towels to drain.
2. Meanwhile, in a bowl, combine crumbs (1½ cups), garlic, Parmesan (½ cup) or Romano cheese, parsley (⅓ cup), salt (½ tsp.) and pepper (¼ tsp.).
3. Stuff tomatoes; place in a greased shallow baking dish. Drizzle with oil.
4. Top with mozzarella cheese (8 oz.).
5. Bake, uncovered, at 400° for 15 to 20 minutes or until lightly browned on top.
