> This recipe is from my Aunt Judy and is delicious. I like to serve it with mashed potatoes, steamed fresh green beans and dinner rolls.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb. boneless veal shoulder, cut in 1½" cubes
- 1 lb. pork butt roast, cut in 1½" cubes
- ½ cup fine cracker crumbs
- ½ cup corn flake crumbs
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. paprika
- ¾ tsp. poultry seasoning
- Dash of pepper
- 1 slightly beaten egg
- Milk (no amount given)
- 2 Tbsp. fat
- 1 chicken bouillon cube
- ¾ cup hot water

## Steps

1. Push veal and pork cubes alternately onto 6 skewers.
2. Combine cracker and corn flake crumbs (½ cup) and seasonings (¾ tsp.). Combine egg (1) and milk.
3. Dip meat in egg mixture, then in crumbs. Brown slowly on all sides in hot fat (2 Tbsp.).
4. Dissolve bouillon cube (1) in ¾ cup hot water; add to meat.
5. Cover tightly; simmer 1 hour or until tender (or place on rack in roaster pan, cover, and bake in moderate oven (350°) until tender).
6. Serve meat liquid as gravy, thickened with a little flour and water if necessary.
