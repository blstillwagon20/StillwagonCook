## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- ½ cup water
- 2 cups flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- Your own mashed potatoes, for filling

## Steps

1. Mix; roll out. Cut in circles.
2. Fill with small amount of your own mashed potatoes recipe (as long as they're not runny).
3. Fold over the dough and seal.
4. Cook 5 minutes in boiling water (½ cup).
