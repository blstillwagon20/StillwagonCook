## Ingredients

- 4 to 5 lb. beef brisket
- 1 tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. pepper

## Steps

1. Put meat, salt (1 tsp.) and pepper (½ tsp.) in baking dish.
2. Cover with water and bake 3 hours at 325°.
3. Have Dad over for dinner and serve to Buddy (the dog). HaHa!
