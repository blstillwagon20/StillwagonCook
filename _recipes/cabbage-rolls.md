> This recipe makes a bunch. I usually put it all in a big roasting pan. It freezes well, too.

## Ingredients

- 1 large head of cabbage or 2 medium, cored, boiled until leaves are tender and drained

### Filling

- 1 cup uncooked regular white rice, cooked according to package directions
- 1½ cups finely diced onion, sautéed in a little oil till tender
- 1 cup tomato sauce (taken from the sauce below)
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ¾ tsp. pepper
- ½ tsp. garlic powder
- 2 lbs. ground beef or turkey

### Sauce

- 3 (29 oz. each) cans tomato sauce, from which 1 cup has been removed for the filling
- 2½ tsp. sugar
- 1½ tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 1 cup water

## Steps

1. Mix together the filling ingredients in a big bowl.
2. Put about large meatball size amounts of this mixture into leaves of cabbage (1 large head) and roll up.
3. Mix the sauce ingredients together.
4. Pour about ⅓ of the sauce in the bottom of a roasting pan. Place the cabbage rolls on top of this.
5. Put another ⅓ of sauce and another layer of cabbage rolls. Place the last ⅓ of sauce on top.
6. Bake in a 350° oven for about 2 hours, checking it occasionally to see if it needs a little water (1 cup) now and then.

This is delicious served with mashed potatoes.
