> These are the best gingerbread cookies I've ever had and the recipe is from my Aunt Sue.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter
- ½ cup Crisco
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup dark brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- ¼ cup light molasses
- 3 cups flour
- ¼ tsp. baking soda
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. cloves
- ½ tsp. ginger
- ½ tsp. nutmeg

## Steps

1. Cream butter (½ cup) and Crisco (½ cup) with sugar (½ cup) and brown sugar (½ cup). Add egg (1 large) and molasses (¼ cup).
2. In a separate bowl, combine flour (3 cups), soda (¼ tsp.), salt (¼ tsp.), and spices. Mix with the above mixture.
3. Chill dough. Roll out dough in sugar, cut into desired shapes and bake at 350° for 7 minutes.
