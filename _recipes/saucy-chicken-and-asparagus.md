## Ingredients

- 1½ lbs. fresh asparagus spears, halved
- 4 boneless skinless chicken breast halves
- 2 Tbsp. cooking oil
- ½ tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 1 can (10¾ oz.) condensed cream of chicken soup, undiluted
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 1 tsp. lemon juice
- ½ tsp. curry powder
- 1 cup (4 oz.) shredded cheddar cheese

## Steps

1. If desired, partially cook asparagus; drain. Place the asparagus in a greased 9" square baking dish.
2. In a skillet over medium heat, brown the chicken in oil (2 Tbsp.) on both sides. Season with salt (½ tsp.) and pepper (¼ tsp.). Arrange chicken over asparagus.
3. In a bowl, mix soup, mayonnaise (½ cup), lemon juice (1 tsp.) and curry powder (½ tsp.); pour over chicken.
4. Cover and bake at 375° for 40 minutes or until the chicken is tender and juices run clear.
5. Sprinkle with cheese (1 cup, 4 oz.). Let stand 5 minutes before serving.
