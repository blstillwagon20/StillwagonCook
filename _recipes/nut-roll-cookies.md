> This is another recipe from my mother that we always made for Christmas. They're great right out of the freezer.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 cake yeast or package
- 1¼ cups butter
- ½ cup sour cream
- 5 egg yolks or 3 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla

### Nut filling

- 1 cup ground walnuts
- ½ cup sugar
- Enough milk to moisten

## Steps

1. Add flour (4 cups), salt (1 tsp.) and crumbled yeast together. Blend in butter (1¼ cups) with fingers or pastry blender until mixture resembles fine corn meal.
2. Combine sour cream (½ cup), eggs, and vanilla (1 tsp.). Add to flour mixture and mix thoroughly. Divide dough into 6 portions and roll out each into 9" circles.
3. Spread filling over each circle. Cut each circle into 12 or more pie shaped wedges and roll each one from outside to pointed end.
4. Bake at 325° for 30 minutes. Roll in sugar (½ cup).
