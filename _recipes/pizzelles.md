## Ingredients

- 12 eggs
- 1½ cups oleo
- 1¼ cups Crisco
- 4 cups sugar
- 1 tsp. anise oil
- 1 tsp. lemon extract
- 1 Tbsp. anise seed
- 10 cups flour

## Steps

1. Beat eggs (12), sugar (4 cups), oil (1 tsp.), lemon extract (1 tsp.) and seed (1 Tbsp.).
2. Heat and melt oleo (1½ cups) and Crisco (1¼ cups). Let cool. Add to egg mixture.
3. Pour in flour (10 cups) and form into walnut size balls. Chill. Makes 12 dozen.
