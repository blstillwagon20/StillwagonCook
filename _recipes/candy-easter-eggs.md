> This is a very versatile recipe for homemade candy easter eggs. It can be used as a base for any ingredients you might want to add to it such as nuts, coconut, dried fruit, or even Oreo cookies.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups sugar
- 1 cup lite corn syrup (Karo)
- 1 cup water
- 1 egg white, stiffly beaten
- Coconut, nuts, dried fruit, Oreo cookies, maple flavoring, etc.
- Chocolate compound coating

## Steps

1. Put the sugar (3 cups), corn syrup (1 cup) and water (1 cup) in a medium saucepan and boil for 1 minute. Take out 1 cup and add to the stiffly beaten white of one egg with the mixer.
2. Boil remaining syrup until it is at a very hard ball stage in cold water. Add to egg white (1) while mixer is running in a steady stream. Mix until it holds its shape.
3. You can use this mixture plain or add any number of ingredients to it. For example: coconut, nuts, dried fruit, maple flavoring, Oreo cookies, etc.
4. Shape into egg shapes and place on waxed paper to cool. Coat with chocolate compound coating. These make wonderful gifts for any holiday (just change the shape).
