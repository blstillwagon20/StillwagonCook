## Ingredients

- 2 beef bones or chicken
- 1 or 2 bay leaves
- 8 peppercorns
- 1 small onion, chopped
- Carrots and potatoes — however many you want
- Egg noodles
- 2 tsp. salt
- 2 cans (5.5 oz.) Spicy Hot V-8 juice
- Tomato sauce (mentioned in the directions; no amount given)

## Steps

1. In large pot, combine beef bones (2) or chicken, bay leaves, salt (2 tsp.), peppercorns (8), tomato sauce, V-8 juice, and onion (1 small). Fill pot between ½ and ¾ full with water before combining all of the above.
2. Simmer approximately 1½ to 2 hours.
3. Remove chicken or soup bones and take meat off bones and put meat in pot.
4. Add carrots and potatoes; cook until they're done.
5. While cooking potatoes and carrots, cook egg noodles in a separate pan according to package directions.
6. To serve, place egg noodles in bowl (for individual servings); pour broth and vegetables over noodles.
