## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter, melted
- 1½ cup graham cracker crumbs
- 1 cup chopped nuts
- 1 cup (6 oz.) semi-sweet chocolate bits
- 1⅓ cup flaked coconut
- 1⅓ cups sweetened condensed milk

## Steps

1. Pour melted butter (½ cup) in 9" x 13" pan. Add rest of ingredients in layers, starting with cracker crumbs (1½ cup). Pour condensed milk (1⅓ cups) over all.
2. Bake in 350° oven 25 minutes or until lightly brown.
3. Allow 15 minutes to cool before cutting.
