> This recipe is based off of my mother's own spaghetti sauce. It's very good and I get many compliments on it.

## Ingredients

- 2–3 lbs. lean ground beef or turkey
- 1 lb. fresh mushrooms, sliced
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 large bell pepper, diced
- 1 cup water (use in blender)
- ½ of 46 oz. can tomato juice
- 18 oz. tomato paste
- 2 (1 lb. 12 oz. each) cans diced tomatoes, or you can use fresh tomatoes
- 1½ cups water
- ¾ Tbsp. salt
- 2 bay leaves
- 3 Tbsp. parsley
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 Tbsp. dried oregano
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 1 Tbsp. dried basil
- ¼ tsp. thyme
- 1½ tsp. garlic powder
- 1 tsp. oil

## Steps

1. In skillet, brown ground meat and drain off fat. Put in a large pot.
2. In same skillet, put 1 tsp. oil, onion (1 large), and bell pepper (1 large) and sauté until tender.
3. Place onions and peppers (¼ tsp.) in a blender with 1 cup of water and blend until smooth. Add this to the ground meat.
4. Add all remaining ingredients and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer for 1½ to 2 hours.
5. Don't forget to remove the bay leaves (2) before serving over your favorite pasta.

This is also great when making lasagne.
