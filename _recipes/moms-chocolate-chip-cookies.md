> This is another recipe Mom used often when we were growing up and at Christmas, we used to eat these straight from the freezer.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter
- ½ cup shortening
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2¼ cups flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. hot water
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 10–12 oz. chocolate bits

## Steps

1. Cream butter (½ cup), shortening (½ cup), sugars (½ cup); add eggs (2). Beat well.
2. Add flour (2¼ cups), salt (1 tsp.), and baking soda (1 tsp.) that have been mixed together beforehand. Add hot water (½ tsp.) and vanilla (1 tsp.). Stir in chocolate bits (10–12 oz.).
3. Drop onto ungreased cookie sheet. Bake until done (about 10 minutes) at 350°.
