## Ingredients

- 1 (10 oz.) pkg. Milk Duds
- ¼ cup milk

## Steps

1. In saucepan over medium heat, cook and stir milk duds (1 pkg., 10 oz.) and milk (¼ cup) until smooth. Make 1 cup sauce.
2. Serve warm over ice cream. Refrigerate leftovers.
