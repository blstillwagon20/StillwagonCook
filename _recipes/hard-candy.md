## Ingredients

- 4½ cups sugar
- ¾ cup water
- 1½ cup white Karo corn syrup
- Flavoring oil (9 drops)
- Food coloring
- Powdered sugar

## Steps

1. Stir all together. Boil til hard crack stage. Remove from heat and add flavoring oil and food coloring.
2. Pour into a buttered cookie sheet. Let cool a little and mark into squares. When completely cool and hard, break into pieces and dust with powdered sugar (4½ cups).
3. Use brown lunch bag to keep from sticking together. Put contents of bag into colander and shake excess powdered sugar from candy. Put this powdered sugar back into brown bag and you are ready for next batch of candy.
4. As you are finished with thermometer, pot, and spoon, just put them in hot water (¾ cup) to get clean.
