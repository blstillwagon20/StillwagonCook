## Ingredients

- ¼ cup cornstarch
- ⅛ tsp. salt
- 1 cup sugar
- ⅓ cup lemon juice
- 1½ cups boiling water
- 2 egg yolks, beaten
- 1 lump of butter
- 1 baked pie crust

### Meringue

- 2 egg whites
- Pinch of salt
- A spot of cream of tartar
- 8 Tbsp. sugar

## Steps

1. Mix together cornstarch (¼ cup), salt (⅛ tsp.), sugar and lemon juice (⅓ cup).
2. Add 1½ cups of boiling water. Cook until thick.
3. Stir slowly in beaten egg yolks (2) and cook 1 minute longer, and add one lump of butter (1). Cool and pour into baked pie crust (1).
4. **Meringue:** Beat egg whites (2) until foamy. Add pinch of salt and a spot of cream of tartar. Add sugar (8 Tbsp.). Beat until stiff peaks.
5. Pour over lemon mixture clear to the edges of pie. Bake in a 425° oven for 5 minutes.
