> We love these peppers on our homemade pizzas or just thrown into any Italian dish.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups white vinegar
- 2 cups apple cider vinegar
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon mustard seed
- 1 teaspoon celery seed
- 1 pound banana peppers (tops removed & sliced)
- Jars with lids and rings

## Steps

1. Bring vinegar, sugar (1 cup), mustard seed (1 teaspoon), & celery seed (1 teaspoon) to a boil.
2. Pour brine over peppers (1 pound) to within ½" of the top.
3. Wipe off rim and put lid & ring on. Allow to cool & place in fridge.
4. Leave for at least one week to marinate.

Lasts 3 months in the fridge.
