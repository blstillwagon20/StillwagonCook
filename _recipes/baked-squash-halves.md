## Ingredients

- 3 medium acorn squashes
- Salt to taste
- 3 Tbsp. butter
- 3 Tbsp. brown sugar
- Nutmeg to taste

## Steps

1. Cut squash in half lengthwise, remove seeds and place halves cut side down on cookie sheet.
2. Bake at 350° for about a half hour.
3. Turn halves cut side up. Sprinkle each with salt, butter (3 Tbsp.), brown sugar (3 Tbsp.), and nutmeg.
4. Return to oven and bake 30 minutes more.

**Acorn Supreme:** Fill with 2 tsp. melted butter, ½ cup heavy cream, and ¼ cup maple syrup.
