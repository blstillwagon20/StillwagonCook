## Ingredients

- 2 lb. frozen hash brown potatoes, thawed
- 1 stick oleo
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 small jar Cheez Whiz
- ¼ cup chopped onion
- ¼ cup milk
- 8 oz. sour cream
- Corn flakes

## Steps

1. Butter a 9" x 13" baking dish. Fill with thawed potatoes.
2. Heat in saucepan until melted: soup (1 can), oleo (1 stick), Cheez Whiz (1 small jar), onions (¼ cup), milk (¼ cup) and sour cream (8 oz.).
3. Pour over potatoes. Top with corn flakes and melted oleo.
4. Bake at 350° for 1 hour.
