## Ingredients

- 6 eggs, separated
- 1 quart milk
- ⅔ cup sugar
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- ¾ to 1 cup of rum, brandy or whisky (if desired)
- Whipped cream
- Nutmeg

## Steps

1. Beat together yolks, milk (1 quart), sugar (⅔ cup), and salt (¼ tsp.). Stir in vanilla (1 tsp.) and if desired the rum (¾ to 1 cup), brandy or whisky. Chill for 3 or 4 hours.
2. Whip egg whites until stiff. Fold eggnog mixture into whipped cream. Then gently fold in egg whites.
3. Sprinkle with nutmeg and serve immediately.
