## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup sugar
- ⅓ cup packed lt. brown sugar
- ⅓ cup smooth peanut butter
- ¼ cup margarine, softened
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- Vanilla extract (no amount given)
- 4 cups Rice Chex cereal, crushed to make 2 cups
- 2 cups miniature marshmallows
- 1 cup miniature candy-coated semi-sweet chocolate pieces

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 375°. In large bowl combine sugar (⅓ cup), brown sugar (⅓ cup), peanut butter (⅓ cup), margarine (¼ cup), egg (1), and vanilla extract until well blended; stir in cereal (4 cups).
2. With rubber spatula, spread mixture on ungreased 12" pizza pan. Bake 10 minutes.
3. Remove from oven and sprinkle evenly with marshmallows (2 cups) and chocolate pieces (1 cup). Bake 7 to 10 minutes longer, until lightly browned.
4. Remove from oven and cool completely on wire rack. Refrigerate 1 to 2 hours before cutting into 16 wedges. Store in airtight container in refrigerator.
