## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup butter
- 1½ cup sugar
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 cup mashed bananas
- 4 Tbsp. sour milk + 1 tsp. soda
- 1 cup nuts (optional)
- 2 cups flour
- 2 egg whites
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Steps

1. Cream butter (⅔ cup) and sugar (1½ cup); add the yolks (2) and beat.
2. Add bananas (1 cup), sour milk, flour (2 cups), and vanilla (1 tsp.). Beat egg whites (2) that have been beaten until stiff peaks. Fold egg whites in with the cake mixture.
3. Bake until toothpick comes out clean, about 35 minutes at 350°.
