## Ingredients

- 1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 egg
- ½ tsp. vanilla
- 1¼ cups cooked pumpkin (11 oz.)
- Pinch salt
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- ¼ tsp. ginger
- ¼ tsp. nutmeg
- 2 slightly beaten eggs
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1 unbaked 9-inch pie shell

## Steps

1. Combine first four ingredients and spread evenly in an unbaked pie shell (1). Place in refrigerator until set (20 to 30 minutes).
2. Combine remaining ingredients and pour over the cheese (1 pkg., 8 oz.) mixture.
3. Bake at 350° for 65 minutes. Makes 1 (9 inch) pie.
