> This is a very good marinade for steaks. It makes them terrific!

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup A-1 Steak Sauce
- ½ cup prepared Italian salad dressing
- 1 tsp. garlic powder

## Steps

1. Combine all ingredients.
2. Marinate any beef at least 1 hour.
3. Use leftover marinade to baste beef while cooking.
