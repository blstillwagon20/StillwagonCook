> I got this recipe from a friend of mine who served it for dinner one time when we were there. It's quite good and much easier than regular lasagne.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ lb. ground beef
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 1½ tsp. Worcestershire sauce
- 3 cups water
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 cup tomato paste
- 1½ tsp. sugar
- 1 lb. mozzarella cheese
- 1 tsp. oregano
- 1 lb. uncooked noodles (macaroni or ziti or whatever you like)

## Steps

1. Brown ground beef (1¼ lb.) and onion (1).
2. Stir in the tomato paste (1 cup), water (3 cups) and seasoning. Simmer 5 minutes.
3. Put ½ of noodles in bottom of 9" x 13" pan. Pour ½ of sauce and ½ of cheese over noodles.
4. Repeat with remaining ingredients.
5. Cover and bake at 350° for 1 hour.

If you want to get fancy, you can also add ricotta cheese and parmesan along with the mozzarella.
