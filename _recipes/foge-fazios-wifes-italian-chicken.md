> I got this from my Galley family cookbook. It's very good and easy to prepare. I like to serve this with rice and steamed broccoli and cauliflower.

## Ingredients

- 4 to 5 boned chicken breasts
- 4 eggs
- Progresso Italian bread crumbs
- Butter or margarine
- ¾ can College Inn chicken broth (not the big can)
- 2 small cans drained mushrooms
- 12 oz. grated Monterey Jack cheese

## Steps

1. Cut chicken breasts (4 to 5) into bite-size pieces.
2. Beat eggs (4) and soak chicken pieces in beaten eggs for 8 hours.
3. Bread chicken pieces in Progresso Italian bread crumbs. Brown in butter or margarine.
4. Place in large casserole. Add broth (¾ can), mushrooms (2 small cans) and cheese (12 oz.).
5. Cover and bake at 350° for 50 to 60 minutes.

Everyone has loved this and it serves 8.
