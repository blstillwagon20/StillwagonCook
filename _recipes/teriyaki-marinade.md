> This marinade is great for steak and chicken and wonderful on the grill!

## Ingredients

- 1 (8 oz.) can crushed pineapple (don't drain)
- ¼ cup teriyaki sauce
- 2 Tbsp. lemon juice
- 2 Tbsp. red wine vinegar
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 Tbsp. olive oil
- ¼ tsp. liquid smoke

## Steps

1. Mix all ingredients together.
2. Marinate your meat overnight in a tight container.
