> This is a wonderful alternative if you're wanting to leave out the turkey in the Thanksgiving meal and is delicious and beautiful!

Look for prepared "chicken"-flavored seitan in the refrigerated or frozen-food section of your health food store. Alternatively, the dry mix version works well, and leaves behind a delicious liquid with which to make brown gravy.

## Ingredients

### Stuffing

- 1½ cups chopped celery (with leaves)
- ¾ cup finely chopped onion
- ¾ cup finely chopped carrot
- ¾ cup margarine or butter (or if you're watching your fat intake, substitute ½ cup vegetable broth and just ¼ cup of butter)
- 9 cups soft bread cubes
- 1 tsp. ground sage
- 1 tsp. dried thyme leaves
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- Golden raisins (optional)

### "Turkey"

- 2 lb. "chicken"-flavored seitan, cut horizontally into 1-inch thick slices (or I've used gluten roast "dough"; pull off pieces and flatten, then brown in butter on both sides)
- 1 pkg. frozen puff pastry sheets, thawed
- 1 cup Rich Brown Gravy (below)
- Skim milk or soymilk, for brushing

### Rich Brown Gravy

- 2 tsp. margarine
- 4 Tbsp. finely chopped onion (or if you prefer, a pinch of onion powder)
- 4 rounded Tbsp. whole wheat pastry flour
- 4 cups seitan cooking liquid or water
- 4 Tbsp. sodium-reduced tamari or soy sauce (omit if using seitan cooking liquid)
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Steps

1. **Stuffing:** Cook and stir celery, onion (¾ cup), and carrot (¾ cup) in margarine (¾ cup) in Dutch oven until very soft; remove from heat. Stir in remaining stuffing ingredients. Yield: 5 cups stuffing.
2. **Gravy:** Melt margarine (2 tsp.) over medium heat in a heavy nonstick skillet. Add onion (4 Tbsp.); sauté, stirring, until onion becomes very soft, about 2 minutes. Transfer onion to a small bowl; wipe skillet clean with a paper towel or napkin.
3. Add flour (4 rounded Tbsp.) to skillet and cook, stirring constantly, over medium heat until flour browns and gives off a toasted fragrance, about 5 minutes.
4. Remove flour from heat; add seitan (2 lb.) liquid (4 cups), or water flavored with tamari (4 Tbsp.) or soy sauce. Return to heat; cook, whisking constantly, until gravy comes to a boil and thickens, about 5 minutes. Stir in reserved onion and pepper (¼ tsp.) to taste. If necessary, thin with a little more liquid if the gravy looks too thick; simmer 1 minute. Makes 4½ cups.
5. **Assemble:** Preheat oven to 400°. Line a baking sheet with heavy-duty foil; spray foil with nonstick cooking spray.
6. Brush seitan with gravy on both sides; arrange overlapping slices on foil in a row, mounding in center. Press stuffing mixture around seitan to cover completely. Wet hands lightly, and press into a neat oval.
7. Roll pastry sheets (1 pkg.) out to 1/16" thickness; arrange, overlapping slightly, to make a large rectangle. Seal edges with dabs of cold water.
8. Drape pastry over mound; trim excess away around bottom. Use scraps to cut out decorative shapes (leaves (1 tsp.), apples, etc.); attach with dabs of cold water. For a shiny crust, brush pastry lightly with skim milk or soymilk.
9. Bake 1 to 1¼ hours, covering pastry with foil if it browns too much.
10. Transfer "turkey" to a platter. To serve, cut straight down through pastry, stuffing and seitan to make neat ¾" slices. Spoon gravy over each serving.
