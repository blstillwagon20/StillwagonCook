## Ingredients

- ½ cup pineapple juice
- ½ cup vegetable oil
- ½ cup dark corn syrup
- 4 Tbsp. lime juice
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 3 tsp. brown sugar
- 3 tsp. prepared mustard
- 3 tsp. soy sauce
- 2 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. ground coriander
- ½ tsp. ground ginger

## Steps

1. Mix altogether.
2. Marinate overnight if you can. If using pork chops, use thick ones.
