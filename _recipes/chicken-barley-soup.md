> This is a recipe I got from my mother years ago and is very tasty and filling.

## Ingredients

- 1 chicken (skin and fat removed)
- 2 qts. water
- 1½ cups diced carrots
- 1 cup diced celery
- ½ cup barley
- ½ cup chopped onion
- 1 chicken bouillon cube
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 bay leaf
- ½ tsp. poultry seasoning
- ½ tsp. pepper
- ½ tsp. dried sage

## Steps

1. Cook chicken (1) in water (2 qts.) until tender.
2. Cool broth and skim off remaining fat.
3. Bone chicken and cut into bite-size pieces.
4. Return to broth with remaining ingredients. Simmer for at least 1 hour.
5. Remove bay leaf (1). Serve.
