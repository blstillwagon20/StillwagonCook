## Ingredients

- 2 (1 lb.) cans beets
- 1 to 2 dozen eggs
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1¼ cups sugar
- 1 cup vinegar

## Steps

1. Put eggs (1 to 2 dozen) in saucepan and cover with cold water. Bring to a boil and reduce heat so eggs boil slowly for 20 minutes.
2. Remove from heat, drain off hot water, add cold water and peel off shells.
3. Put in large container with lid (half gallon jar, large bowl which can be covered with a bowl cover or tight fitting plate.)
4. In another saucepan add beets (2 cans, 1 lb.), salt (½ tsp.), vinegar (1 cup) and sugar (1¼ cups) and bring to a rolling boil, stirring until sugar is dissolved.
5. Pour hot beet mixture over eggs and cool. Refrigerate.

At first the beet juice will seem too strong but after refrigeration for one day it will be fine.
