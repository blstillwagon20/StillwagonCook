## Ingredients

### 9-inch pie

- Pastry for one crust pie (I use Miracle Pie Crust)
- 2 eggs
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 can (16 oz.) pumpkin
- 1 can (12 oz.) evaporated milk
- 1 tsp. ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. ground ginger
- ¼ tsp. ground cloves

### 10-inch pie

- Pastry for one crust pie
- 3 eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- 2¾ cups canned pumpkin
- 2¼ cups evaporated milk
- 1½ tsp. ground cinnamon
- ¾ tsp. salt
- ¾ tsp. ground ginger
- ½ tsp. ground cloves

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 425°. Prepare pastry.
2. Beat eggs slightly with hand beater; beat in remaining ingredients.
3. Place pastry-lined pie plate on oven rack; pour in filling. Bake 15 minutes.
4. Reduce oven temperature to 350°. Bake until knife inserted in center comes out clean, 9-inch pie 45 minutes longer, 10-inch pie 55 minutes longer.
5. Refrigerate until chilled, at least 4 hours. Serve with Cool Whip. Refrigerate any remaining pie immediately.
