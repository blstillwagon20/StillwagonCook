## Ingredients

- 3 cups sugar
- ½ cup Crisco
- 2 sticks margarine
- 5 large eggs
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 3 cups flour
- 1 small can milk (add water to make 1 cup)*
- 2 Tbsp. of vanilla butter nut flavoring

## Steps

1. Cream Crisco (½ cup), oleo, and sugar (3 cups), eggs (5 large) and salt (¼ tsp.).
2. Add flour (3 cups) and milk (1 small can) alternating and ending with milk.
3. Bake in 10 inch bundt or tube pan. **Start in a cold oven.** Bake at 300° for 1 hour and 45 minutes. **Do not open door while baking.**
4. Remove from pan immediately. Loosen around edge first.

*If using large can of milk, use 2 Tbsp. plus ¼ cup of water to make the 1 cup plus milk.
