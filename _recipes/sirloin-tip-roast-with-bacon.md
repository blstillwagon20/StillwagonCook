> I got this recipe a few years ago from a friend I was attending beauty school with and it's definitely a winner! I like to serve this with parsley potatoes, dinner rolls, and a nice salad.

## Ingredients

- 2½ to 3 lb. boned and tied sirloin tip roast
- 3 small cloves garlic, slivered
- 2 Tbsp. Dijon style mustard
- ½ tsp. dried thyme leaves
- 6 slices bacon

## Steps

1. Cut slits in the roast (2½ to 3 lb.) and insert slivered garlic (3 small cloves), placing pieces evenly around the whole roast.
2. Rub with 1 Tbsp. Dijon mustard. Sprinkle with thyme.
3. Place roast on roasting rack; top with the bacon (6 slices), with ends covering sides.
4. Insert meat thermometer into the thickest portion of meat. Roast at 325° until meat thermometer registers 145° for rare or 160° for medium to well done.
5. Transfer meat to serving board or platter. Remove bacon and strings.
6. Cut bacon into small bits and put in small skillet to cook until done well and nice and chewy to crispy. Drain off excess fat.
7. Pour pan drippings from the roast into small skillet with bacon. Whisk in remaining 1 Tbsp. Dijon mustard and heat to boiling. Continue boiling until drippings are shiny and thickened.
8. Serve sauce with roast.
