> This is the best Boston cream pie I've ever had and have been complimented on it many times. I hope you like it, too.

## Ingredients

- 1 yellow cake mix
- 1 batch Homemade Vanilla Pudding (see that recipe)

### Glaze

- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- 3 Tbsp. light corn syrup
- 2 Tbsp. water
- 4–6 oz. semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Steps

1. Prepare one yellow cake mix (1) according to directions in two round pans. Cool 15 minutes and remove from pans to cool completely.
2. In the meantime, prepare the Homemade Vanilla Pudding (1) recipe. Allow to cool completely. Use the above for the filling.
3. **Glaze:** In a saucepan, bring sugar (¼ cup), corn syrup (3 Tbsp.), and water (2 Tbsp.) to a boil over low heat, stirring constantly, until sugar has dissolved. Remove from heat. Add chocolate; let stand for 1 minute. Whisk until smooth. Allow to cool some until the mixture has thickened so that it won't all run completely off the cake.
4. **Assemble:** Place one round cake on serving tray. Spread the filling mixture (you'll probably have enough for an extra bowlful for someone) on top of the first cake; place second round cake on top of the filling.
5. Gradually pour glaze over cake, allowing it to drip down sides. Let stand until glaze sets. Keep refrigerated.
