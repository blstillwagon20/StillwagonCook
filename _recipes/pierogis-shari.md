> This recipe came from my mother-in-law. It's great!

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 2¼ cups flour
- 4 oz. cream cheese
- 1 egg
- Water

### Filling

- 6 medium potatoes
- 10–12 oz. cheddar cheese, grated
- Garlic powder and salt

### To serve

- Onion and butter for sautéing
- Garlic salt

## Steps

1. **Dough:** Mix flour (2¼ cups), cream cheese (4 oz.) and egg (1). Add enough water to make a nice dough.
2. **Filling:** Peel, dice and boil potatoes (6 medium). Drain. Mix potatoes and cheese (a little at a time); add some garlic powder and salt. Cool.
3. Flour board and dough. Roll out and cut with a glass.
4. Fill, dab edge with water, fold and seal with a fork.
5. Boil a pot of water, drop in pierogi's and boil 4 to 5 minutes.
6. Sauté onion in butter, add pierogi's, and some garlic salt. Stir pierogi's around. Serve immediately.
