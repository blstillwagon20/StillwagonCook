## Ingredients

- 2 cups raw gluten
- ½ cup oil
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 1 tsp. salt
- ½ cup walnuts, peanuts, almonds, or peanut butter
- ½ tsp. garlic powder
- ⅛ tsp. black pepper
- 1 pkg. dry Lipton onion soup
- ⅓ cup nutritional yeast flakes
- Equal parts oil, soy sauce and water, for covering

## Steps

1. Mix all ingredients and put in an oiled loaf pan.
2. Cover with equal parts oil (½ cup), soy sauce (¼ cup), and water.
3. Bake at 350° for 1 to 1½ hours.

**Variation:** Instead of using a loaf pan, use a Dutch oven or roaster following the above recipe. Place sliced potatoes and carrots around gluten and top with sliced onion. Cover with equal portions oil, soy sauce, and water. Bake at 350° uncovered for the first ½ hour, covered for ½ hour, and uncovered the last ½ hour. Baste throughout with the oil, soy sauce and water mixture.
