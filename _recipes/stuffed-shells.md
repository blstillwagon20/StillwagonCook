> This is a recipe my sister, Sandy, gave me and is the best I've had. I make it for a special occasion or for cook-outs or picnics since it makes a lot.

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 1½ lb. ricotta cheese
- 8 oz. shredded mozzarella cheese
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 egg, slightly beaten
- ⅛ tsp. pepper
- ½ tsp. oregano
- 1 Tbsp. fresh basil, chopped, or ½ tsp. dried
- ¼ tsp. salt

### To assemble

- Large stuffing shells
- Spaghetti sauce
- Parmesan cheese for sprinkling

## Steps

1. Mix together cheeses and egg (1). Add remaining filling ingredients.
2. Cook large stuffing shells according to directions on package.
3. Stuff each shell with 1 or 2 Tbsp. of cheese filling.
4. Place in baking dish that has a thin layer of spaghetti sauce covering the bottom.
5. Cover shells with more spaghetti sauce and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese (¼ cup).
6. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes in 350° oven or until hot and bubbly.
