> This is my mom's butter cookie recipe. All us kids used to decorate these cookies for Christmas every year.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup soft butter
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 3 tsp. vanilla
- 3 cups flour
- ½ tsp. baking powder
- Colored sugars and decorations

## Steps

1. Mix together butter (1 cup), sugar (½ cup) and egg (1) thoroughly. Stir in vanilla (3 tsp.).
2. Sift together flour (3 cups) and baking powder (½ tsp.) and stir in.
3. Chill dough. Roll very thin. Cut into desired shapes.
4. Place on ungreased baking sheet. Decorate with colored sugars, etc.
5. Bake until delicately brown in a 425° oven, approximately 5 to 7 minutes.
