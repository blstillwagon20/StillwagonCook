## Ingredients

- 3 to 3½ lb. cut up broiler-fryer chicken
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 1 Tbsp. chopped fresh or 1 tsp. dried basil leaves
- 1½ tsp. chopped fresh or ½ tsp. dried oregano leaves
- 1½ tsp. paprika
- ¾ tsp. chopped fresh or ¼ tsp. dried marjoram leaves
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 2 Tbsp. vegetable oil
- 1 cup chicken broth or dry white wine
- 12 small pitted ripe olives
- 8 medium carrots, cut into fourths
- 8 whole small onions
- 4 medium baking potatoes, cut into fourths
- 1 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1 Tbsp. cold water

## Steps

1. Remove skin from chicken (3 to 3½ lb.) pieces. Mix flour (¼ cup), basil, oregano, paprika (1½ tsp.), marjoram, and pepper (¼ tsp.). Coat chicken with flour mixture.
2. Heat oil (2 Tbsp.) in nonstick Dutch oven or 12-inch skillet until hot. Cook chicken in oil about 15 minutes or until light brown on all sides; remove chicken and drain.
3. Stir in broth (1 cup), olives (12 small), carrots (8 medium), onions (8), and potatoes (4 medium); add chicken. Heat to boiling; reduce heat.
4. Cover and simmer 35 to 40 minutes or until juices of chicken run clear.
5. Remove chicken and vegetables; keep warm.
6. Mix cornstarch (1 Tbsp.) and cold water (1 Tbsp.); stir into liquid in Dutch oven. Heat to boiling, stirring constantly. Boil and stir 1 minute.
7. Serve with chicken and vegetables.
