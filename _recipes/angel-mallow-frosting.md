## Ingredients

- ½ cup sugar
- 2 egg whites
- 2 Tbsp. water
- 2 cups (7 oz. jar) marshmallow creme
- ½ tsp. vanilla
- Red food coloring

## Steps

1. Combine sugar (½ cup), egg whites (2) and water (2 Tbsp.) in double boiler. Beat over boiling water until soft peaks form.
2. Add marshmallow creme (2 cups, 7 oz. jar). Beat to stiff peaks.
3. Remove from heat, beat in vanilla (½ tsp.). Tint with food coloring.
