## Ingredients

- 2¼ cups flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 cup butter
- ¼ cup sugar
- ¾ cup packed brown sugar
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 pkg. vanilla instant pudding
- 2 eggs
- 1 (12 oz.) pkg. M&M's

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 375°. Cream butter (1 cup), sugars (¼ cup), vanilla (1 tsp.) and pudding (1 pkg.).
2. Mix in eggs (2) and baking soda (1 tsp.). Stir in flour (2¼ cups). Stir in M&M's (1 pkg., 12 oz.).
3. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes on greased cookie sheets. (The batter doesn't taste the greatest before you bake them, but they do turn out nice and yummy. It must be something with the pudding.)
