## Ingredients

- 2 (1 lb.) cans cream style corn
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup cracker or bread crumbs
- ½ cup finely chopped onion
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. pepper

## Steps

1. Mix ingredients together and pour corn (2 cans, 1 lb.) mixture into 2 quart baking dish.
2. Bake 55 minutes. (No oven temperature is given in the original; 350° is typical.)
