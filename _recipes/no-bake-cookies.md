> I make these quick chocolatey no bake cookies occasionally when I'm having a craving for sweets and they hit the spot.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- ½ cup cocoa
- ½ cup milk
- ½ cup butter
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 3 cups rolled quick oats

## Steps

1. Mix and boil sugar (2 cups), cocoa (½ cup), milk (½ cup), butter (½ cup), and salt (¼ tsp.) for 3 to 5 minutes.
2. Add vanilla (1 tsp.) and rolled quick oats (3 cups).
3. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto wax paper and allow to cool.
