## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup sugar
- ¼ cup flour
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- ½ tsp. lemon juice
- ¼ tsp. nutmeg
- 5 cups blackberries
- 1 Tbsp. butter
- Unbaked pie shell and top crust

## Steps

1. Combine sugar (⅔ cup), flour (¼ cup), cinnamon (½ tsp.), lemon juice (½ tsp.), and nutmeg (¼ tsp.). Mix with blackberries (5 cups). Let stand.
2. Spoon into unbaked pie shell. Dot with butter (1 Tbsp.). Cover with top crust.
3. Bake at 425° for 50 minutes. Watch the edges of the crust and cover with foil when browned.
