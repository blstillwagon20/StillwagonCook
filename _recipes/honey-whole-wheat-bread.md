## Ingredients

- 3 cups whole wheat flour
- ⅓ cup honey
- ¼ cup shortening
- 1 Tbsp. salt
- 2 pkgs. regular or quick-acting active dry yeast
- 1¼ cups scalded milk (allow to cool to a very warm temp.)
- 1 cup very warm water (120° to 130°)
- 3 to 4 cups all-purpose or unbleached flour
- Margarine, whole wheat flour or crushed rolled oats, for the tops
- Butter, for brushing

## Steps

1. Mix whole wheat flour (3 cups), honey (⅓ cup), shortening (¼ cup), salt (1 Tbsp.) and yeast in 4-quart bowl; add warm milk (1¼ cups) and water (1 cup). Beat on low speed, scraping bowl frequently, 1 minute. Beat on medium speed, scraping bowl frequently, 1 minute.
2. Stir in enough all-purpose (3 to 4 cups) flour, 1 cup at a time, to make dough easy to handle. (Don't put too much flour in or your bread will be dry. Just put enough in so it's no longer too sticky and can be handled.)
3. Turn dough onto lightly floured surface; knead until smooth and elastic, about 10 minutes. Place in greased 2½-quart bowl; turn greased side up. Cover and let rise in warm place until double, 40 to 60 minutes. (Dough is ready if indentation remains when touched.)
4. Punch down dough; divide into halves. Let rest 5 minutes.
5. Flatten each half with hands or rolling pin into rectangle, 18" x 9", on lightly floured surface. (If dough shrinks, gently stretch into rectangle.) Fold 9-inch sides crosswise into thirds, overlapping sides.
6. Roll up tightly toward you, beginning at open end. Pinch edge of dough into roll to seal well. Press each end with side of hand to seal; fold ends under loaf.
7. Place loaves, seam sides down, in 2 greased loaf pans. Brush loaves lightly with margarine and sprinkle with whole wheat flour or crushed rolled oats if desired. Let rise until double, 35 to 50 minutes, covered.
8. Heat oven to 350°. Place loaves on low rack so that tops of pans are in center of oven. Bake until loaves are deep golden brown and hollow when tapped, 40 to 45 minutes.
9. Remove from pans; brush tops with butter and cool on wire rack.
