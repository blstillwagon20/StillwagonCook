> This is the best corn bread I've ever had or made. It's always nice and moist and sweet and just delicious!

## Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup yellow cornmeal
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 Tbsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. salt
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup whipping cream or milk
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
- ¼ cup honey

## Steps

1. In a bowl, combine flour (1 cup), cornmeal (1 cup), sugar (¼ cup), baking powder (1 Tbsp.) and salt (½ tsp.).
2. In a small mixing bowl, beat the eggs (2). Add cream (1 cup), oil (¼ cup) and honey (¼ cup); beat well.
3. Stir into the dry ingredients just until moistened.
4. Pour into a greased 9-inch square baking pan. Bake at 400° for 20 to 25 minutes or until a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean.

These also do well as muffins. Just put into muffin tins that have been coated with a cooking spray, bake until done, then allow to cool outside of tins.
