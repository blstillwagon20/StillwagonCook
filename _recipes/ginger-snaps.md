> Absolutely wonderful! The kids love them right out of the oven.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup shortening
- 1 cup sugar
- ¼ cup molasses
- 1 egg
- 2 cups flour
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 2 tsp. soda
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. cloves
- 1 tsp. ginger
- Sugar, for dipping

## Steps

1. Cream shortening (¾ cup) and sugar (1 cup). Add molasses (¼ cup) and egg (1) and mix well.
2. Sift flour (2 cups), salt (¼ tsp.), soda (2 tsp.), and spices and add to mixture. Chill dough.
3. Roll into small balls (about ¾ inch). Dip in sugar.
4. Bake 10 to 12 minutes at 350°.
