## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 2 eggs
- ¼ cup milk
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 3½ cup flour
- 2 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 – 10 oz. pkg. Hershey kisses or hugs

## Steps

1. Cream sugars (1 cup), butter (1 cup) and peanut butter (1 cup). Beat in eggs (2), milk (¼ cup) and vanilla (2 tsp.).
2. Sift together flour (3½ cup), soda (2 tsp.), and salt (1 tsp.). Stir into egg mixture. Shape into balls; roll in sugar. Place on ungreased cookie sheet.
3. Bake 375° for 10 to 12 minutes. Immediately press a candy into each. Cool slightly; remove from cookie sheet onto wire rack. Cool completely. Makes 7 dozen.
