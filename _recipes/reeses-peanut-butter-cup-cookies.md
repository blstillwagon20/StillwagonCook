## Ingredients

- ¾ cup peanut butter
- ½ cup shortening
- ⅓ cup sugar
- ⅓ cup packed lt. brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 2 Tbsp. milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 1⅓ cup flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- Sugar
- About 30 Reese's Peanut Butter Cup Miniatures

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 350°. In large mixer bowl cream peanut butter (¾ cup), shortening (½ cup), ⅓ cup sugar and brown sugar. Add egg (1), milk (2 Tbsp.) and vanilla; beat well.
2. Combine flour (1⅓ cup) and baking soda (1 tsp.); gradually add to creamed mixture, blending thoroughly. Shape dough into 1½ inch balls; roll in sugar. Place on ungreased cookie sheet; bake 10 to 12 minutes.
3. Remove from oven; immediately place unwrapped peanut butter cup on top of each cookie, pressing down so that cookie cracks around edges. Cool slightly; remove from cookie sheet onto wire rack. Cool completely. Makes about 2½ dozen cookies.
