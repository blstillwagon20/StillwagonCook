## Ingredients

- 1 pkg. imitation crabmeat
- 1 pkg. soft cream cheese
- 1 jar cocktail sauce
- Tostitos

## Steps

1. Spread cream cheese (1 pkg.) on tray.
2. Spread cocktail sauce (1 jar) and then top with crab meat.
3. Dip Tostitos into this. This is easy and delicious!
