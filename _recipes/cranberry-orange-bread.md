> This is a very pretty and yummy quick bread I hope you try.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1½ tsp. baking powder
- ¾ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. baking soda
- ¼ cup margarine or butter, softened
- 1 Tbsp. grated orange peel
- ¾ cup orange juice
- 1 egg
- 1 cup fresh or frozen cranberries, chopped
- ½ cup chopped nuts

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 350°. Grease bottom only of loaf pan.
2. Mix flour (2 cups), sugar (¾ cup), baking powder (1½ tsp.), salt (¾ tsp.) and baking soda (½ tsp.); stir in margarine (¼ cup) until mixture is crumbly.
3. Stir in orange peel (1 Tbsp.), orange juice (¾ cup), and egg (1) just until moistened; stir in cranberries and nuts (½ cup). Spread in pan.
4. Bake until wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean: 8-inch loaf 1¼ hours, 9-inch loaf 55 to 65 minutes.
5. Cool 5 minutes. Loosen sides of loaf from pan; remove from pan. Cool completely before slicing.
