> I got this recipe from a good friend of mine who cooks the best rice dishes. This is very good and easy!

## Ingredients

- 4 pork chops (pork tenderloin is good also)
- 2 cans (10¾ oz. each) condensed cream of mushroom soup
- 1½ cans water
- 1⅓ cups white rice
- 1 can (10¾ oz.) Campbell's double strength beef broth
- 1 can (10¾ oz.) Campbell's French Onion soup

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 375°. Brown pork chops (4) and place in baking dish in a single layer.
2. Add cream of mushroom soup (2 cans, 10¾ oz. each) and ½ to 1 can of water to pork drippings. Cook until smooth and pour over pork and cover.
3. Cook pork chops for 1½ hours.
4. After pork has cooked for ½ hour, prepare rice (1⅓ cups): pour rice into round casserole dish. Add beef broth (1 can, 10¾ oz.), French Onion soup (1 can, 10¾ oz.) and ½ can of water.
5. Cover and cook for 45 minutes to 1 hour.
