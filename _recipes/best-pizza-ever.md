> This is a recipe I found in my husband's Vegetarian Dishes cookbook. He says it's the best pizza he's ever had and I agree with him. If you don't like to make your own dough, at least use this sauce recipe. It's the best ever!

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 3 tsp. fresh yeast or 1 pkg. dry rapid rise yeast
- ½ tsp. sugar
- ½ cup lukewarm water
- 1½ cups flour
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 2 Tbsp. olive oil

### Pizza sauce

- 1 tsp. olive oil
- ¼ tsp. garlic powder
- 1 (14½ oz.) can stewed tomatoes with onion, celery, and green peppers
- 1 Tbsp. tomato paste
- ½ tsp. dried oregano
- ½ tsp. dried basil
- 1 tsp. sugar
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. black pepper

### Topping

- Mozzarella and parmesan cheese
- Your favorite toppings
- A little cheddar (optional)

## Steps

1. Cream the yeast (3 tsp.) with the sugar in a bowl, add lukewarm water (½ cup), and let it stand for 10 minutes or until bubbles appear on the surface. Sift the flour (1½ cups) and salt into a bowl, make a well in the center, and add the oil and yeast mixture. Mix to a firm dough. (Make dough only firm enough so that it's no longer sticky to handle. If it's too firm, the dough will be tough and dry. Just add a touch more water if need be.)
2. Turn the dough onto a floured surface and knead it for 10 minutes or until the dough is smooth and elastic. Place it in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and leave in a warm place for 30 minutes or until the dough has doubled in size.
3. Knock the dough down with your fist and knead into a small ball. Flatten out the dough with a rolling pin and roll it into a circular sheet that will just fit a 10 inch pizza pan. Place the dough carefully in the pan.
4. Meanwhile make your sauce: heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over moderate heat. When hot, add the garlic powder (¼ tsp.) and sauté momentarily. Add the undrained canned stewed tomatoes (which have been pureed), tomato paste (1 Tbsp.), oregano (½ tsp.), basil (½ tsp.), sugar, salt, and pepper (¼ tsp.). Bring the sauce to a boil; then reduce the heat and, stirring occasionally, simmer uncovered 10 to 15 minutes or until the sauce is thick and smooth. Allow the filling to cool somewhat.
5. Spread the cooled filling over the pizza dough, leaving a little border uncovered. Sprinkle with mozzarella cheese and a little parmesan cheese. Use your favorite toppings and end with a little more mozzarella and parmesan cheeses. A little cheddar can be used also.
6. Bake in preheated 425°F hot oven for 15 to 20 minutes.
