> The best caramel corn we've ever eaten. I got this recipe from a Mennonite and Amish cookbook.

## Ingredients

- 6 qt. popped popcorn
- 2 cups brown sugar
- ½ cup light corn syrup
- 1 cup butter (2 sticks)
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. cream of tartar
- 1 tsp. baking soda

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 200°. Divide the popcorn (6 qt.) between 2 large bowls. Oil 2 lipped cookie sheets very well and set aside.
2. In a deep sauce pan, combine the sugar, corn syrup (½ cup), butter (1 cup), salt (1 tsp.), and cream of tartar (¼ tsp.). Insert a candy thermometer. Bring to boil and cook the mixture over medium heat for about 5 minutes stirring occasionally, until the temperature registers 260°.
3. Remove from heat and quickly stir in soda (1 tsp.). The mixture foams up. Pour the syrup mixture over the popcorn, half into each bowl. Work quickly to coat all the popcorn with the caramel.
4. Spread popcorn evenly on the cookie sheets and bake for 1 hour stirring 3 times. Remove from the oven and spread popcorn on wax paper to cool. Store in airtight container.
