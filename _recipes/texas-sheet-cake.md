## Ingredients

### Sift

- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- ¼ tsp. salt

### Melt to boil

- 2 sticks of butter
- ¼ cup cocoa
- 1 cup water

### Beat

- 2 eggs
- ½ cup buttermilk
- 1 tsp. vanilla

### Icing

- 1 stick butter
- ¼ cup cocoa
- 6 Tbsp. buttermilk
- 1 box (1 lb.) powdered sugar

## Steps

1. Sift the dry ingredients. Melt butter, cocoa and water (1 cup) to a boil. Beat eggs (2), buttermilk and vanilla (1 tsp.).
2. Add liquid to flour (2 cups) and sugar (2 cups) mixture. Mix well. Add the chocolate mixture; mix well again.
3. Grease a jelly roll pan well. Bake at 350° for 15 to 20 minutes.
4. **Icing:** Mix together. Let cake cool, then frost.
