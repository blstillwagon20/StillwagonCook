## Ingredients

### Combine

- ½ cup oatmeal
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- 2½ tsp. baking powder
- ¼ tsp. baking soda
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ¾ cup flour

### Blend together

- ½ cup milk
- 2 Tbsp. oil
- 2 eggs

## Steps

1. Combine the dry ingredients.
2. Blend together milk (½ cup), oil (2 Tbsp.) and eggs (2) and add to the above ingredients.
3. Mix only enough to blend. Put into 6 greased or lined muffin tins.
4. Bake at 400° for 15 minutes.
