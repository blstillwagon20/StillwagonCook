## Ingredients

- ½ cup packed brown sugar
- 1 pkg. (3⅝ oz.) butterscotch regular (not instant) pudding
- ¼ cup butter, melted
- 1 Tbsp. milk
- ½ tsp. ground cinnamon
- 1 pkg. active dry yeast
- ¾ cup warm water (105° to 115°)
- 2¾ cups Bisquick baking mix

## Steps

1. Mix brown sugar (½ cup), pudding (dry), melted butter (¼ cup), milk (1 Tbsp.) and cinnamon (½ tsp.).
2. Dissolve yeast (1 pkg.) in warm water (¾ cup); stir in baking mix (2¾ cups) until soft dough forms.
3. Turn dough onto cloth-covered board dusted with Bisquick. Knead until smooth, about 20 times. Sprinkle with additional Bisquick if dough is too sticky.
4. Roll into rectangle 15" x 9". Spread ¾ of the butterscotch mixture over dough. Roll up tightly beginning at 15" side; cut into 9 slices.
5. Pour remaining butterscotch mixture into square pan, 9" x 9" x 2", and place slices in pan. Cover and refrigerate up to 24 hours.
6. To bake immediately, cover and let rise in warm place until double, 25 to 30 minutes.
7. Heat oven to 375°. Bake until golden brown, 25 to 30 minutes.
8. Immediately invert pan on heatproof serving plate. Let pan remain a few minutes so butterscotch drizzles over rolls.
