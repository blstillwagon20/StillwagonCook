## Ingredients

- 1 cup diced fully cooked ham
- ¾ cup shredded Swiss cheese
- 5 bacon strips, cooked and crumbled
- ¾ cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 3 Tbsp. chopped onion
- 3 Tbsp. green pepper, chopped (optional)
- 1 cup milk
- ¼ cup biscuit mix
- 2 eggs
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ⅛ tsp. pepper

## Steps

1. In a greased 10-inch quiche dish or pie plate, layer ham (1 cup), Swiss cheese (¾ cup), bacon, cheddar cheese (¾ cup), onion (3 Tbsp.) and green pepper (3 Tbsp.).
2. Place the remaining ingredients in a blender in the order given. Blend for 30 to 40 seconds.
3. Pour over meat, cheese, and vegetables; do not stir.
4. Bake uncovered at 350° for 30 to 35 minutes or until set and lightly browned.
5. Let stand 5 minutes before cutting.
