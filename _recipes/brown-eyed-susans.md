> My mom always made these big sugar cookies and they're just delicious. They're the best if you cook them on the airbake cookie sheets and don't overbake or they'll get hard.

## Ingredients

- 5 cups flour
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 1½ cups margarine or butter
- 2 cups sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 (6 oz.) pkg. semi-sweet chocolate bits
- Yellow or white sugar

## Steps

1. Cream butter with sugar (2 cups) until light. Beat in eggs (3), 1 at a time. Stir in vanilla (1 tsp.).
2. Add flour (5 cups), baking powder (1 tsp.) and salt (¼ tsp.) slowly.
3. Roll 3 tablespoons at a time into balls (makes a large cookie). Place 5 inches apart on greased cookie sheet.
4. Use a jar, greasing bottom with shortening, then dipping in sugar to flatten cookie balls to ¼ inch thick.
5. Press 3 chocolate bits in center. Sprinkle with sugar. Bake at 400° for 8 minutes.
