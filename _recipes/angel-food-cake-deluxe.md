> I found this recipe in an old Betty Crocker cookbook and found it to be just wonderful. You can make it in the regular Angel food tube pan or into lined cupcake tins.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Softasilk flour (or 1 cup all-purpose flour which has 1 Tbsp. of cornstarch in it to replace 1 Tbsp. of the flour)
- 1½ cups sifted confectioner sugar
- 1½ cups egg whites (12)
- 1½ tsp. cream of tartar
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 1½ tsp. vanilla
- 1 cup granulated sugar

## Steps

1. Set out but do not grease a 10" tube pan, 4" deep, or lined cupcake tin.
2. Measure and sift together the flour (1 cup) and confectioner sugar (1½ cups) 3 times.
3. Measure into large mixing bowl the egg whites (1½ cups), cream of tartar (1½ tsp.), salt (¼ tsp.) and vanilla (1½ tsp.). Beat on highest speed of mixer until foamy, then gradually add the granulated sugar (1 cup), 2 Tbsp. at a time.
4. Continue beating until meringue holds stiff peaks. Sift the flour-sugar mixture gradually over the meringue.
5. Fold in gently just until the flour-sugar mixture disappears. Push batter into ungreased tube center pan or lined cupcake tins (you can decorate them as cupcakes with sprinkles, cinnamon sugar, or whatever you like if you do them as cupcakes). Gently cut through batter with a knife to help remove air bubbles.
6. Bake at 375° for 30 to 35 minutes (for tube pan) or until cake springs back when touched. When cake tests done, invert. For cupcakes, allow to cool slightly in pan. Carefully remove them to cool on another surface (do this carefully or you will cause them to deflate). Let cake hang until cold.
