## Ingredients

### Sweet roll dough

- 2 pkgs. regular or quick-acting active dry yeast
- 1 cup warm water (105° to 115°)
- 1 cup lukewarm milk (scalded then cooled)
- ⅔ cup sugar
- ⅔ cup melted shortening or margarine or butter
- 2 tsp. salt
- 2 eggs
- 7–8 cups all-purpose flour

### Filling

- ⅓ cup softened margarine or butter
- ¾ cup sugar
- 6 tsp. ground cinnamon

### Glaze

- 3 cups powdered sugar
- 3 Tbsp. milk
- 1½ tsp. vanilla

## Steps

1. Dissolve yeast in warm water (1 cup) in large bowl. Stir in milk, sugar, shortening (⅔ cup), salt (2 tsp.), eggs (2) and 4 cups of the flour. Beat until smooth.
2. Stir in enough remaining flour to make dough easy to handle. Turn onto lightly floured surface; knead until smooth and elastic, about 5 minutes.
3. Place in greased large bowl; turn greased side up. Cover; let rise in warm place until double, about 1½ hours. (Dough is ready if indentation remains when touched.) Punch down dough.
4. Flatten dough with hands or rolling pin into a rectangle 15 inches long (dough should be about ¼" thick) on lightly floured surface; spread with softened margarine (⅓ cup) or butter.
5. Mix sugar and cinnamon (6 tsp.); sprinkle all of this over rectangle. Roll up tightly, beginning at 15-inch side. Pinch edge of dough into roll to seal well. Stretch roll to make even.
6. Cut into 1½" slices. (To cut even slices, place a piece of dental floss or heavy thread under roll. Bring ends of floss up and crisscross at top of roll. Pull strings in opposite directions.)
7. Place about ¾" apart on greased pan. Let rise until double, about 40 minutes.
8. Heat oven to 375°. Bake until golden brown, 20 to 30 minutes.
9. **Glaze:** Mix powdered sugar (3 cups), milk (3 Tbsp.) and vanilla (1½ tsp.) until smooth and of desired consistency. Spread rolls with glaze while warm.

**Do-ahead tip:** After kneading, cover dough and refrigerate in greased bowl no longer than 3 days.
