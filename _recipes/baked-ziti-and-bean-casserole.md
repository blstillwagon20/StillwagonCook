## Ingredients

- 1 can (28 oz.) whole tomatoes, drained
- 1 cup nonfat ricotta cheese
- ¼ cup chopped red onion
- 1 Tbsp. chopped fresh parsley
- 1 Tbsp. chopped fresh or 1 tsp. dried thyme leaves
- ½ tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. crushed red pepper (optional)
- 4 cups hot cooked ziti or penne pasta
- 1 can (15 oz.) great northern beans, rinsed and drained
- 3 slices part-skim mozzarella cheese, about 6½" x 4"
- Grated parmesan cheese, if desired

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 400°. Spray rectangular baking dish, 11" x 7" x 1½", with nonstick cooking spray.
2. Break up tomatoes (1 can, 28 oz.) in large bowl. Stir in ricotta cheese (1 cup), onion (¼ cup), parsley (1 Tbsp.), thyme, salt (½ tsp.) and red pepper (¼ tsp.).
3. Carefully fold in pasta and beans (1 can, 15 oz.).
4. Pour pasta mixture into baking dish, spreading evenly. Arrange mozzarella cheese (3 slices) on top.
5. Bake uncovered about 30 minutes or until mixture is hot and cheese is golden brown. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese.
