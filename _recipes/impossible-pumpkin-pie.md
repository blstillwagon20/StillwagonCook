> This is a recipe from my Grandmother. It's very easy and delicious and it makes its own crust!

## Ingredients

- 1½ cup pumpkin
- 1½ cups milk
- 4 eggs, slightly beaten
- ⅔ cup sugar
- ½ cup flour
- ⅓ cup butter, softened
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- ½ tsp. ginger
- A little cinnamon, for the top

## Steps

1. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl and mix well.
2. Put in well greased and floured pie pan. Sprinkle a little cinnamon (1 tsp.) over the top to make it pretty.
3. Bake at 350° for 45 minutes to 1 hour. Very good!
