> This is a very easy dish, and very low-fat.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups water
- 1 cup rice
- 4 Tbsp. oleo
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 green pepper, chopped
- 1 can diced tomatoes (chopped)
- ⅓ cup picante sauce

## Steps

1. Boil water (2 cups) and add rice (1 cup); cover and simmer 20 minutes.
2. Sauté garlic (1 clove), onion (1) and green pepper (1) in oleo (4 Tbsp.) until tender.
3. Add tomatoes (1 can) and picante sauce (⅓ cup). Simmer with the garlic and onion mixture.
4. Add rice and stir.
