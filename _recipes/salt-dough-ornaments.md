> Use your favorite cookie cutter shapes to create these antique-looking ornaments. Display them singly on a tree or as package ornaments, or string several to make a garland.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup salt
- 2 cups whole wheat flour
- 1 cup water
- 2–3 drops anise oil (available at health food or drug stores)

Other materials include: a rolling pin; jar of allspice; selection of cookie cutters; a cookie sheet; a selection of whole ground cloves or cinnamon; whole cloves; whole anise stars or peppercorns; string or strips of homespun fabric for hanging ornaments (if desired); and clear craft spray. If you plan to fashion some of the cookies into garlands, you will also need florist wire; wire snips; and a selection of natural items such as sugar gum tree balls, buckeyes or unshelled nuts and dried cranberries.

## Steps

1. Mix salt (1 cup) and flour (2 cups) together. Add water (1 cup) and anise oil (2–3 drops); mix well. Knead mixture for seven to 10 minutes on a bread board or counter surface.
2. Using a rolling pin, roll the salt dough to ⅜-inch thickness. Sprinkle dough with allspice, ground cloves or cinnamon and gently rub the spice into the dough to give it an uneven brown surface.
3. Use the cookie cutters to cut out shapes and carefully transfer cookies onto a cookie sheet. Decorate cookies as desired with whole cloves, anise stars or whole peppercorns.
4. If you plan to hang the cookies on a tree or fashion several into a garland, punch holes with a soda straw before baking them. The holes will not shrink during baking so do not make them larger than needed.
5. Bake cookies for about 8 to 12 minutes in a 300° oven or allow to air dry for two or three days. Bake denser dough ornaments longer.
6. Spray your dough ornaments with clear acrylic craft spray; this will make them last longer. Set the cookies on a piece of newspaper or brown paper. Spray one side and edges first and allow to dry before turning over to spray the other side.
7. When dry, hang ornaments with strips of home-spun fabric or fashion into garlands as desired.

**Decoration only — not to be eaten.**
