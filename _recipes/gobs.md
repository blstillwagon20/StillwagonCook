## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- ½ cup shortening
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup sour milk (add 1 Tbsp. vinegar to regular milk for this and let stand a few minutes)
- ½ cup cocoa
- ¾ cup boiling water
- 4 cups flour
- 2 tsp. soda
- ½ tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- Red food coloring

### Gob icing

- 5 Tbsp. flour
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup Crisco
- Vanilla

## Steps

1. Cream sugar (2 cups) and shortening (½ cup). Add eggs (2) and sour milk (1 cup).
2. Mix cocoa (½ cup) and boiling water (¾ cup); add to it.
3. Add flour, soda (2 tsp.) and baking powder (½ tsp.). Stir in vanilla (1 tsp.) and enough red food coloring to make it a nice rich color.
4. Bake at 400° for 5 to 7 minutes on greased sheet. Allow to cool.
5. In the meantime, make your icing. You will probably want to start the icing even before you make the cookies, since the first part of the recipe is cooked on the stove and must cool at room temp. before you continue the recipe.
6. **Icing:** Cook flour (5 Tbsp.) and milk (1 cup) until thick. (You must whisk this the entire time it cooks to prevent lumps from forming. If lumps develop, throw it out and start over. It will ruin your icing.) Allow to cool at room temp. I usually cover this with plastic wrap once some of the heat is off it so the top doesn't harden.
7. Once cool, beat in sugar, Crisco (1 cup), and some vanilla. Spread between two cookies.
