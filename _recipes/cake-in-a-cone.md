> These are great for the kids and they can help make them, too!

## Ingredients

- 1 carrot-cake mix
- 1 cup solid-pack pumpkin
- 3 eggs
- ⅓ cup vegetable oil
- ¼ cup water
- 2 Tbsp. brown sugar
- 1½ tsp. ground cinnamon
- 18 ice cream wafer cones

### Frosting

- ⅔ cup confectioners' sugar
- ¼ cup solid-pack pumpkin
- ½ tsp. vanilla extract
- 8 oz. pkg. cream cheese

## Steps

1. Beat all the above ingredients except ice cream wafer cones (18) together with electric mixer on low, until cake is wet. Increase speed to medium and beat 2 minutes.
2. Spoon batter evenly among 18 ice cream wafer cones, filling each cone ¾ full. Bake at 350° for 25 minutes, until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Remove cones to wire rack; cool completely.
3. **Frosting:** Beat all ingredients with electric mixer on low, about 3 minutes, until frosting is smooth.
4. Spread top of each cone with frosting, using a small wooden or metal spatula. Decorate each cone with either candy corn, sprinkles or whatever you like.
