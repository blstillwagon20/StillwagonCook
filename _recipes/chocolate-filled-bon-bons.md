## Ingredients

- ¾ cups Crisco
- ½ cup sugar
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 1¾ cup flour
- ½ tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. salt
- 3½ to 4 doz. chocolate kisses or Rolos
- Confectioner sugar or frosting, to finish

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°. Using electric mixer cream Crisco (¾ cups) and sugar (½ cup) til fluffy. Add egg (1), vanilla (2 tsp.). Beat well. Add flour (1¾ cup), baking powder (½ tsp.), and salt (½ tsp.). Mix.
2. Form dough into 1 inch balls. Press ball around candy until completely covered.
3. Bake 12 minutes on ungreased cookie sheet. Frost or decorate as desired or roll in confectioner sugar.
