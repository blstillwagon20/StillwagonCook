> I got this recipe from my Taste of Home magazine and it's absolutely delicious. I hope you try it!

## Ingredients

- ½ cup oil
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 can (10¾ oz.) tomato soup, undiluted
- 1 egg
- 2 cups flour
- 1½ tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- Dash salt
- 1 cup shredded carrots
- ½ cup chopped walnuts, plus more for the top
- ½ cup golden raisins (optional)

### Frosting

- 1 pkg. (8 oz.) cream cheese
- 3 cups confectioners sugar
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Steps

1. Cream oil (½ cup) and sugar (1 cup). Add soup (1 can, 10¾ oz.) and egg (1). Mix well.
2. Beat into mixture flour (2 cups), baking soda (1½ tsp.), cinnamon (1 tsp.) and salt. Stir in carrots (1 cup), walnuts (½ cup) and raisins (½ cup).
3. Pour into 10 inch fluted tube pan or loaf pan. Bake at 350° for 45 to 50 minutes or until cake tests done.
4. Cool in pan 10 minutes before removing. Cool cake.
5. **Frosting:** Beat until smooth. Frost and sprinkle with chopped walnuts.
