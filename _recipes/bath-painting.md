> Do your kid's love to paint but make a terrible mess when doing so? Try this out!

## Items needed

- Ivory dishwashing detergent or detergent very mild and white or clear colored
- Assortment of food coloring
- Plastic ice cube tray
- Small paintbrush

## Steps

1. If you have a little artist on your hands, this is a great way for them to create and get clean all at the same time.
2. Just put some very mild liquid detergent into each compartment of the ice cube tray. Add one or two drops of food coloring to make an array of interesting colors, stir and hand over the paintbrush.
3. Let your kids get clean at the same time by filling the tub up for their baths. This will just rinse right off the walls in the bathtub so they can have a blast!!!
