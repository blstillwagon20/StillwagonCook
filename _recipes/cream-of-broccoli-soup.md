> I got this recipe off the back of a powdered milk recipe and it's very good and low in fat!

## Ingredients

- 3½ cups (two 14½ oz. cans) chicken broth
- 3 pounds fresh broccoli, cut into 3 inch pieces
- 1 cup coarsely chopped onion
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 cup dry nonfat milk
- ¼ cup flour
- 1 cup water
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Steps

1. Heat chicken broth (3½ cups, two 14½ oz. cans) until boiling; add onion (1 cup), ⅔ of the broccoli, and garlic (1 clove) and cook covered until tender.
2. Meanwhile, take the remaining ⅓ of the broccoli and steam until tender.
3. Allow to cool 5 to 7 minutes. Put the chicken broth mixture into blender and blend until smooth (you'll probably have to do it in 2 or more batches).
4. Mix nonfat dry milk (1 cup), flour (¼ cup), and water (1 cup) in bowl. Add to the pureed mixture and return to stove. Heat through.
5. Add salt and pepper to taste. Stir in the steamed broccoli.

**Tip:** To make broccoli cheese soup, add 1 cup cheddar cheese.
