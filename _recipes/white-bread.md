## Ingredients

- 6 to 7 cups flour
- 3 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 Tbsp. salt
- 2 Tbsp. shortening
- 2 pkgs. regular or quick-acting active dry yeast
- ½ cup warm water (120° to 130°)
- 1¾ cups scalded milk (then allowed to cool to warm temp.)
- Margarine, for brushing

## Steps

1. Mix 3½ cups of the flour, the sugar (3 Tbsp.), salt (1 Tbsp.), shortening (2 Tbsp.), and yeast in 4-quart bowl; add warm water (½ cup) and milk (1¾ cups). Beat on low speed, scraping bowl frequently, 1 minute. Beat on medium speed, scraping bowl frequently, 1 minute.
2. Stir in enough remaining flour, 1 cup at a time, to make dough easy to handle. (Just add enough to make it workable, otherwise your bread will be dry.)
3. Turn dough onto lightly floured surface; knead until smooth and elastic, about 10 minutes. Place in greased 2½-quart bowl; turn greased side up. Cover and let rise in warm place until double, 40 to 60 minutes. (Dough is ready if indentation remains when touched.)
4. Punch down dough; divide into halves. Let rest 5 minutes.
5. Flatten each half with hands or rolling pin into rectangle, 18" x 9", on lightly floured surface. (If dough shrinks, gently stretch into rectangle.) Fold 9" sides crosswise into thirds, overlapping sides.
6. Roll up tightly toward you, beginning at open end. Pinch edge of dough into roll to seal well. Press each end with side of hand to seal; fold ends under loaf.
7. Place loaves seam sides down in 2 greased loaf pans. Cover, and let rise until double, 35 to 50 minutes.
8. Heat oven to 350°. Place loaves on low rack so that tops of pans are in center of oven. Bake until deep golden brown, about 25 to 30 minutes.
9. Remove from pans. Brush loaves with margarine for a soft crust and allow to cool on wire rack.
