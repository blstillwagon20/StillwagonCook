> This is delicious and low-fat. The pudding looks like mud when you put it in the oven, but it bakes up perfectly.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt
- ⅔ cup sugar
- 6 Tbsp. cocoa, divided
- ½ cup milk
- 2 Tbsp. butter, melted
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1½ cups boiling water

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°. Sift together flour (2 cups), powder (2 tsp.), salt (1 tsp.), sugar (⅔ cup), and 2 Tbsp. cocoa into a large bowl. Add the milk (½ cup), butter (2 Tbsp.) and vanilla (1 tsp.); mix only until smooth. Then transfer to an oiled 1 qt. baking dish (10" x 6").
2. In a medium bowl, combine the brown sugar (1 cup) and the remaining 4 Tbsp. cocoa; sprinkle over the top of the chocolate mixture.
3. Pour the boiling water (1½ cups) over all, and DO NOT STIR IN. Bake for 40 minutes, or until top appears to be firm.
4. Allow to stand 10 minutes before cutting into squares. Serve warm, pudding side up.
