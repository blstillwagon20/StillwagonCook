> This is a very festive, light dessert perfect for the Christmas holiday.

## Ingredients

- 1 Keebler Ready-Crust graham cracker pie crust (or make your own)
- 1 cup crushed Keebler Grasshopper Fudge Mint Cookies
- 3 Tbsp. hot water
- 3 oz. cream cheese, softened
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp. milk
- ¼ tsp. peppermint extract
- 3½ cups (8 oz.) Cool Whip
- 6–10 drops green food coloring
- Red hot cinnamon candies
- Spearmint leaves

## Steps

1. Mix cookies (1 cup) and hot water (3 Tbsp.). Spoon into prepared graham cracker crust and gently spread to cover the bottom.
2. Beat cream cheese (3 oz.) until smooth; gradually beat in sugar (⅓ cup), milk (2 Tbsp.) and peppermint extract (¼ tsp.). Fold in whipped topping.
3. Divide mixture in half; stir food coloring (6–10 drops) into one half until evenly colored.
4. Fill pie crust (1) with alternate drops of white and green whipped topping mixtures. Smooth top of pie with spatula.
5. Chill 3 hours.
6. Make berries and holly leaves using the cinnamon candies and spearmint leaves to decorate around the edge and middle of the pie.
