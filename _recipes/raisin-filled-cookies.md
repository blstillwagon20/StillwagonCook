## Ingredients

### Filling

- 1 lb. box raisins (ground) (optional)
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups water
- 3 Tbsp. flour

### Cake dough

- 2 cups sugar
- 1 large cup shortening
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 1 cup sweet milk
- 1 tsp. salt
- 7 cups flour (all purpose)
- 4 tsp. cream of tartar
- 2 heaping tsp. baking soda

## Steps

1. **Filling:** Cook until thick. Best if made the day before and completely cooled (spreads easier).
2. **Cake dough:** Cream sugar (2 cups) and shortening (1 large cup). Add eggs (2). Add vanilla (2 tsp.) to milk (1 cup). Combine dry ingredients and add alternating with milk.
3. Roll on floured board to ¼ inch thick. Cut into rounds.
4. Put filling on first round; top with second and seal edges by pressing together. Pierce top with fork a couple times to vent.
5. Place on greased cookie sheet. Bake at 350° for 10 to 15 minutes or until light brown.
