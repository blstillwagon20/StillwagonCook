> Before baking cut ¼ inch diameter hole in dough on top to hang.

## Ingredients

- 1¾ cups flour
- ¼ cup cornstarch
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 large egg
- ½ tsp. vanilla
- ½ cup butter

## Steps

1. Mix flour (1¾ cups), cornstarch (¼ cup), and baking powder (1 tsp.), set aside.
2. Cream butter (½ cup) and sugar (1 cup) until pale and fluffy. Beat in egg (1 large) and vanilla (½ tsp.).
3. Gradually stir in flour mixture until blended. Chill until firm.
4. Roll, cut into desired shapes. Place on greased cookie sheet. Bake 350° for 8 minutes.
5. Decorate and hang on tree with red ribbon.
