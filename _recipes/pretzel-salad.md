## Ingredients

### Crust

- 2 cups broken pretzels (not too fine)
- ¾ cup melted butter
- 1 Tbsp. sugar

### Cream cheese layer

- 8 oz. cream cheese
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 large container Cool Whip

### Topping

- 1 large box strawberry gelatin
- 2 cups boiling water
- 2 (10 oz.) pkgs. frozen strawberries

## Steps

1. Combine crust ingredients and set in 9" x 13" pan. Bake at 400° for 8 minutes.
2. Cream together cream cheese (8 oz.) and sugar. Fold in Cool Whip (1 large container). Spread on top of cold crust.
3. Mix gelatin (1 large box), boiling water (2 cups) and frozen strawberries (2 pkgs., 10 oz.).
4. When gelatin has just slightly thickened, spoon over cheese layer and chill.
