## Ingredients

- 1 large green pepper, diced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 Tbsp. oil
- 2 pounds ground beef
- 2 (12 oz.) cans tomato paste
- 1 quart tomato juice
- 1 quart stewed tomatoes, slightly chopped
- ¾ quart water
- 1 Tbsp. salt
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- 1½ Tbsp. dried oregano
- 1¼ Tbsp. dried basil
- 1½ tsp. garlic powder
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 16 oz. mushrooms, fresh or canned (optional)

## Steps

1. In skillet, sauté green pepper (1 large) and onion (1 medium) in oil (2 Tbsp.) until tender. Remove from pan.
2. In same skillet, brown ground beef (2 pounds).
3. In large saucepan, combine tomato paste (2 cans, 12 oz.), tomato juice (1 quart), stewed tomatoes (1 quart) and water (¾ quart).
4. Season with salt (1 Tbsp.), sugar (1 Tbsp.), oregano (1½ Tbsp.), basil (1¼ Tbsp.), garlic powder (1½ tsp.) and pepper (¼ tsp.).
5. Add green pepper, onion and ground beef. Simmer until thickened.

If you like mushrooms, add 16 oz. either fresh or canned to the sauce before simmering.
