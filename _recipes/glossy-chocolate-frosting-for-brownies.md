## Ingredients

- 1½ Tbsp. shortening
- 1½ squares (1½ oz.) unsweetened chocolate
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- ⅛ tsp. salt
- 2 Tbsp. milk
- ½ tsp. vanilla

## Steps

1. Heat shortening (1½ Tbsp.) and chocolate over low heat until melted.
2. Stir in remaining ingredients; beat until smooth.
3. Place pan of frosting in bowl of ice and water; continue beating until frosting is smooth and able to spread.
