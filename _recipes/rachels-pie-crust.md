## Ingredients

- 4 cups flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1¾ cups shortening
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp. vinegar
- ½–¾ cup water

## Steps

1. Beat egg (1), vinegar (1 tsp.) and water (½–¾ cup) together.
2. Mix flour (4 cups), salt (½ tsp.) and shortening (1¾ cups) together; then add egg mixture to it.
3. Roll out on floured table. Makes 5 pie crusts.
