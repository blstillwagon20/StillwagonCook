> This is a recipe for homemade bubble solution for the kids. It works well and is much less expensive!

## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- 4 Tbsp. dish soap
- 1 Tbsp. salad oil or glycerin

## Steps

1. Mix together.
2. Make bubbles! °o°o°o°o°o°o°
