> This recipe is very easy and very good.

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs. ground meat, browned and fat drained
- 2 packs McCormick chili seasoning
- 12 oz. tomato paste
- 40 oz. canned kidney beans
- 4 cups diced canned tomatoes
- Enough water to make the correct consistency

## Steps

1. Combine all ingredients in a large pot.
2. Bring to boil and reduce heat to a simmer.
3. Allow to cook for 1 to 2 hours.
