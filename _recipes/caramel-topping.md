## Ingredients

- 1 (12 oz.) pkg. caramels
- 1 can sweetened condensed milk

## Steps

1. Put both ingredients into a microwave safe bowl and microwave on high at one minute intervals, stirring after each, until completely melted.
2. If this mixture is a little too thick, just add some milk and thin to your liking.
3. Use over ice cream or even dip apple slices into this. It's very good! Refrigerate after serving.
