> My mother makes this dish and until a couple of years ago, I wouldn't even try it since I wasn't a fan of tomatoes in my teens. Then to my surprise when I finally got up the nerve to try a tomato and found that I not only liked them, I loved them, I started preparing this dish for my husband and I. It's very easy, quick and delicious and I usually serve it as a side dish.

## Ingredients

- 4 slices white toast
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- ½ tsp. garlic salt
- 2½ cups tomatoes (peeled and stewed)
- 1 tsp. grated onion
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 8 oz. mozzarella cheese
- ¼ cup parmesan cheese
- 1½ Tbsp. parsley

## Steps

1. Spread toast (4 slices) with butter (2 Tbsp.); sprinkle with garlic salt (½ tsp.) and cut toast into cubes.
2. Combine tomatoes (2½ cups), onion (1 tsp.), salt (1 tsp.) and pepper (¼ tsp.).
3. Place cubes in greased casserole; add tomatoes.
4. Top with mozzarella and parmesan cheeses (¼ cup) and parsley (1½ Tbsp.). (I added the cheese to this dish. I think it's very good with or without it.)
5. Bake in 375° oven for 30 minutes.
