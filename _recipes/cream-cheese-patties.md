> This is a tasty recipe from my mother-in-law.

## Ingredients

- 1 (3 oz.) pkg. cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup ground cracker crumbs
- 1 cup chopped walnuts or lima beans or sunflower seeds
- 2 eggs or 2 tsp. egg replacer
- ¼ tsp. sage
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 1 finely chopped onion, or replace with 1 envelope Lipton's dry onion soup mix
- 1 (10¾ oz.) can cream of mushroom soup + 1 can water

## Steps

1. Mix all ingredients well except soup and water.
2. Drop by spoonfuls on a well-greased frying pan. Flatten and let brown. Brown both sides.
3. Place patties in a deep casserole dish.
4. Mix soup and water and pour over patties.
5. Bake at 350° for 20 minutes or until bubbly.
