> This is a very good marinade for fish steaks on the grill. I've used it on both catfish steaks and swordfish steaks. I hope you try it.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lbs. fresh or thawed frozen thick fish steaks or fillets
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
- ¼ cup water
- ⅓ cup lemon juice
- 3 Tbsp. soy sauce
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. garlic salt

## Steps

1. Cut fish in serving portions.
2. Mix the remaining ingredients and pour marinade over fish.
3. Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes, turning once.
4. Grill.
