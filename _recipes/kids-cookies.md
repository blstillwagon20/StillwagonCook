> This sturdy kid's cookies dough makes it easy for small hands to create fun shapes. And since these treats hold their form while they bake, there won't be any sad surprises when they come out of the oven. Try it! Your kids will have a ball making these yummy goodies and showing off their creations.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups unsalted butter, softened
- 1½ cups sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp. vanilla extract
- 6 cups flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- Food colors, candy decorations*

## Steps

1. In bowl, at high speed, beat butter (1½ cups) and sugar (1½ cups) until light and fluffy. Beat in eggs (2 large) and vanilla.
2. In two batches, beat in flour (6 cups) and salt (½ tsp.). Tint dough with food colors. Use immediately, or cover and store in refrigerator. Allow to come to room temperature before using.
3. Preheat oven to 450°. Shape and decorate dough. Place on baking sheet; bake until set.
4. Cool completely on wire rack. Makes 6 dozen.

*Offer kids nonsticky toppings, such as M&Ms, colored sprinkles, gumdrops, red hots, licorice and nuts.
