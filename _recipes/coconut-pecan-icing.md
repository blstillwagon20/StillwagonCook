> This is my dad's favorite icing for on his German chocolate cakes, and I'll have to admit, it is good and fattening!

## Ingredients

- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1 cup sugar
- 3 egg yolks
- ½ cup butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1½ cups flaked coconut
- 1 cup chopped pecans

## Steps

1. Combine milk, sugar (1 cup), egg yolks (3), butter (½ cup) and vanilla (1 tsp.). Cook and stir over medium heat until thickened (approximately 12 minutes); remove from heat.
2. Add in coconut (1½ cups) and pecans (1 cup) (if necessary, add extra amounts of these to thicken). Beat until thick.
3. Spread between layers and on top of devils food or German chocolate cake.
