## Ingredients

- Pastry for single-crust pie
- 1 cup sugar
- ⅓ cup flour
- 1 tsp. ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp. ground allspice
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 12 cups thinly sliced, peeled cooking apples (4 lbs.)
- 3 Tbsp. butter
- Milk and sugar, for the top
- Light cream or cheddar cheese, for serving

## Steps

1. Prepare pastry. Roll out to a 13 x 8½-inch rectangle; cut slits in pastry.
2. Combine sugar (1 cup), flour (⅓ cup), cinnamon (1 tsp.), allspice (½ tsp.), and salt (¼ tsp.); mix lightly with apples.
3. Turn into a 12 x 7½ x 2-inch baking dish (apples will mound higher than sides). Dot with butter (3 Tbsp.).
4. Carefully place pastry atop apples. Flute to the sides but not over the edge. Brush with milk and sprinkle with sugar.
5. To prevent overbrowning, cover edge of pie with foil. Bake at 375° for 25 minutes.
6. Remove foil and bake until crust is golden, 20 to 25 minutes more.
7. Serve warm in dishes; pass cream or cheddar cheese.
