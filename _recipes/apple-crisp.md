## Ingredients

- 4 cups sliced apples
- ⅔ to ¾ cup brown sugar
- ½ cup flour
- ½ cup oats
- ¾ tsp. cinnamon
- ¾ tsp. nutmeg
- ⅓ cup butter or margarine, softened

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 375°. Grease square pan (8" x 8" x 2").
2. Place apple slices in pan.
3. Mix remaining ingredients thoroughly. Sprinkle over apples (4 cups).
4. Bake 30 minutes or until apples are tender and topping is golden brown.
