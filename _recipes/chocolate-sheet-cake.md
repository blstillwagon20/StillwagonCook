## Ingredients

- 1¼ cup margarine, divided
- ½ cup cocoa, divided
- 1 cup water
- 2 cups flour
- 1½ cups packed brown sugar
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. cinnamon (optional)
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 (14 oz.) can sweetened condensed milk, divided
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 cup confectioners sugar
- 1 cup chopped nuts (optional)

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°.
2. In small saucepan melt 1 cup margarine; stir in ¼ cup cocoa, then water (1 cup). Bring to boil; remove from heat.
3. Combine flour (2 cups), brown sugar (1½ cups), soda (1 tsp.), cinnamon (1 tsp.), and salt (1 tsp.) in large bowl. Add cocoa mixture; beat well.
4. Stir in ⅓ cup condensed milk, eggs (2) and vanilla (1 tsp.). Pour into greased jelly roll pan. Bake 15 minutes.
5. In small saucepan melt remaining margarine; stir in remaining ¼ cup cocoa and remaining condensed milk. Stir in confectioner sugar and nuts (1 cup). Spread on warm cake.
