> This is a recipe from my mother and is just great.

## Ingredients

- 6 cups milk
- ¾ cup sugar
- 4 heaping Tbsp. cornstarch
- 4 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- Coconut, as much as you like
- 2 baked pie shells
- Meringue (see Perfect Meringue)

## Steps

1. I like to do all of this in the microwave so it doesn't burn on the bottom, but it can be done on the stove if you like.
2. Heat milk (6 cups) until scalding. Add sugar (¾ cup) and cornstarch (4 heaping Tbsp.) to milk.
3. Separate eggs (4) and beat yolks.
4. Stir ½ cup hot milk mixture into egg yolks, then add egg mixture to the rest of the milk mixture and cook until thick, stirring very often. Remove from heat.
5. Stir in vanilla (1 tsp.) and enough coconut for your liking. Pour into pie shells (2).
6. Add meringue onto hot pie filling and bake at 400° about 5 to 10 minutes.
