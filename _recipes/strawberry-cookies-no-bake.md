## Ingredients

- 1 pkg. (8 oz.) dates, chopped
- ½ cup flaked coconut
- ½ cup sugar
- 4 Tbsp. butter or margarine
- 1 slightly beaten egg
- 1½ cup rice krispies
- ½ cup chopped walnutes
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- Red sugar or strawberry Jello, for rolling
- Frosting leaves

## Steps

1. Cook dates (1 pkg., 8 oz.), coconut (½ cup), sugar (½ cup), butter (4 Tbsp.) and egg (1) in skillet on low heat until gooey.
2. Take off heat; add rice krispies (1½ cup), walnuts and vanilla (1 tsp.).
3. Cool until workable. Wet hands. Use 1 Tbsp., shape into strawberry, roll in red sugar or strawberry Jello, trim with frosting leaves.
