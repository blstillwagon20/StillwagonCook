## Ingredients

- 1⅓ cup oil (canola)
- ½ cup vinegar (red wine)
- ⅓ cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- 1½ Tbsp. oregano (dried or fresh)
- 2 Tbsp. parsley (dried or fresh)
- 1½ Tbsp. yellow mustard
- ½ tsp. garlic powder
- 1½ tsp. Salad Supreme seasoning
- ¼ tsp. paprika
- 2 tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. pepper
- Chicken

## Steps

1. Mix all together in blender.
2. Marinate chicken at least 2 hours.
3. Grill to perfection!
