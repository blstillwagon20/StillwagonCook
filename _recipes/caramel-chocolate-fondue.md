## Ingredients

- 1 can (14 oz.) sweetened condensed milk
- 1 jar (12 oz.) caramel topping
- 1 pkg. (6 oz.) semi-sweet chocolate pieces

## Steps

1. In saucepan combine condensed milk (1 can, 14 oz.), caramel topping (1 jar, 12 oz.) and chocolate pieces (1 pkg., 6 oz.).
2. Stir over low heat til chocolate melts. Place in fondue pot.
