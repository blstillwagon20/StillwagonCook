## Ingredients

### Crust

- 1½ cups flour
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 tsp. salt
- ½ cup salad oil
- 2 Tbsp. milk

### Filling

- 1 small box strawberry Jello
- 2 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1 cup sugar
- 1¾ cups boiling water
- Whole strawberries
- Whipped cream

## Steps

1. **Crust:** Sift together flour (1½ cups), sugar (2 Tbsp.) and salt (1 tsp.). Mix and whip oil (½ cup) and milk (2 Tbsp.). Stir and mix above ingredients together. Press into a 9" pie plate. Bake at 425° for 10 minutes or until lightly brown.
2. **Filling:** Pour boiling water (1¾ cups) over Jello (1 small box), cornstarch (2 Tbsp.) and sugar (1 cup). Bring to boil, stirring. Remove from heat and let thicken slightly.
3. Put whole strawberries in crust. Pour filling over strawberries. Chill thoroughly.
4. Serve topped with whipped cream.
