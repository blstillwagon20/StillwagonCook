## Ingredients

- 1 cup milk
- 5 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 cup cool water
- 6 Tbsp. melted Crisco shortening
- 1 package yeast
- 5–6 cups flour

## Steps

1. Scald milk (1 cup); add sugar (5 Tbsp.) and salt (1 tsp.). Add cool water (1 cup) to cool the mixture (this should leave it a warm temperature still). Add melted Crisco. Add yeast (1 package).
2. Add 3 cups flour and blend till smooth. Add up to another 2 to 3 cups of flour till dough is slightly sticky but can be handled (don't put too much flour in, otherwise your rolls will be dry).
3. Knead about 10 minutes until dough is nice and elastic.
4. Put into greased bowl and flip over so dough has some shortening (6 Tbsp.) on the top of it. Cover and let rise in a warm place about 1 hour till doubled.
5. Punch down. Let rest 5 minutes. Then make into whatever shape desired.
6. Cover and let rise until double again. Bake in a 350° oven until golden brown.

These are delicious and can be used for any type of roll desired, including pepperoni rolls.
