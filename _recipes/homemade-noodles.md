> I got this recipe out of a very old Betty Crocker cookbook and it is just wonderful. I hope you try it the next time your recipe calls for noodles. They're great for stroganoff also! I usually triple the below recipe.

## Ingredients

### Beat until very light

- 3 egg yolks
- 1 whole egg

### Beat in

- 3 Tbsp. cold water
- 1 tsp. salt

### Stir in and work in with hands

- 2 cups flour

## Steps

1. Beat egg yolks (3) and egg (1) until very light. Beat in cold water (3 Tbsp.) and salt (1 tsp.). Stir in flour (2 cups) and work in with hands.
2. Divide dough into 3 parts. Roll out each piece as thin as possible (paper thin) on lightly floured surface.
3. Fold dough to make 2" wide (sort of jelly roll style but flat) and cut with a sharp knife into desired width (⅛" for fine noodles, up to ½" for broad noodles). Shake out the strips.
4. Boil in salted water until they float to the top. It takes a very short time to cook these noodles. Don't overcook! Enjoy!
