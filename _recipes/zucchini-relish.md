## Ingredients

### Day 1

- 12 cups shredded zucchini (unpeeled)
- 3 cups chopped onions (or 2¼ cups shredded)
- 5 Tbsp. canning & pickling salt

## Steps

### Day 1

1. Shred the zucchini (12 cups). Do not peel. If the zucchini is large, cut it in half and scrape out the seeds. (2 cups of zucchini = 250 g.)
2. Shred or chop the onions (3 cups if chopped, or 2¼ cups if shredded — ¾ of the chopped amount).
3. Add the salt (5 Tbsp. canning & pickling salt). Combine in a NON-metallic bowl, cover, and refrigerate overnight.
