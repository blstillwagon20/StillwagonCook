> This recipe is so pretty for Christmas not to mention tasty and chewy. My sister Vicki made them the first time I ever had them and they're perfect to spruce up the holiday plate of cookies.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter
- 40 large marshmallows
- 1 Tbsp. green food coloring
- 4 cups corn flakes or Special K (works great!)
- Little red hots candy

## Steps

1. Melt together butter (½ cup) and marshmallows (40 large). Stir in food coloring (1 Tbsp.).
2. Add corn flakes (4 cups). Stir rapidly to coat thoroughly.
3. Drop by spoonfuls on wax paper. Place 3 small red candies on each immediately.
