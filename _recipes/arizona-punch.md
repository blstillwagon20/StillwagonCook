## Ingredients

- 2 pkg. (3 oz.) strawberry Jello
- 2 cups boiling water
- 2 cups sugar
- 4 cups cold water
- 2 (46 oz.) cans pineapple juice
- 3–2 liter bottles of gingerale

## Steps

1. Dissolve Jello (2 pkg., 3 oz.) and sugar (2 cups) with boiling water in large bowl. Add water and pineapple juice (2 cans, 46 oz.).
2. Divide into 3 (approximately ½ gallon each) containers, plastic or milk cartons. Freeze.
3. Take out 2 hours before serving. When ready, chip block up to make it slushy (not too fine). (You may have to cut container to remove frozen punch.)
4. Mix in punch bowl or large pitcher 1 bottle of cold gingerale and 1 container of punch mixture.
