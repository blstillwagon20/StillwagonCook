## Ingredients

- 1½ cups peach brandy
- Sugar (2½ cups, three times — see steps)
- 1 can sliced peaches (2½ cups)
- 1 large can crushed pineapple
- 2 (8 oz. each) jars maraschino cherries
- 1 box cake mix (for each cake)
- 4 eggs (for each cake)
- ⅓ cup oil (for each cake)
- Nuts, coconut, or chocolate chips

## Steps

1. Put 1½ cups peach brandy in 1 gallon glass jar. Add 1 large can sliced peaches (1 can) and 2½ cups sugar. Stir once a day for 10 days.
2. Add 1 large can crushed pineapple (1 large can) and 2½ cups sugar. Stir once a day for 10 days.
3. Add 2 (8 oz. each) jars maraschino cherries and 2½ cups sugar. Stir once a day for 10 days.
4. Divide cherries and 2½ cups at end of the 30 days. Use 1 box cake mix to 1½ cups fruit. Add 4 eggs and ⅓ cup oil. Add nuts, coconut, or chocolate chips if you like.
5. Bake 350° until done.
