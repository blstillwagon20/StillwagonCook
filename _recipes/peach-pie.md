## Ingredients

### 9-inch pie

- Pie pastry for two-crust pie (I use Miracle Pie Crust)
- 1 cup sugar
- ¼ cup flour
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 5 cups sliced fresh peaches or frozen (unthawed)
- 1 tsp. lemon juice
- 2 Tbsp. margarine or butter

### 10-inch pie

- Pie pastry for two-crust pie
- 1¼ cups sugar
- ⅓ cup flour
- 1¼ tsp. cinnamon
- 6 cups sliced fresh peaches or frozen (unthawed)
- 1 tsp. lemon juice
- 3 Tbsp. margarine or butter

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 425°. Prepare pastry.
2. Mix sugar, flour and cinnamon. Stir in peaches and lemon juice. Turn into pastry-lined pie plate. Dot with margarine.
3. Dampen edge of bottom crust; cover with top crust that has slits cut in it; seal and flute.
4. Cover edge with 2 to 3 inch strip of aluminum foil to prevent excessive browning; remove foil during last 15 minutes of baking.
5. Bake until crust is brown and juice begins to bubble through slits in crust, 35 to 45 minutes.
6. Serve with vanilla ice cream.
