## Ingredients

- 24 oz. oyster crackers
- 1 Tbsp. garlic salt
- 1 Tbsp. onion salt
- 1 Tbsp. dill weed
- 1 Tbsp. parsley
- 1 cup vegetable oil

## Steps

1. Put the above in a large bowl. Mix together until oil (1 cup) disappears.
2. Let it set, then mix again.

Once you start on these, you can't stop!
