## Ingredients

- 6 eggs
- 2 cups oil
- 4 cups grated zucchini
- 4 cups sugar
- 4 tsp. vanilla
- 6 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 4 tsp. baking soda
- 2 tsp. salt
- 4 tsp. cinnamon

## Steps

1. Sift flour (6 cups), baking powder (2 tsp.), baking soda (4 tsp.), salt (2 tsp.) and cinnamon (4 tsp.) together and set aside.
2. Beat eggs (6) well. Slowly add oil (2 cups), sugar (4 cups) and raw zucchini (4 cups). Add the flour mixture. Add the vanilla (4 tsp.) last.
3. Place in greased and floured pans. Bake at 350°: full-size loaf pans 60 to 75 minutes; muffins 20 minutes.
4. Test with knife in middle of loaves to make sure they are baked through.
