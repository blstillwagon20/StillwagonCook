## Ingredients and tools

- Corn starch
- Water
- Cold cream
- Food coloring
- Muffin tin

## Steps

1. In each cup of the muffin tin, mix 1 tsp. corn starch, ½ tsp. water, ½ tsp. cold cream and a couple of drops of food coloring — a different color for each cup. Stir.
2. You're ready. See if you can get your grown-up assistant to sit still and let you practice.
