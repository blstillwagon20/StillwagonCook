> This recipe is my mother's and is very good. It can be used in spaghetti sauce or meatball sandwiches.

## Ingredients

- 4 slices bread
- 1 lb. ground beef
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup grated parmesan cheese
- ½ tsp. garlic salt
- Oil, for browning
- Your favorite spaghetti sauce

## Steps

1. Soak bread (4 slices) in water 2 to 3 minutes. Squeeze out excess moisture.
2. Mix with remaining ingredients. Make into balls and brown in oil.
3. Add to favorite spaghetti sauce. Cook at least 20 minutes longer.
