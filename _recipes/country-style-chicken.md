## Ingredients

- 3–3½ lb. broiler-fryer chicken
- 1 Tbsp. margarine or butter
- 1 can (10½ oz.) condensed chicken broth
- ¾ tsp. chopped fresh or ¼ tsp. dried thyme leaves
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 8 medium carrots, cut into fourths
- 8 whole small white onions
- 4 medium potatoes, cut into fourths
- ½ cup dry white wine or chicken broth
- 2 Tbsp. cold water
- 1 Tbsp. cornstarch

## Steps

1. Fold wings of chicken (3–3½ lb.) across back with tips touching. Tie or skewer drumsticks to tail.
2. Heat margarine (1 Tbsp.) in nonstick Dutch oven until melted. Cook chicken in margarine over medium heat until brown on all sides; drain.
3. Heat oven to 375°. Pour broth over chicken in Dutch oven. Sprinkle with thyme and pepper (¼ tsp.). Cover and bake 45 minutes.
4. Arrange carrots (8 medium), onions (8) and potatoes (4 medium) around chicken. Cover and bake 1 to 1¼ hours longer or until juices of chicken run clear.
5. Remove chicken and vegetables from Dutch oven; keep warm.
6. Skim fat from pan juices in Dutch oven. Stir wine (½ cup) or broth into juices. Heat to boiling.
7. Mix cold water (2 Tbsp.) and cornstarch (1 Tbsp.); stir into wine mixture. Heat to boiling, stirring constantly. Boil and stir 1 minute.
8. Serve chicken and vegetables with sauce.
