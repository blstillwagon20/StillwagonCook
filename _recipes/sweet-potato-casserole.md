> I got this recipe from my mother-in-law. It's a great side dish for a Thanksgiving meal.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups sweet potatoes, boiled, cooled, then skin removed (6–8, drained)
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 stick butter, melted
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla

### Topping

- ⅓ cup flour
- ¼ cup butter
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup nuts

## Steps

1. Mix together all but the sweet potatoes (3 cups) until well blended. Stir in sweet potatoes.
2. Mix topping ingredients together.
3. Put sweet potato mixture into casserole dish. Sprinkle all the topping on top.
4. Bake at 350° for ½ hour.
