## Ingredients

- ½ cup shortening
- 2 cups flour
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- 3 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¾ cup milk

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 450°. Cut shortening (½ cup) into flour (2 cups), sugar (1 Tbsp.), baking powder (3 tsp.) and salt (1 tsp.) with pastry blender until mixture resembles fine crumbs.
2. Stir in milk (¾ cup) until dough leaves side of bowl (dough will be soft and sticky).
3. Turn dough onto lightly floured surface. Knead lightly 10 times. Roll or pat ½ inch thick. Cut with floured 2½-inch round cutter.
4. Place on ungreased cookie sheet about 1 inch apart for crusty sides, touching for soft sides.
5. Bake until golden brown, 10 to 12 minutes. Immediately remove from cookie sheet. Butter the tops and serve warm.

**Drop biscuits:** Increase milk to 1 cup. Drop dough by spoonfuls onto greased cookie sheet.
