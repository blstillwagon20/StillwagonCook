## Ingredients

- ½ or more cup Oreo cookies, crushed
- 1 Tbsp. chocolate ice cream sauce
- Strawberry ice cream — ¼ container of 1 qt.
- Vanilla ice cream — ½ container of 1 qt.
- Chocolate ice cream — ¼ container of 1 qt.
- 3 cups mixed fruit (fresh), optional

## Steps

1. Coat inside of bundt pan with cooking spray. Place pan in freezer.
2. Combine cookie crumbs and chocolate sauce in small bowl. Spoon softened strawberry ice cream into pan pressing firmly. Cover and freeze at least 1 hour. Spoon vanilla ice cream adding cookie crumbs over layer. Freeze. Add chocolate ice cream. Freeze until firm. Can be done a day or two ahead of time.
3. When ready to serve, run thin blade of knife around center part of pan. Quickly dip pan in water. Invert pan onto serving plate. Spoon fruit (3 cups) into center.
