## Ingredients

- 4 oz. cream cheese, softened
- 1 Tbsp. milk or half & half
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- 1½ cups Cool Whip, plus more for garnish
- 1 graham cracker pie crust
- 1 cup milk or half & half
- 2 pkg. (4 serving size) Jell-O vanilla instant pudding
- 1 can (16 oz.) pumpkin
- 1 tsp. ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp. ginger
- ¼ tsp. ground cloves
- Nuts (optional)

## Steps

1. Mix cream cheese (4 oz.), 1 Tbsp. milk or half & half, and sugar (1 Tbsp.) in large bowl with wire whisk until well blended. Gently stir in Cool Whip (1½ cups). Spread on bottom of crust.
2. Pour 1 cup milk or half & half into bowl. Add pudding (2 pkg., 4 serving size) mix. Beat with wire whisk until well blended, 1 to 2 minutes (mixture will be thick).
3. Stir in pumpkin (1 can, 16 oz.) and spices. Mix well. Spread over cream cheese layer.
4. Refrigerate at least 3 hours. Garnish with additional Cool Whip and nuts if desired.
