> This icing is great to decorate cakes with. It gets hard on top but stays soft underneath and is easy to handle.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Crisco
- 2 egg whites
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 lb. powdered sugar
- 2 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1 tsp. flavoring (½ vanilla and ½ butter flavoring if you have it)

## Steps

1. Beat Crisco (1 cup) with ½ of powdered sugar until well blended.
2. Then add rest of ingredients and beat for 5 minutes until very smooth.
3. This keeps in the refrigerator for 1 month or it can be frozen.
