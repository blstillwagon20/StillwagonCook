> A true Tex-Mex dish. The reduced-fat soup and sour cream work just as well for this dish for all you calorie watchers.

## Ingredients

- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 (8 oz.) container sour cream
- 1 small can chopped green chilies
- 2–3 cups chopped or shredded chicken
- Salt, pepper, and garlic
- 2 cups grated jack cheese
- 1 cup chopped onions
- 1 pkg. flour or corn tortillas

## Steps

1. Combine first 3 ingredients in a large bowl.
2. Cook chicken in a skillet or boil it. Chop or shred. Return to heat and add seasonings.
3. In a medium bowl, combine the salt, pepper, garlic, cheese (2 cups), and onions (1 cup).
4. Heat tortillas and fill with meat, then soup mixture, and cheese mixture. Roll up and place in 9" x 13" baking dish seam side down.
5. Top with remaining soup and cheese.
6. Bake at 350° for 20 to 30 minutes or until sides bubble.
