## Ingredients

- ½ cup cooking oil
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 2 cups flour
- ½ tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. baking powder
- 3 Tbsp. milk
- ½ tsp. vanilla
- ½ cup nuts

## Steps

1. Beat oil (½ cup) and sugar (1 cup) together. Add eggs (2) and banana pulp; beat well.
2. Add sifted dry ingredients, milk (3 Tbsp.) and vanilla (½ tsp.). Mix well and stir in nuts (½ cup).
3. Pour into greased and floured 9" x 5" x 3" pan. Bake at 350° for 1 hour.
