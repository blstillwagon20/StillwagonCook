> I got the idea for this recipe from my mother-in-law who is vegetarian and a great cook. She makes sloppy joes with tofu so I just made my own recipe using that idea. We like to eat it on open faced rolls and I think it is better than the meat variety. Try it!

**Tip:** Tofu takes on a better texture for this recipe if it is first frozen, then thawed, then the tofu whey squeezed out of it. Crumble this for the tofu or meat substitute. It's high in protein and takes on the flavor of whatever you use with it.

## Ingredients

- 1 pkg. (19 oz.) firm tofu, done as stated above
- 1 Tbsp. oil
- 1 small onion, chopped very finely, or you can use onion powder
- 1 green or red bell pepper, chopped fine
- ½ cup creamy peanut butter
- 2 small cans tomato sauce (or as much as needed to get the sauce for the sloppy joe's)
- 2 Tbsp. soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
- Salt and pepper
- A little cayenne pepper for spice (use as much as you like, just remember a little goes a long way)
- Open-faced rolls, for serving

## Steps

1. Heat a large skillet on medium, add oil (1 Tbsp.) and sauté onion (1 small) and pepper until soft.
2. Turn heat up and add crumbled tofu (1 pkg., 19 oz.) and fry, stirring constantly, to cook and brown a little.
3. Turn the heat to medium-low, add the rest of the ingredients and stir until well combined.
4. Heat through and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes. Serve over open-faced rolls.
