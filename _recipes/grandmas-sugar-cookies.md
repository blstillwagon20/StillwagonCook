> These are a favorite of mine from my grandmother. There a nice soft cookie everyone loves.

## Ingredients

### Cream

- 1½ cups confectioner sugar
- 1 cup butter

### Add

- 1 egg
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- ½ tsp. almond flavoring (optional)

### Mix

- 2½ cups flour
- 1 tsp. cream of tartar
- 1 tsp. soda

## Steps

1. Cream sugar and butter (1 cup). Add egg (1), vanilla (1 tsp.) and almond flavoring (½ tsp.).
2. Mix flour (2½ cups), cream of tartar (1 tsp.) and soda (1 tsp.); add to other ingredients.
3. Refrigerate 2 or 3 hours or overnight.
4. Roll out, cut into desired shapes. Bake at 375° for 7 to 8 minutes.
5. Decorate if desired with icing, or they can be sprinkled with sugar before baking.
