## Ingredients

- 4 eggs, separated
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- ¾ cup cake flour
- ¼ cup baking cocoa
- ¾ tsp. baking powder
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 3 cups ice cream, any flavor, softened

### Chocolate sauce

- 2 squares (1 oz. each) unsweetened baking chocolate
- ¼ cup butter or margarine
- ⅔ cup evaporated milk, heated to 160°–170°
- 1 cup sugar

## Steps

1. In a large mixing bowl, beat egg yolks on high for 3 minutes or until light and fluffy. Gradually add sugar and vanilla, beating until thick and lemon-colored.
2. Combine flour, cocoa (¼ cup) and baking powder (¾ tsp.); gradually add to egg yolk mixture. Beat egg whites and salt (¼ tsp.) until soft peaks form. Fold a small amount of egg whites into batter on low until well mixed (mixture will be thick). Fold in the remaining egg whites until no streaks of white remain.
3. Grease a 15" x 10" x 1" pan; line with waxed paper and grease and flour paper. Spread batter evenly in pan. Bake at 350° for 15 minutes or until cake springs back when lightly touched.
4. Turn out onto a linen towel dusted with confectioners' sugar. Peel off paper and roll up in towel. Cool for 30 minutes.
5. Unroll cake; spread with ice cream (3 cups) to within 1 inch of edges. Roll up again. Cover with plastic wrap and freeze until serving.
6. **Sauce:** In the top of a double boiler over hot water, melt chocolate and butter (¼ cup). Gradually add warm milk and sugar (1 cup); stir constantly for 5 minutes or until completely dissolved. Spoon over slices of cake. Yield: 10 servings (1¼ cups sauce).
