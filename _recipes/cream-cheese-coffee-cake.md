> These impressive loaves really sparkle on the buffet. You can't just eat one slice of this treat with its rich filling peeking out of sweet yeast bread. Once you drizzle on the glaze and savor that first piece, you'll know the extra work is worth it.

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 1 cup (8 oz.) sour cream
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup butter or margarine
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 pkgs. (¼ oz. each) active dry yeast
- ½ cup warm water (110° to 115°)
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 4 cups all-purpose flour

### Filling

- 2 pkgs. (8 oz. each) cream cheese, softened
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- ⅛ tsp. salt

### Glaze

- 2½ cups confectioners' sugar
- ¼ cup milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- Toasted sliced almonds, optional

## Steps

1. In a saucepan, combine sour cream (1 cup, 8 oz.), sugar, butter (½ cup) and salt. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, for 5 to 10 minutes or until well blended. Cool to room temperature.
2. In a mixing bowl, dissolve yeast (2 pkgs., ¼ oz. each) in water (½ cup). Add sour cream mixture and eggs; mix well. Gradually stir in flour (4 cups). (Dough will be very soft.) Cover and refrigerate overnight.
3. Next day, combine filling ingredients in a mixing bowl until well blended.
4. Turn dough onto a floured board; knead 5 to 6 times. Divide into four equal portions. Roll each portion into a 12" x 8" rectangle.
5. Spread ¼ of the filling on each to within 1 inch of the edges. Roll up jelly-roll style from long side; pinch seams and ends to seal.
6. Place, seam side down, on greased baking sheet. Cut six X's on top of loaves. Cover and let rise until nearly doubled, about 1 hour.
7. Bake at 375° for 20 to 25 minutes or until golden brown. Cool on wire racks.
8. Combine the first three glaze ingredients; drizzle over loaves. Sprinkle with almonds if desired. Store in the refrigerator.
