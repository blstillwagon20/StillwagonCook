## Ingredients

- 2 cups butter
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 4 eggs
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 4 cups flour
- 5 cups oatmeal (put small amounts into blender until it turns to powder; measure first, then blend)
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 2 tsp. baking soda
- 24 oz. bag chocolate chips
- One (8 oz.) Hershey bar (grated)
- 3 cups chopped nuts (any kind)
- Raisins (optional)

## Steps

1. Cream together butter (2 cups) and sugars (2 cups). Add eggs (4) and vanilla (2 tsp.).
2. Mix together flour (4 cups), oatmeal (5 cups), salt (1 tsp.), baking powder (2 tsp.) and baking soda (2 tsp.); mix together all ingredients.
3. Add chocolate chips (24 oz.), grated Hershey bar, nuts (3 cups) and raisins.
4. Make golf-ball size cookies and place them on the cookie sheets 2 inches apart. Bake on ungreased cookie sheet at 375° for 10 minutes. Makes 112 cookies.
