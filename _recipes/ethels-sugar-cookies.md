> These cookies were my favorites when I was a little girl. Now my boys love them. They expect these for every holiday.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup shortening
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2½ cups flour
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt

## Steps

1. Mix shortening (¾ cup), sugar (1 cup), eggs (2), and vanilla (1 tsp.) thoroughly. Stir flour (2½ cups), powder (1 tsp.) and salt (1 tsp.). Blend in.
2. Chill dough at least 1 hour.
3. Roll dough ⅛ inch on lightly floured surface. Cut with favorite cookie cutters.
4. Bake at 400° for 6 to 8 minutes.
