## Ingredients

- 1½ cups boiling water
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 Tbsp. butter or margarine, soft
- 3 eggs, separated
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- ¼ tsp. baking soda
- ½ can creamed corn

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 375°. Grease a 2 qt. casserole dish.
2. In large bowl, stir boiling water (1½ cups) into cornmeal (1 cup). Blend in butter (1 Tbsp.) and egg yolks.
3. Stir in buttermilk (1 cup), salt (1 tsp.), sugar (¼ cup), baking powder (1 tsp.) and baking soda (¼ tsp.). Put in creamed corn (½ can).
4. Beat egg whites just until soft peaks form; fold into batter.
5. Pour into casserole. Bake 45 to 50 minutes. Serve hot with butter.
