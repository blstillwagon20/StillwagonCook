## Ingredients

- 1 (18 oz.) pkg. Pillsbury sugar cookies
- 1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese
- 4 oz. Cool Whip
- Any fruit (strawberries, bananas, peaches, pineapple, etc.)

### Glaze

- ½ cup orange juice
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp. lemon juice
- ¼ cup water
- 1 Tbsp. cornstarch

## Steps

1. Slice and arrange Pillsbury sugar (½ cup) cookie dough side by side on pizza pan. Make into one whole pizza cookie and bake as directed on package. Cool crust.
2. Soften cream cheese (1 pkg., 8 oz.); mix with Cool Whip (4 oz.). Spread on cooled crust. Arrange fruit on top of cheese mixture.
3. In a small pan, mix water (¼ cup) and cornstarch (1 Tbsp.); stir in juice, sugar, and lemon juice (2 Tbsp.). Cook and stir; boil for 1 minute.
4. Pour over fruit; chill and serve in wedges.
