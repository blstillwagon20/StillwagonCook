## Ingredients

### 1st layer

- Any kind of brownie mix for a 9" x 13" pan

### 2nd layer

- 2 cups powdered sugar
- ½ cup soft butter
- 3 to 4 Tbsp. Creme de Menthe (either white or green, but if using white, add green food coloring)

### 3rd layer

- 6 Tbsp. butter
- 6 oz. Nestle semi-sweet morsels

## Steps

1. **1st layer:** Make brownies according to directions. Cool completely.
2. **2nd layer:** Mix altogether with mixer; put in refrigerator for ½ hour until cool. Then spread on top of brownies and put back in the refrigerator. Let cool again.
3. **3rd layer:** Melt butter (½ cup) on low heat and semi-sweet morsels (6 oz.) together. Then spread on top of your icing layer and refrigerate again.
4. When very cold, cut into small squares.
