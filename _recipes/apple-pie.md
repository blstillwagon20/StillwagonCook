## Ingredients

### 9-inch pie

- Pie pastry, enough for two-crust pie (I use Miracle Pie Crust)
- ¾ cup sugar
- ¼ cup flour
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- Dash of salt
- 6 cups thinly sliced pared tart apples (about 6 medium)
- 2 Tbsp. margarine or butter

### 10-inch pie

- Pie pastry, enough for two-crust pie
- 1 cup sugar
- ⅓ cup flour
- 2 tsp. cinnamon
- Dash of salt
- 8 cups thinly sliced pared tart apples (about 8 medium)
- 3 Tbsp. margarine or butter

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 425°. Prepare pastry.
2. Mix sugar, flour, cinnamon, and salt. Stir in apples. Turn into pastry-lined pie plate. Dot with margarine.
3. Cover with top crust that has slits cut in it; seal and flute.
4. Cover edge with 3-inch strip of aluminum foil to prevent excessive browning; remove foil during last 15 minutes of baking.
5. Bake until crust is brown and juice begins to bubble through slits in crust, 40 to 50 minutes.
