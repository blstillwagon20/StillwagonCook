## What you need

- 1 egg
- ½ cup (1 stick) margarine, melted and cooled
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- 2 Tbsp. milk
- 1 tsp. lemon juice
- 2 cups sifted all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp. baking powder

## What to do with it

1. Preheat oven to 400°.
2. Break the egg (1) into a large mixing bowl. Add the margarine (½ cup, 1 stick), sugar (1 cup), cinnamon (½ tsp.), milk (2 Tbsp.), and lemon juice (1 tsp.). Beat with an eggbeater until creamy.
3. Sift the flour (2 cups) and baking powder (1 tsp.) together into another bowl. Add this to the mixture in the first bowl and mix well with a spoon.
4. Drop the dough by the tsp. onto 2 cookie sheets (you don't have to grease them). Leave at least 1 inch of space between the cookies.
5. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes or until the cookies are golden brown. Immediately, remove the cookies from the cookie sheets with a spatula. Place them on wire racks to cool. Makes about 36 cookies.

**If you only have 1 cookie sheet,** bake 1 batch of cookies, then let the sheet cool before you drop and bake the rest.
