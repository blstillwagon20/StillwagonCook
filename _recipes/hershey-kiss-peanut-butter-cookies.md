## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter
- ⅓ cup peanut butter
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup packed brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1¾ cup flour
- Hershey Kisses or Hugs

## Steps

1. Cream together butter (½ cup), peanut butter (⅓ cup), and sugars (½ cup). Add vanilla (1 tsp.) and egg (1).
2. Put in flour (1¾ cup), baking soda (1 tsp.), and salt (½ tsp.). Make balls.
3. Bake 8 minutes. Put Hershey Kiss or Hug in center and put back in oven for 2 to 5 minutes longer.
