## Ingredients

- 3 cans sliced carrots
- 1 can cream of celery soup (Campbell's)
- 1 small can Durkee dried onions (French's fried onions)
- 10 slices Velveeta cheese

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Layer the following in a casserole dish, in this order: 1½ cans of carrots, ½ can soup, 5 slices of cheese, ½ can of onions, the remaining carrots, the remaining soup, 5 more slices of cheese, and the remaining onions.
3. Bake 30 minutes until cheese is melted.
