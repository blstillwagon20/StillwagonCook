## Ingredients

- 5 cans sliced carrots
- 1 can cream of celery soup (Campbell's)
- 1 large can French's fried onions
- ½ brick Velveeta cheese

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Layer the following in a casserole dish, in this order: half the carrots, half the soup, a layer of Velveeta, half the onions, the remaining carrots, the remaining soup, another layer of Velveeta, and the remaining onions.
3. Bake 30 minutes until cheese is melted.
