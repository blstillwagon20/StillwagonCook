## Ingredients

- 2½ cups sugar
- ½ cup light corn syrup
- ½ cup water*
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 2 egg whites
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2 sq. (2 oz.) bitter chocolate, melted and cooled
- 1 cup chopped pecans or walnuts

*Use 1 Tbsp. less water on humid days.

## Steps

1. Cook sugar (2½ cups), corn syrup (½ cup), water (½ cup), and salt (¼ tsp.) in saucepan over low heat, stirring constantly, until sugar is dissolved. Cook, without stirring, to 248° on candy thermometer (or until small amount of mixture dropped into very cold water forms a firm ball).
2. Beat egg whites (2) until stiff. Pour half of mixture over stiffly beaten egg whites slowly, beating constantly until blended. Cook the remaining syrup to 272°F (thin thread stage). Add this to first mixture slowly, beating with electric mixer.
3. Add vanilla (1 tsp.) and melted bitter chocolate (2 sq., 2 oz.). Beat until mixture holds its shape and becomes slightly dull. (Mixture may become too stiff for mixer.) Fold in nuts.
4. Drop mixture from buttered spoon onto waxed paper. Let stand at room temperature, turning candy over once, until outside of candy is firm, at least 12 hours. Store in airtight container.
