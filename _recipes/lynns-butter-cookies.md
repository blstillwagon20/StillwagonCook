> These cookies are very good!

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cup sugar
- 1 cup Crisco (butter flavored)
- 2 eggs
- ¼ cup light corn syrup
- 1 Tbsp. vanilla
- 3 cups flour
- ¾ tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. salt

## Steps

1. Cream together sugar (1¼ cup) and shortening. Mix in eggs (2), vanilla (1 Tbsp.) and corn syrup (¼ cup).
2. Add baking soda (½ tsp.), baking powder (¾ tsp.), and salt (½ tsp.). Mix in flour (3 cups).
3. Refrigerate until you can work with dough, 1 to 2 hours. Note: when working with dough, take only a small amount out of refrigerator at a time, as the dough is hard to work with. As soon as it gets a little warm, you can't work with it.
4. Bake at 375° for 5 to 9 minutes.
