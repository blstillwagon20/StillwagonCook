> A friend of mine introduced me to this German dish. It is traditionally served with french fries.

## Ingredients

- Boneless very thin strips of pork (if you can't find very thin strips, pound them down with a mallet so that they're nice and thin)
- Bread crumbs
- Beaten egg for dipping
- Salt and pepper
- Butter
- Lemon, cut into wedges

## Steps

1. Dip pork in egg, then bread crumbs to coat. Sprinkle with salt and pepper.
2. Fry in a skillet in butter until browned on both sides and cooked through.
3. Serve with the lemon wedges. Allow each guest to squeeze the lemon juice onto the schnitzel (this really makes them delicious, so don't exclude it!). Enjoy!
