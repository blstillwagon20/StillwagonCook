> Juicy meat loaf baked over sliced potatoes.

## Ingredients

### Potato layer

- 5 cups thinly sliced, peeled potatoes
- 1 medium onion, finely diced and sautéed in oil until soft
- 1½ tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 2 tsp. parsley flakes
- A little flour and water

### Meat mixture

- 2 lb. ground beef
- 1 can (10¾ oz.) condensed cream of mushroom soup
- 2 eggs
- 1½ cups bread crumbs
- 1 large onion, finely chopped and sautéed in oil till soft
- 1½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. pepper
- Ketchup, for the top

## Steps

1. Stir together the potato layer ingredients and arrange in layers in a greased large baking dish, sprinkling each layer with a little flour. Put in a little water, just a little over, to moisten and make a nice sauce while it's cooking.
2. Mix the meat mixture ingredients.
3. Spread evenly over potatoes; decorate top of meat mixture with ketchup.
4. Bake in 350° oven for 1½ to 2 hours until tender.
