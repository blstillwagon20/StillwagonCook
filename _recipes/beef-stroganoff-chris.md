## Ingredients

- 1–1½ lb. round steak, cut into strips
- 3 cans beef broth
- 1 Tbsp. sour cream
- 3 Tbsp. flour
- Little bit of water
- 1 small onion
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- A little pepper
- Egg noodles, for serving

## Steps

1. Put butter (2 Tbsp.) in pan and let melt. Brown strips of round steak (1–1½ lb.); add onion (1 small).
2. Once browned, add 3 cans of broth and a little pepper. Cover and simmer 1½ to 2 hours.
3. Then thicken with flour (3 Tbsp.), sour cream (1 Tbsp.) and water.
4. Serve over egg noodles.
