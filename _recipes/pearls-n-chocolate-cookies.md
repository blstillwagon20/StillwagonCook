## Ingredients

- 2¼ cups flour
- ⅔ cup Nestle Baking Cocoa
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. salt
- ⅔ cup Crisco (butter flavor)
- ⅔ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- ⅔ cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 (10 oz.) pkg. white chocolate pieces

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°. In bowl, combine flour (2¼ cups), baking cocoa (⅔ cup), baking soda (1 tsp.) and salt (1 tsp.); set aside.
2. In bowl, beat Crisco (⅔ cup), butter (⅔ cup), sugar (1 cup), brown sugar (⅔ cup), and vanilla until creamy. Add eggs (2), one at a time, beating well after each. Gradually add flour mixture. Stir in white baking pieces.
3. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheets. Bake 9 to 10 minutes.
4. Allow to stand 2 minutes before removing from cookie sheets. Makes about 2½ dozen 3" cookies.
