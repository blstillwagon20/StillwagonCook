## Ingredients

- 2 lb. round steak
- 1 large onion
- ¼ cup butter
- ½ to 1 lb. sliced fresh mushrooms
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 cups water with 2 tsp. chicken bouillon
- Flour, for coating
- Noodles, for serving

## Steps

1. Sauté finely diced onion (1 large) and mushrooms (½ to 1 lb.) in 2 Tbsp. butter until onion is very soft and remove from pan.
2. Remove fat and bone from steak (2 lb.); cut into strips 2½ inches long and ¾ inch wide.
3. Melt additional 2 Tbsp. butter in pan. Toss strips of meat in flour, coating thoroughly. Brown meat in butter.
4. Add salt (1 tsp.) and bouillon water mixture. Add remaining flour from tossing meat and simmer for about 1¼ hours, stirring occasionally.
5. Serve over noodles.
