## Ingredients

- 1 (8 oz.) pkg. softened cream cheese
- ½ cup milk
- 2 cups (8 oz.) shredded sharp Cheddar cheese
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- Chicken in a Biskit crackers

## Steps

1. In small bowl, with electric mixer at medium speed, beat cream cheese (1 pkg., 8 oz.) until smooth; gradually blend in milk (½ cup).
2. Add remaining cheeses, beating until well blended.
3. Serve as a dip with the Chicken in a Biskit crackers. Makes about 3¼ cups.
