## Ingredients

- ¼ cup shortening
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 egg
- ½ cup molasses
- 1 tsp. soda dissolved in ½ cup water
- 2 cups flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. ginger
- ½ tsp. nutmeg
- ½ tsp. cloves
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- Glaze or cream cheese frosting

## Steps

1. Mix together thoroughly shortening (¼ cup), sugar (½ cup), egg (1), and molasses (½ cup).
2. Stir in the soda dissolved in water.
3. Sift together and stir in flour (2 cups), salt (½ tsp.) and spices.
4. Chill dough. Drop teaspoonfuls on lightly greased baking sheet.
5. Bake until set at 350°. Frost with either a glaze or cream cheese frosting.
