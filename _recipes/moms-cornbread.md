## Ingredients

- 1½ cups cornmeal
- ½ cup flour
- 3½ tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 egg
- 1 cup milk
- 3 Tbsp. sugar

## Steps

1. Mix together all ingredients.
2. Bake at 425° in a greased 8" x 8" pan for 20 to 25 minutes.
