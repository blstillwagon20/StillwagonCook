> This is my mom's homemade ice cream recipe. We had an old hand crank ice cream freezer and used to take turns cranking the handle around til it was finally done (which seemed like forever when we were kids, but it was fun and exciting to make our own). Just watch out for those ice cream headaches!

## Recipe #1

- 8 eggs
- 1½ cups sugar
- 1 can sweetened condensed milk
- 1 can evaporated milk
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- Whole milk

1. Beat together eggs and sugar.
2. Add condensed milk (1 can), evaporated milk (1 can) and vanilla (2 tsp.).
3. Pour into an ice cream freezer. Add enough whole milk (2½ cups) to make freezer ⅔ to ¾ full. Makes 1½ gallons of ice cream.

## Recipe #2

- 2½ cups milk
- 4 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1¼ cups sugar
- 2 cups sugar
- 10 eggs
- Vanilla
- 3 pkgs. vanilla instant pudding
- Milk

1. Cook milk, cornstarch (4 Tbsp.) and 1¼ cups sugar.
2. Pour over 2 cups sugar beaten with 10 eggs and vanilla.
3. Add enough milk to fill ⅔ to ¾ full and 3 pkgs. vanilla instant pudding. Makes 2 gallons.
