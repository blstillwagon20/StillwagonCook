## Ingredients

- 1 lb. fish fillets of your choice
- ½ cup fine bread crumbs
- 2 Tbsp. melted margarine
- 1 Tbsp. lemon juice
- ⅓ cup sliced almonds

## Steps

1. Lightly grease baking dish. Lay fish fillets in dish.
2. Mix crumbs (½ cup), margarine (2 Tbsp.) and lemon juice (1 Tbsp.) together. Sprinkle on top of fish. Top with almonds (⅓ cup).
3. Bake in oven until fish flakes, or it can be cooked in microwave: cover with plastic wrap, venting one corner. Cook on high 3 minutes, rotate dish a half-turn and cook 2 to 4 more minutes until fish is done.
