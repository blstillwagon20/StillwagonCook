> This recipe came with a heart shaped cookie cutter I purchased and I thought it was cute!

## Ingredients

- Candlelight (scent optional)
- Music (to taste)
- 1 part tenderness
- 1 part caring
- 1 heaping helping of hugs
- 1 pinch of conversation
- 2 generous portions of kisses
- 1 dash of passion
- 3 little words: "I LOVE YOU"

## Steps

1. Preheat room to 75 degrees.
2. Place all ingredients in candlelit room. Add music. Carefully blend tenderness (1) and caring (1) until mixture softens. Fold in some slow, easy hugs (1 heaping), along with pinch of gentle conversation (1).
3. Vigorously whisk in liberal amounts of smoldering kisses (2 generous). Whip into a frenzy, then roll out on warm surface and lovingly press against heart.
4. Brush with passion (1) and bake until golden and bubbly. Sprinkle with remaining kisses. Garnish with words, "I love you (3)". Serve piping hot.

Creates two loving hearts.
