> Everyone loves this soup! It's very creamy and delicious.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups cubed potatoes
- ½ cup chopped carrots
- ½ cup chopped celery
- 1 tsp. parsley flakes
- 2½ cups water (more if needed)
- A little pepper
- 1 chicken bouillon cube
- ½ tsp. salt (or to taste)
- ½ lb. Velveeta cheese, cubed
- 1½ cups milk + 4 Tbsp. flour

## Steps

1. Cook vegetables until tender in the 2½ cups of water.
2. Mix together milk and flour until smooth. Add to vegetables and cook until thickened.
3. Add Velveeta cheese (½ lb.), cubed. Stir until cheese is melted.
4. Add salt (½ tsp.), pepper and parsley flakes (1 tsp.) and serve steaming hot with either breadsticks or crackers.

**Tip:** You can also use Lite Velveeta in place of the Velveeta, and for the milk, use half evaporated skimmed milk and half skim milk to help with the calories and fat. This is what I usually do and you can't even tell the difference except in your waistline.
