> These are a great dessert especially for a gathering since it makes large pan full, and they're delicious to boot!

## Ingredients

- 1⅔ cup sugar
- 1 cup oil
- 1 (16 oz.) Libby's pumpkin
- 4 eggs
- Vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 2 tsp. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. soda
- Cream Cheese Frosting (see that recipe)

## Steps

1. Mix first 5 ingredients together, then add the remainder of ingredients.
2. Spread into a 15" x 10" x 1" ungreased pan (jelly roll pan); bake at 350° for 25 to 30 minutes. Allow to cool.
3. Use Cream Cheese Frosting to frost cake with. Cut into squares and keep refrigerated until ready to use.
