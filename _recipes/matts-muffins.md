## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 1 cup buttermilk
- ½ cup brown sugar (packed)
- ⅓ cup shortening
- 1 cup quick-cooking oats
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. soda

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 400°. Grease bottoms of muffin cups.
2. Beat egg (1); stir in buttermilk (1 cup), brown sugar (½ cup) and shortening (⅓ cup).
3. Mix in remaining ingredients just until flour (1 cup) is moistened. Batter should be lumpy.
4. Fill muffin cups. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until light brown.
