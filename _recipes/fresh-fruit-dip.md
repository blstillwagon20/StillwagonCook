## Ingredients

- 12 Oreo cookies, coarsely crushed
- ¼ cup firmly packed brown sugar
- 2 cups dairy sour cream
- Assorted fresh fruits in dipping size pieces
- 1 large pineapple

## Steps

1. Combine Oreos, brown sugar (¼ cup) and sour cream (2 cups). Chill several hours. Do not stir after chilling.
2. Cut a slice from pineapple (1 large) about 1 inch below bottom leaves. Use a sharp knife to hollow out center, leaving shell intact.
3. Cut pineapple pulp into bite size pieces, discarding hard center core.
4. Spoon sour cream dip into shell. Place pineapple in the center of a large serving platter.
5. Arrange assorted fruit around pineapple. This is very good!
