> I first had this potato dish at my father-in-law's house and found them to be just marvelous! Try this wonderful potato dish and you'll fall in love with it, too. He used a spice called yellow asafoetida powder in place of the garlic powder I have listed. Since this spice isn't readily available, I use the garlic powder instead.

## Ingredients

- 8 medium potatoes, peeled and sliced into ¼" disks
- Water
- 1 Tbsp. olive oil
- ¾ tsp. garlic powder
- ½ tsp. ground dried rosemary
- ¼ tsp. ground black pepper
- ½ tsp. turmeric
- 3 cups sour cream
- 1 Tbsp. melted butter
- 2 tsp. salt
- ¼ cup water
- 1 tsp. sweet paprika
- 2 Tbsp. chopped fresh parsley

## Steps

1. Boil the potato slices in lightly salted water (¼ cup) in a 4 quart saucepan until they are cooked but firm. Remove and drain.
2. Add the olive oil (1 Tbsp.) to a medium saucepan over moderate heat, and when hot, add the garlic powder (¾ tsp.). Sauté momentarily; add the rosemary (½ tsp.), black pepper (¼ tsp.), and turmeric (½ tsp.) and stir briefly.
3. Add the sour cream (3 cups), melted butter (1 Tbsp.), salt (2 tsp.), and water. Whisk it into a smooth sauce and remove from the heat.
4. Combine the potato slices and sour cream sauce in a mixing bowl. Pour the mixture into a casserole dish, sprinkle with paprika (1 tsp.), and place in the top of a preheated 375° oven.
5. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes or until the top is golden brown. Garnish with fresh parsley (2 Tbsp.) and serve hot.
