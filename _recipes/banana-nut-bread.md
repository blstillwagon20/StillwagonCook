> This is a recipe from my mother. It's the best I've had and can be made into either muffins or bread.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups sugar
- ½ cup shortening
- 2 eggs
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 3 cups flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- ½ cup sour milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- ½ cup nuts

## Steps

1. Cream sugar (1½ cups), shortening (½ cup) and eggs (2) together until light.
2. Sift flour (3 cups), salt (½ tsp.), soda (1 tsp.), and baking powder (1 tsp.). Add to creamed mixture.
3. Add the remainder of the ingredients except the nuts (½ cup). Stir the nuts in well.
4. Bake in 2 greased loaf pans at 350° for 45 minutes for small loaves or 1 hour for a large one (make sure you check to see if these are done with a toothpick because mine normally take longer).

You can also use this recipe for muffins.
