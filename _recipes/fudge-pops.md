## Ingredients

- 1 pkg. (3.4 ounces) cook-and-serve chocolate pudding mix
- 3 cups milk
- ¼ cup sugar
- ½ cup whipping cream, whipped
- Wooden sticks or plastic spoons

## Steps

1. In a saucepan over medium heat, combine pudding, milk (3 cups), and sugar (¼ cup); bring to a boil. Cook and stir for 2 minutes. Cool for 30 minutes, stirring several times.
2. Fold in cream (½ cup). Pour into molds or small paper cups. Freeze until partially frozen; insert wooden sticks or plastic spoons into center of pops.
3. Freeze until firm, about 3 to 4 hours. Yield: 13 servings.
