> This is a very low-fat recipe. Sometimes, I add a small can of mushrooms. I also like to dip every bite into salsa or picante sauce.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups diced potatoes
- Vegetable cooking spray
- 1 cup chopped tomato
- ¼ cup chopped onion
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 1½ cups egg substitute
- ½ cup shredded reduced-fat cheddar cheese

## Steps

1. Cook potato in a saucepan in boiling water to cover, 10 to 12 minutes or until tender; drain.
2. Coat a large nonstick skillet with cooking spray; place over medium heat until hot.
3. Add potato, tomato (1 cup), onion (¼ cup), salt (¼ tsp.) and pepper (¼ tsp.); sauté until onion is tender.
4. Pour egg substitute (1½ cups) over vegetable mixture; cover and cook over medium-low heat 15 minutes or until set.
5. Sprinkle with cheese (½ cup). Cover; cook 2 minutes or until cheese melts.
6. Cut into 6 wedges and serve.
