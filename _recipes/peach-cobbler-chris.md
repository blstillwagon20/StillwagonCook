> A good friend of mine gave me this recipe. It's very simple and very good.

## Ingredients

- 6 Tbsp. oleo, melted in a 2 qt. baking dish
- 1 cup sugar
- ¾ cup flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- Dash of salt
- ¾ cup milk

### Peach mixture

- 2 cups fresh peaches
- 1 cup sugar

## Steps

1. Combine the sugar, flour (¾ cup), baking powder (2 tsp.) and salt, and then add the milk (¾ cup). Stir until well mixed.
2. Pour batter over the oleo (6 Tbsp.) in the baking dish. DO NOT STIR!!!
3. Stir the peaches (2 cups) and sugar together and spoon over the batter.
4. Bake at 350° for 1 hour.

Canned peaches can be substituted for the fresh, but do not add sugar and bake only for ½ hour.
