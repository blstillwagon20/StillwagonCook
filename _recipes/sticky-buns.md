## Ingredients

- 1 batch Homemade Rolls dough (see Homemade Rolls recipe)
- Butter
- Brown sugar
- Cinnamon
- 1 stick butter, melted
- ⅔ cup brown sugar
- Nuts (optional)

## Steps

1. First prepare the Homemade Rolls recipe. After letting the roll dough (1) rise the first time and punching it down, again let it rest 5 minutes.
2. Then roll part of dough into a rectangle. Butter (1 stick) the dough; sprinkle this with brown sugar (⅔ cup) and cinnamon.
3. Roll the dough up; pinch the edges together so it doesn't come apart and slice into about 1" slices.
4. Melt one stick butter and add ⅔ cup brown sugar (if you like, you can also put nuts in this). Put this in bottom of a 9" x 13" pan.
5. Place rolls on top of this and cover and let rise until doubled.
6. Bake in a 350° oven until golden brown.
7. When done, remove from oven and let set for a little bit. Then turn pan upside down to let goo drip down the rolls.
