## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp. oil
- 1 lb. boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into thin strips
- 1 (16 oz.) jar Thick 'N Chunky Picante
- 1 (12-count) box soft taco shells
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 4 oz. shredded cheddar cheese
- ½ cup sour cream

## Steps

1. Heat oil (1 Tbsp.) in large skillet over high heat until hot. Add chicken; cook and stir until no longer pink.
2. Stir in picante (1 jar, 16 oz.) sauce. Reduce heat to medium; simmer 10 to 12 minutes until picante has thickened, stirring occasionally.
3. Heat tacos as directed on package.
4. Fill each shell with about 3 Tbsp. chicken filling. Top each with lettuce (1 cup), cheese (4 oz.) and sour cream (½ cup).
