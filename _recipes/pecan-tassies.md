> The first time I made these cookies was when I was 8 months pregnant with my daughter, Paitan. I made them early before Christmas and froze them and found that I liked them best straight out of the freezer. They're just like miniature pecan pies and are just delicious.

## Ingredients

- 1 (3 oz.) pkg. cream cheese
- ⅓ cup butter
- 1 cup flour

### Filling

- 2 eggs
- ⅛ tsp. salt
- 1½ cups brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp. melted butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- ⅔ cup coarsely broken pecans

## Steps

1. Cream butter and cheese (1 pkg., 3 oz.) together. Blend in flour (1 cup); mix thoroughly.
2. Divide dough into 24 balls and press evenly around bottoms and sides of ungreased 1¾ inch muffin pan.
3. **Filling:** Beat eggs (2), sugar, butter (1 Tbsp.) and vanilla (1 tsp.) until smooth.
4. Put half of pecans on bottom of shells. Pour in filling and sprinkle pecans on top.
5. Bake at 350° for 25 minutes. Keep in tins until cool.
