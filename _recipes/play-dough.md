## Ingredients and tools for each

- 1 cup white flour
- ¼ cup salt
- 2 tsp. food coloring
- 1 Tbsp. vegetable oil
- 1 cup water
- Cream of tartar (amount not typed on the page)
- Medium pot
- Plastic bag or airtight container

## Getting ready

1. Mix flour (1 cup), salt (¼ cup), and cream of tartar in medium pot.
2. Add water (1 cup), food coloring (2 tsp.), and oil (1 Tbsp.).

## Cooking

1. Stir over medium heat for 3 to 5 minutes. Don't worry if the mixture looks like a globby mess; it'll turn into dough!
2. When the mixture forms a ball in the center of the pot, take it out and put it on a floured surface. Squish it and punch it around a bit; this is called kneading.
3. When you're done, put it in a plastic bag or airtight container and store it in the fridge.
