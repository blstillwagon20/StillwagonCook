## Ingredients

- Lasagna noodles (San Giorgio rippled edge)

### Sauce

- Country style pork sausage
- 1 lb. Hunt's tomato sauce
- 12 oz. Hunt's tomato paste
- Salt and pepper

### Cheese mixture

- 1 lb. ricotta cheese
- 2 eggs
- A little grated parmesan cheese
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Brown sausage in a little oil.
2. Mix sauce (1 lb.), paste (12 oz.), and salt and pepper. Cook sauce for 2 to 3 hours on low heat.
3. Mix cheese (1 lb.) mixture together.
4. Cook lasagna noodles only until halfway done.
5. Layer: sauce, noodles, sauce, cheese mixture, noodles, etc.
6. Cook for 2 to 3 hours at 300°.
