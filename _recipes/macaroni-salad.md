## Ingredients

- ¾ cup mayo
- 2 Tbsp. vinegar
- 2 tsp. sugar
- 2 tsp. salt
- ⅛ tsp. pepper
- 8 oz. pkg. elbow macaroni
- 1 cucumber
- 1 onion
- 1 tomato

## Steps

1. Mix mayo (¾ cup), vinegar (2 Tbsp.), sugar (2 tsp.), salt (2 tsp.) and pepper (⅛ tsp.) together.
2. Cook macaroni (8 oz.) according to package directions.
3. Cut up the cucumber (1), onion (1), and tomato (1).
4. Combine all together and refrigerate.
