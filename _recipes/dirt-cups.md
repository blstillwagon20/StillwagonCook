## Ingredients

- 2 cups cold milk
- 1 pkg. chocolate instant pudding
- 1 (8 oz.) Cool Whip
- 1 pkg. (16 oz.) crushed Oreos
- 8 to 10 (7 oz.) plastic cups
- Gummy worms, etc.

## Steps

1. Pour milk (2 cups) into large bowl and add pudding mix. Beat until well blended. Let stand 5 minutes.
2. Stir in Cool Whip (1, 8 oz.) and ½ of the crushed Oreos.
3. Place 1 Tbsp. crushed Oreos into cups (8 to 10, 7 oz.). Fill cups ¾ full with pudding mixture. Top with remaining cookie crumbs.
4. Refrigerate 1 hour before serving. Decorate with gummy worms and etc.
