## Ingredients

### Soak up to 1 hour

- 1 loaf day old bread
- 1 qt. chocolate milk

### Mix together

- 3 eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp. vanilla
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- 1 cup mini chocolate chips

## Steps

1. Soak bread (1) in chocolate milk (1 qt.) up to 1 hour.
2. Mix together eggs (3), sugar (1 cup), vanilla (2 Tbsp.), cinnamon (½ tsp.) and chocolate chips (1 cup).
3. Pour bread mixture into egg mixture and stir.
4. Pour into 9" x 11" greased pan. Bake at 350° for 1 hour 15 minutes. Serve warm.
