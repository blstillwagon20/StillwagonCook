## Ingredients

### Soup

- 2 whole chickens
- 2 whole onions
- 2 stalks celery
- 2 cloves garlic
- Salt and pepper
- 3 boxes frozen spinach

### Meatballs

- 1 lb. ground chuck
- ½ cup bread crumbs
- ½ cup parmesan cheese
- 1 egg
- Salt and pepper

### Egg mixture

- 6 eggs
- ½ cup parmesan cheese
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Boil chickens (2), onions (2), celery (2 stalks), garlic (2 cloves), salt, and pepper in water for a couple hours.
2. Take out chickens and debone and cut meat into bite-size pieces. Strain the broth and throw out the vegetables.
3. Cook spinach (3 boxes) according to package directions. Strain off all liquid. Set aside.
4. **Meatballs:** Mix ground chuck (1 lb.), bread crumbs (½ cup), parmesan, egg (1), salt and pepper all together. Shape into little marble-size balls. Brown and drain grease. Set aside. (You can make meatballs ahead of time, just freeze. Then thaw and brown.)
5. Into the broth, add all the cooked items above.
6. In a separate bowl, mix together the 6 eggs, ½ cup parmesan cheese, salt and pepper.
7. Bring broth to a rolling boil and then reduce heat to allow the broth to be at a low boil. Throw this egg mixture into broth. DO NOT STIR!!! Put lid on and let it cook for 5 to 8 minutes.
8. Now, slowly stir soup. Add salt and pepper to taste. Serve.
