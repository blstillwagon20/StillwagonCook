> This is one of Aunt Sue's many delicious recipes she has brought to Grandma's house on occasion.

## Ingredients

- 1 (8 oz.) pkg. Philadelphia cream cheese
- 1 (8 oz.) pkg. cold pack sharp Cheddar cheese
- 1 (3 oz.) pkg. cold pack Bleu cheese
- Chopped almonds
- Sprig of parsley

## Steps

1. Combine all cheeses and mix well with buttered hands.
2. Form 1 large or 2 small balls or shape into log.
3. Roll in chopped almonds and top with sprig of parsley. Chill well.
4. Serve with your favorite crackers or pretzel logs.
