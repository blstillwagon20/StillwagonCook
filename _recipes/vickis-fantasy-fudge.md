## Ingredients

- 6 cups sugar
- 1½ cups (3 sticks) margarine
- 1⅓ cup evaporated milk
- 2 pkgs. (12 oz. each) semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 1 jar (13 oz.) Kraft marshmallow creme
- 2 cups nuts, chopped (optional)
- 2 tsp. vanilla

## Steps

1. Stir together margarine (1½ cups, 3 sticks), sugar (6 cups) and milk in heavy 5 qt. pan, bring to full boil, stirring constantly. Boil 5 minutes over medium heat, stirring constantly to prevent scorching.
2. Remove from heat. Gradually stir in chips until melted. Add remaining ingredients; mix until well blended.
3. Pour into greased pan. Cool at room temperature. Cut into squares.
