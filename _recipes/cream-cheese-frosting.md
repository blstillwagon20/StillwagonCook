> This frosting can be used on top of pumpkin bars, carrot cake, cookies, spice cake, cupcakes, or just about anything. It's delicious!

## Ingredients

- 3 oz. cream cheese
- ½ cup butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2 cups powdered sugar

## Steps

1. Mix first 3 ingredients until light and creamy, then add sugar.
2. Beat until smooth and frost on dessert of your choose.
3. Keep this refrigerated because of the cream cheese (3 oz.).
