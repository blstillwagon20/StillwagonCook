## What you need

- ½ cup margarine, softened
- About 3 cups sifted confectioner's sugar
- 4 Tbsp. milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract

## What to do with it

1. Put the softened margarine (½ cup) in a large mixing bowl. Cream it until it's soft and fluffy by rubbing it against the side of the bowl with the back of a spoon.
2. Slowly mix in 1 cup of the sugar. Then mix in 2 Tbsp. of the milk. Mix in another cup of sugar and another Tbsp. of the milk. Then mix in the rest of the sugar and the rest of the milk. The mixture should be creamy so that it will be easy to spread.
3. Add the vanilla and beat with an eggbeater until frosting is light and fluffy. If you want a softer frosting, stir in another Tbsp. of milk. This makes enough to frost and thinly fill a 9" layer cake or 24 cupcakes.
