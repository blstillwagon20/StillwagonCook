## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- ½ tsp. salt
- 8 Tbsp. sugar
- Vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- 1 medium jar maraschino cherries
- Powdered sugar

## Steps

1. Allow the butter (1 cup) to soften. Mix sugar (8 Tbsp.), flour (2 cups), salt (½ tsp.), and vanilla into the butter and cream with mixer or hands until well mixed.
2. Drain cherries (1 medium jar) very well. Take a cherry and wrap a small amount of dough around it. Form into balls. The smaller the better.
3. Put on greased cookie sheet and bake in oven 10 to 15 minutes or until slightly tan. Then roll in powdered sugar.
